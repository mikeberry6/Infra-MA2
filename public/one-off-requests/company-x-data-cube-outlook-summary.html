Company X | Data Cube Scope & Main Fields

Below is a condensed view of the six priority datasets and the main fields required for analysis.

COMMERCIAL & CUSTOMER DATA CUBE

Approach: Establish a single customer and site hierarchy, link contracts and pricing to covered assets, and use monthly relationship data to measure retention, churn and renewals.

01. Customer & Site Master

  • Customer: Customer ID; customer legal name; billing parent; ultimate parent; customer type; end market; national account flag; customer start date; customer status; active site count; active asset count; current recurring revenue; TTM revenue.
  • Site: Site ID; site name; Customer ID; billing customer ID; city / state; region; service branch; site type; end market; site start date; site status; active contract count; active asset count; current recurring revenue; TTM revenue.

02. Contract Master

  • Contract: Contract ID; Customer ID; Site ID; Asset ID; effective date; expiration date; remaining term; auto-renewal flag; termination rights; notice period; early termination fee; consent requirements; contract status; covered asset count; contracted MRR; remaining revenue.
  • Pricing & Coverage: Pricing line ID; Contract ID; Deployment ID; Site ID; Asset ID; fee type; contracted rate; rate unit; billing frequency; escalation method; escalation %; next increase; recurring revenue; one-time fees; discounts / credits; equipment type; covered quantity; service level; coverage start date; coverage end date; current coverage flag.

03. Customer Churn & Retention

  • Monthly Retention: Month end; Customer ID; Site ID; Contract ID; Asset ID; beginning active flag; beginning unit count; beginning recurring revenue; new / expansion revenue; price escalation revenue; contraction revenue; churned revenue; ending active flag; ending unit count; ending recurring revenue.
  • Churn & Renewal: Churn event ID; Customer ID; Site ID; Contract ID; Asset ID; notice date; termination date; removal date; churn level; churn reason; customer / Company X initiated; competitor loss flag; lost units; lost MRR; final outcome; expiration date; notice deadline; renewal completed date; renewal status; renewed term; renewed unit count; nonrenewed unit count; pre-renewal rate; post-renewal rate; revenue retained.

OPERATIONAL & ASSET DATA CUBE

Approach: Build a unit-level asset register and effective-dated status history, then layer monthly revenue, billing, operating events, service cost, utilization and disposition data to reconcile fleet movement with financial performance.

04. Asset Master

  • Asset & Status: Asset ID; serial / tag number; asset type; manufacturer / model; purchase date; acquisition source; ownership type; original cost; installed cost; placed-in-service date; net book value; current status; current location; current customer / site; condition grade; Deployment ID; Contract ID; status start date; status end date.
  • Capital & Disposal: Capital event ID; Disposal record ID; Asset ID; event date; event type; equipment cost; installation cost; refurbishment cost; growth capex; maintenance capex; upgrade capex; retirement date; disposal date; disposal method; disposal reason; final disposition; disposal proceeds; net book value; gain / loss; write-off amount.

05. Monthly Operating Data Tape

  • Asset-Month: Month end; Asset ID; Customer ID; Site ID; beginning status; ending status; deployed days; billed days; contracted rate; recurring revenue; one-time revenue; total revenue; service cost; installation cost; maintenance capex; growth capex.
  • Billing & Events: Invoice ID; invoice date; service period; revenue month; Contract ID; Pricing line ID; fee type; rate / quantity; net billed amount; invoice status; payment status; Event ID; event type; request date; scheduled date; completion date; final billing date; prior site; new site; event reason.

06. Fleet Utilization

  • Status & Utilization: Asset ID; Customer ID; Site ID; branch / yard; status; substatus; available flag; billed flag; status start date; status end date; status days; expected availability; status-change reason; next assignment; month end; asset type; region / branch; deployed days; billed days; idle days; repair days; deployment utilization; billing utilization; billing conversion; idle rate; revenue per fleet day; revenue per billed day; days to redeploy; idle aging bucket.
  • Idle Fleet: Asset ID; asset type; current yard; region; idle start date; idle days; aging bucket; condition grade; repair required; ready-to-deploy flag; estimated repair cost; next customer; expected install date; recommended action; responsible owner.
