Company X | Data Cube Scope & Requested Fields

Below is the proposed structure for the six priority datasets. The approach uses stable customer, site, contract and asset IDs; preserves effective-dated history; and reconciles operating detail to billing and financial records. Each table should be delivered at the stated grain with field definitions and basic quality checks.

COMMERCIAL & CUSTOMER DATA CUBE

Approach: Establish a single customer and site hierarchy, link contracts and pricing to covered assets, and use monthly relationship data to measure retention, churn and renewals.

01. Customer & Site Master

  • Customer List (1 row / customer): Customer ID; customer legal name; billing parent; ultimate parent; customer type; end market; national account flag; account manager; customer start date; customer status; customer tenure; acquisition source; active site count; active asset count; current recurring revenue; TTM revenue.
  • Site List (1 row / site): Site ID; site name; Customer ID; billing customer ID; address; city / state; region; service branch; site type; end market; site start date; site status; active contract count; active asset count; current recurring revenue; TTM revenue.
  • Customer Hierarchy: Ultimate parent; billing parent; parent / child account mapping; site-to-account mapping; effective dates; mapping method.
  • Definitions & Checks: Field definitions; standardized categories; record counts; data-quality checks.
  • Primary analyses: Customer concentration; end-market exposure; geographic mix; site-level revenue analysis.

02. Contract Master

  • Contract Summary (1 row / contract): Contract ID; Customer ID; Site ID; Asset ID; effective date; expiration date; remaining term; auto-renewal flag; termination rights; notice period; early termination fee; consent requirements; contract status; covered asset count; contracted MRR; remaining revenue.
  • Pricing & Escalators (1 row / fee line): Pricing line ID; Contract ID; Site ID; Asset ID; fee type; contracted rate; rate unit; billing frequency; escalation method; escalation %; last increase; next increase; recurring revenue; one-time fees; discounts / credits; effective dates.
  • Contract Coverage (1 row / contract-site-asset): Coverage record ID; Contract ID; Pricing line ID; Deployment ID; Customer ID; Site ID; billing account; ultimate parent; Asset ID; equipment type; covered quantity; service level; coverage start date; coverage end date; current coverage flag; replacement Asset ID.
  • Amendments & Documents: Amendment history; prior / revised terms; signed documents; VDR reference; execution status; completeness check.
  • Definitions & Checks: Field definitions; contract categories; document checks; revenue tie-outs; missing-field checks; duplicate contract checks.
  • Primary analyses: Revenue visibility; renewal profile; escalator analysis; termination exposure.

03. Customer Churn & Retention

  • Monthly Retention (1 row / customer-site-asset-month): Month end; Customer ID; Site ID; Contract ID; beginning active flag; beginning unit count; beginning recurring revenue; beginning rate; new / expansion revenue; price escalation revenue; contraction revenue; churned revenue; ending active flag; ending unit count; ending recurring revenue; customer / site tenure.
  • Churn Events (1 row / churn event): Churn event ID; Customer ID; Site ID; Asset ID; notice date; termination date; removal date; churn level; churn reason; reason detail; customer / Company X initiated; competitor loss flag; lost units; lost MRR; save attempt; final outcome.
  • Renewal Schedule (1 row / renewal opportunity): Contract ID; Customer ID; Site ID; Asset ID; expiration date; notice deadline; renewal due date; renewal completed date; renewal status; renewed term; renewed unit count; nonrenewed unit count; pre-renewal rate; post-renewal rate; rate change; revenue retained.
  • Retention Summary: Customer retention; site retention; unit retention; gross revenue retention; net revenue retention; churn by reason.
  • Definitions & Checks: Churn rules; expansion / contraction rules; replacement treatment; revenue bridge checks; unit-count tie-outs; duplicate event checks.
  • Primary analyses: Revenue retention; churn drivers; renewal performance; cohort durability.

OPERATIONAL & ASSET DATA CUBE

Approach: Build a unit-level asset register and effective-dated status history, then layer monthly revenue, billing, operating events, service cost, utilization and disposition data to reconcile fleet movement with financial performance.

04. Asset Master

  • Asset Register (1 row / physical asset): Asset ID; serial / tag number; asset type; manufacturer / model; purchase date; acquisition source; ownership type; new / used flag; original cost; installed cost; placed-in-service date; net book value; current status; current location; current customer / site; condition grade.
  • Capital History (1 row / capital event): Capital event ID; Asset ID; event date; event type; equipment cost; freight cost; installation cost; refurbishment cost; growth capex; maintenance capex; upgrade capex; capital project ID; vendor; invoice reference; GL account; acquisition source.
  • Location & Status History (1 row / asset-status interval): History record ID; Asset ID; Deployment ID; Contract ID; Customer ID; Site ID; branch / yard; location type; asset status; substatus; start date; end date; status-change reason; prior status; next status; related event ID.
  • Retirements & Disposals (1 row / disposal event): Disposal record ID; Asset ID; retirement date; disposal date; disposal method; disposal reason; customer buyout flag; final disposition; disposal proceeds; net book value; gain / loss; write-off amount; approval status; buyer / counterparty; document reference; acquisition source.
  • Definitions & Checks: Equipment taxonomy; status definitions; fleet-count tie-out; fixed-asset tie-out; duplicate serial checks; missing cost / location exceptions.
  • Primary analyses: Fleet composition; age & condition; capital intensity; ownership / disposition.

05. Monthly Operating Data Tape

  • Asset-Month Data (1 row / asset-month): Month end; Asset ID; Customer ID; Site ID; beginning status; ending status; deployed days; billed days; contracted rate; recurring revenue; one-time revenue; total revenue; service cost; installation cost; maintenance capex; growth capex.
  • Billing Detail (1 row / invoice line): Invoice ID; invoice line ID; invoice date; service period; Contract ID; Pricing line ID; fee type; rate / quantity; gross amount; discount / credit; tax / pass-through; net billed amount; revenue month; GL account; invoice status; payment status.
  • Operating Events (1 row / operating event): Event ID; Asset ID; Customer ID; Site ID; installation; removal; transfer; replacement; request date; scheduled date; completion date; final billing date; prior site; new site; prior status; event reason.
  • Service & Maintenance (1 row / work order): Work-order ID; Asset ID; Customer ID; Site ID; preventive service; reactive repair; emergency service; refurbishment; labor hours; labor cost; parts cost; contractor cost; downtime days; completion status; repeat service flag; resolution notes.
  • Reconciliation & Definitions: GL revenue tie-out; invoice register tie-out; Asset Master tie-out; event-count checks; revenue definitions; cost-category definitions.
  • Primary analyses: Revenue build; rate realization; operating activity; direct cost / margin.

06. Fleet Utilization

  • Asset Status History (1 row / asset-status interval): Asset ID; Customer ID; Site ID; branch / yard; status; substatus; available flag; billed flag; status start date; status end date; status days; expected availability; status-change reason; prior status; next assignment; related event ID.
  • Monthly Utilization (1 row / asset-month): Month end; asset type; region / branch; acquisition source; deployed days; billed days; idle days; repair days; deployment utilization; billing utilization; billing conversion; idle rate; revenue per fleet day; revenue per billed day; days to redeploy; idle aging bucket.
  • Fleet Summary (1 row / month-summary): Beginning fleet; units added; units installed; ending fleet; asset type; region / branch; acquisition source; age band; average deployed fleet; average billed fleet; average idle fleet; average unavailable fleet; deployment utilization; billing utilization; idle rate; unavailable rate.
  • Idle Asset List (1 row / idle asset): Asset ID; asset type; current yard; region; idle start date; idle days; aging bucket; prior removal date; condition grade; repair required; ready-to-deploy flag; estimated repair cost; next customer; expected install date; recommended action; responsible owner.
  • Definitions & Checks: Status hierarchy; utilization formulas; billed-overlay rules; calendar-day tie-out; Asset Master reconciliation; operating tape reconciliation.
  • Primary analyses: Deployment utilization; billing conversion; idle aging; redeployment speed.
