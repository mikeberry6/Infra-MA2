Multi-material and construction-debris recycling

  • Circular Services (Closed Loop Partners; Brookfield Global Transition Fund): Operates recycling and composting facilities, converting municipal and commercial materials into recovered commodities and organic products.
  • ReSource Waste Services (GCM Grosvenor; ReEnergy): Processes construction and demolition debris into reusable materials; also provides hauling, transfer and waste solidification.

Paper and commercial-material recycling

  • Royal Oak Recycling (Tamarix Equity Partners; LO3 preferred equity): Collects, processes and brokers commercial paper, cardboard, plastics and metals through a regional recycling network.
  • Sustana (Blackstone Tactical Opportunities): Collects recovered paper and manufactures recycled fiber and paper products; also provides secure shredding services.

Metals and industrial-material recovery

  • Venture Metals+ (Mill Rock Capital): Processes nonferrous metals and specialty alloys, supplying recycled feedstock to manufacturers through integrated collection and logistics.
  • Sortera Technologies (Macquarie Asset Management and other investors): Sorts mixed aluminum scrap into specification-grade alloy streams using proprietary sensors and automated processing technology.
  • Advanced Recycling Technologies (Ember Infrastructure): Owns and operates systems recovering metals from industrial residuals; also operates conventional scrap-metal processing.
  • Blue Phoenix Group; U.S. operations (InfraVia; Daiwa International Capital Partners): Processes incinerator ash to recover ferrous and nonferrous metals and produce secondary construction aggregates.

Plastics recycling and recycled-material manufacturing

  • Revolution Sustainable Solutions (Arsenal Capital Partners): Collects and recycles polyethylene film into resin, bags and other products through integrated recycling and manufacturing.
  • Tangent Technologies (Platinum Equity): Converts recovered plastics into synthetic lumber and engineered materials for outdoor, marine and structural applications.

Tires and rubber recycling

  • Liberty Tire Recycling (I Squared Capital): Collects and processes used tires into recycled rubber, mulch and other materials; also supplies tire-derived fuel.
  • Bolder Industries (Tiger Infrastructure Partners): Processes end-of-life tires into recovered carbon black and oil for rubber, chemical and fuel applications.
  • Ecore International (General Atlantic BeyondNetZero; minority investment): Converts reclaimed rubber into flooring, athletic surfaces and performance products through integrated recycling and manufacturing.

Organics, composting and nutrient recovery

  • Denali (TPG Growth): Collects and processes organic residuals into feed, compost and soil products; also provides biosolids-management services.
  • Living Earth (ES Capital Partners): Recycles green waste into mulch, compost and engineered soils; also sells landscaping products and aggregates.
  • Veransa (RFE Investment Partners): Processes wood and yard waste into compost, mulch and engineered soils; also provides grinding services.
  • Compost360 / Nature’s Choice (Sterling Group Foundation Fund): Processes organic waste into compost, mulch and engineered soils; combines material-processing services with landscaping-product sales.
  • Sedron Technologies (Ara Partners): Develops and operates waste-processing systems recovering water and fertilizers; municipal applications also produce fuel for electricity.

Used-oil, solvent and refrigerant reclamation

  • Blue Tide (Tailwater Capital; Shell minority): Re-refines used motor oil into lubricant base oils, combining feedstock procurement, processing and recovered-product sales.
  • CleanPlanet Chemical (Nuveen Private Equity Impact): Provides on-site solvent recycling as a service, recovering reusable chemicals from contaminated industrial process streams.
  • A-Gas; U.S. operations (TPG Rise Climate; KKR minority): Recovers and reclaims refrigerants for reuse; also provides gas destruction, on-site recovery and lifecycle-management services.

Battery recycling and recovered battery materials

  • Gopher Resource (Energy Capital Partners): Recycles spent lead-acid batteries, recovering reusable materials and processing customer-owned batteries under multi-year service contracts.
  • Cirba Solutions (The Heritage Group; EQT Infrastructure and Marubeni minority stakes): Collects, transports and recycles batteries across multiple chemistries, recovering materials from manufacturing scrap and retired batteries.
  • Blue Whale Materials (Ara Partners): Processes lithium-ion batteries into concentrated recovered materials containing nickel, cobalt, lithium and other battery metals.

Textile and composite-material recycling

  • Syre (TPG Rise Climate and other investors): Recycles polyester textile waste into circular polyester for new fabrics; develops and scales additional processing capacity.
  • Fairmat; U.S. operations (Infinity Recycling and other investors): Recycles carbon-fiber composite waste into engineered materials used in industrial, sporting-goods and other manufactured products.

Electronics and specialty-material recycling

  • ERI (Closed Loop Partners-backed; other shareholders): Recycles and dismantles electronics, recovers materials and destroys data; also refurbishes and resells reusable equipment.
  • Cal Micro Recycling (Tide Rock; within Full Circle Electronics): Recycles electronics, glass and mattresses; also provides product destruction and related secure material-management services.
