Multi-Material & Construction-Debris Recycling

  • Mixed-stream processing; economics driven by gate/processing fees, recovery yields and commodity sales.
  • Asset intensity centers on MRFs/C&D facilities; route density, permits and local material flows drive defensibility.

Circular Services (Closed Loop Partners; Brookfield Global Transition Fund)

  • Operates large-scale MRFs processing municipal and commercial single-stream and mixed recyclable volumes.
  • Separates paper, plastics, metals and glass into specification-grade commodities sold to downstream manufacturers.
  • Also owns composting and organics assets, broadening inbound material streams and processing infrastructure.

ReSource Waste Services (GCM Grosvenor; ReEnergy)

  • Operates regional facilities receiving and processing construction, demolition and other bulky waste streams.
  • Recovers aggregates, metals, wood and other reusable materials while minimizing residual landfill disposal.
  • Also provides hauling, transfer and material-solidification services, increasing control over inbound waste flows.

Paper & Commercial-Material Recycling

  • Collects and processes OCC, paper and other commercial recyclables for mills and manufacturers.
  • Economics depend on feedstock density, processing efficiency, recovered-fiber pricing and mill relationships.

Royal Oak Recycling (Tamarix Equity Partners; LO3 preferred equity)

  • Collects high-volume OCC, paper, plastics and metals from commercial and industrial waste generators.
  • Processes, grades and markets recovered materials into domestic and international downstream recycling markets.
  • Also provides hauling, brokerage and recycling-program management, supporting recurring customer relationships and feedstock access.

Sustana (Blackstone Tactical Opportunities)

  • Sources recovered paper and processes it into high-quality recycled fiber for manufacturing applications.
  • Vertically integrates recycled-fiber production with paper manufacturing, including packaging and specialty paper products.
  • Also provides paper collection and secure shredding, helping secure upstream feedstock and customer relationships.

Metals & Industrial-Material Recovery

  • Recovers metals from scrap, ash and industrial residual streams for reuse in manufacturing.
  • Value driven by sourcing access, processing capability, recovery yields and commodity/spread management.

Venture Metals+ (Mill Rock Capital)

  • Sources and processes nonferrous scrap, specialty alloys and higher-value industrial metal streams.
  • Produces specification-grade recycled metals sold into aerospace, automotive and broader industrial manufacturing markets.
  • Integrates collection, logistics and material preparation, supporting feedstock control and customer service capabilities.

Sortera Technologies (Macquarie Asset Management and other investors)

  • Processes mixed aluminum scrap using sensor-based, automated sorting and material-identification technology.
  • Separates mixed feedstock into higher-value alloy-specific streams suitable for direct manufacturing reuse.
  • Monetizes improved recovery yields and alloy purity, reducing dependence on lower-value mixed-scrap sales.

Advanced Recycling Technologies (Ember Infrastructure)

  • Owns and operates processing systems embedded at industrial and waste facilities to recover residual metals.
  • Extracts saleable ferrous and nonferrous metals that would otherwise remain within disposal-bound material streams.
  • Also operates conventional scrap-processing assets, providing additional sourcing, processing and commodity-marketing capabilities.

Blue Phoenix Group; U.S. operations (InfraVia; Daiwa International Capital Partners)

  • Operates specialized facilities processing bottom ash generated by municipal waste-to-energy plants.
  • Recovers ferrous and nonferrous metals while separating usable mineral fractions from remaining residual material.
  • Converts mineral output into secondary aggregates, creating both disposal avoidance and recovered-product revenue opportunities.

Plastics Recycling & Recycled-Material Manufacturing

  • Converts post-consumer/post-industrial plastics into resin, lumber and other recycled-content products.
  • Economics span processing margins, virgin-resin substitution, product demand and manufacturing utilization.

Revolution Sustainable Solutions (Arsenal Capital Partners)

  • Collects post-consumer and post-industrial polyethylene film through commercial, agricultural and industrial recycling programs.
  • Washes and processes recovered film into recycled resin suitable for downstream plastics manufacturing applications.
  • Vertically manufactures bags, film and related products, creating an internal outlet for recycled feedstock.

Tangent Technologies (Platinum Equity)

  • Processes post-consumer and post-industrial plastic waste into durable recycled-polymer feedstock and profiles.
  • Manufactures synthetic lumber and engineered materials for outdoor, marine, structural and infrastructure applications.
  • Vertical integration captures both recycling economics and value-added margins from differentiated finished-material products.

Tires & Rubber Recycling

  • Collects end-of-life tires and converts them into recovered rubber, carbon products and other outputs.
  • Economics combine collection/tipping revenue, processing yield and downstream recovered-product pricing.

Liberty Tire Recycling (I Squared Capital)

  • Operates a large collection network sourcing end-of-life tires from commercial, municipal and automotive customers.
  • Processes tires into crumb rubber, mulch and other recycled products for infrastructure and industrial applications.
  • Also monetizes reusable tires and tire-derived fuel, providing multiple outlets for recovered tire material.

Bolder Industries (Tiger Infrastructure Partners)

  • Processes end-of-life tires through thermal conversion facilities designed to recover embedded carbon and hydrocarbons.
  • Produces recovered carbon black and oil that can substitute for virgin inputs in industrial applications.
  • Economics depend on secured tire feedstock, plant utilization and qualification of recovered products with manufacturers.

Ecore International (General Atlantic BeyondNetZero; minority)

  • Sources reclaimed rubber, including end-of-life tire material, for use across vertically integrated processing operations.
  • Manufactures flooring, athletic surfaces and performance materials incorporating high levels of recovered rubber content.
  • Captures value through recycling, proprietary product design, manufacturing and distribution into specialized end markets.

Organics & Nutrient Recovery

  • Processes food, green waste and biosolids into compost, fertilizer, feed, energy and other beneficial-use outputs.
  • Economics combine collection/processing fees with recovered-product, RNG/electricity and environmental-attribute revenue.

Divert (Ara Partners and other investors)

  • Collects unsold food from retailers and other generators through dedicated logistics and processing infrastructure.
  • Depackages and processes food residuals, including through anaerobic digestion, to recover energy and nutrients.
  • Also provides food-donation, waste-prevention and analytics services, strengthening customer integration before material becomes waste.

Generate Upcycle (Generate Capital)

  • Owns and operates organics-processing and anaerobic-digestion facilities receiving food and agricultural residual streams.
  • Converts organic feedstock into renewable natural gas and electricity through controlled biological treatment processes.
  • Also produces fertilizer and nutrient-rich residual products, providing additional beneficial-use outlets and revenue streams.

Agri-Cycle (Closed Loop Partners)

  • Collects commercial food waste from grocery, foodservice, manufacturing and other recurring generator locations.
  • Directs material to anaerobic digestion, composting, animal-feed and other beneficial-use outlets based on suitability.
  • Provides logistics, diversion management and reporting, making route density and customer retention key value drivers.

Denali (TPG Growth)

  • Collects food waste, biosolids and other organic residuals from municipal, commercial and industrial customers.
  • Processes material through depackaging, composting, land application and other beneficial-use pathways.
  • Also provides transportation, grease recovery and residual-management services, creating a broad organics-services network.

Restaurant Technologies (Energy Capital Partners)

  • Installs closed-loop cooking-oil systems at restaurants and other large commercial foodservice customer locations.
  • Delivers fresh cooking oil while automatically collecting used cooking oil through recurring route-based service.
  • Aggregates recovered UCO for renewable-fuel markets, supplementing core service economics with feedstock monetization.

Living Earth (ES Capital Partners)

  • Receives and processes green waste, brush, wood waste and other organic residual materials.
  • Converts inbound material into mulch, compost, engineered soils and other higher-value landscaping products.
  • Also distributes aggregates and landscape materials, providing complementary product revenue through its facility network.

Veransa (RFE Investment Partners)

  • Operates regional processing facilities receiving yard waste, wood waste and other green organic materials.
  • Converts feedstock into compost, mulch and engineered soils sold into landscaping and agricultural markets.
  • Also provides grinding and material-management services, creating both processing-fee and recovered-product revenue streams.

Compost360 / Nature’s Choice (Sterling Group Foundation Fund)

  • Operates organics-processing facilities receiving yard waste and other compostable materials from regional generators.
  • Converts inbound organic material into compost, mulch, soil blends and other beneficial-use products.
  • Pursues a buy-and-build strategy around permitted processing sites, local feedstock relationships and product distribution.

Sedron Technologies (Ara Partners)

  • Develops and operates advanced systems treating municipal biosolids, manure and other high-moisture residual streams.
  • Recovers clean water, concentrated fertilizers and other useful products from traditionally difficult-to-manage waste streams.
  • Certain applications also produce renewable energy, creating multiple recovery pathways from the same inbound material.

Used-Oil, Solvent & Refrigerant Reclamation

  • Recovers used oils, solvents and refrigerants for reuse rather than disposal or virgin replacement.
  • Economics driven by collection density, processing utilization, recovered-product spreads and service fees.

Crystal Clean (J.F. Lehman & Company)

  • Operates route-based collection networks sourcing used motor oil from automotive and industrial customer locations.
  • Re-refines collected oil into reusable base oils and separately recovers solvents and antifreeze for reuse.
  • Also provides parts cleaning, hazardous waste and environmental services, creating broader recurring customer relationships.

Blue Tide (Tailwater Capital; Shell minority)

  • Sources used motor oil and other eligible feedstocks through collection and third-party supply relationships.
  • Re-refines used oil into high-quality lubricant base oils that substitute for virgin petroleum-derived products.
  • Also produces gas oil and asphalt-related outputs, providing additional monetization of the refining process.

CleanPlanet Chemical (Nuveen Private Equity Impact)

  • Installs solvent-recovery equipment directly within customer industrial facilities under recurring service arrangements.
  • Purifies contaminated solvents for repeated on-site reuse, reducing virgin chemical purchases and waste-disposal requirements.
  • Business model combines embedded equipment, recurring processing volumes and recycling-as-a-service customer relationships.

A-Gas; U.S. operations (TPG Rise Climate; KKR minority)

  • Collects used refrigerants from HVAC, refrigeration and other installed equipment across commercial end markets.
  • Reclaims recovered gas to required specifications, enabling resale and reuse instead of virgin refrigerant production.
  • Also provides recovery, destruction and lifecycle-management services, including management of regulated refrigerant inventories.

Battery Recycling & Materials Recovery

  • Collects and processes spent batteries and manufacturing scrap to recover metals and reusable materials.
  • Economics vary by chemistry, feedstock contracts, commodity value, processing depth and output qualification.

Gopher Resource (Energy Capital Partners)

  • Operates large-scale facilities processing spent lead-acid batteries through established closed-loop recycling infrastructure.
  • Recovers lead and polypropylene that are returned to battery and other industrial manufacturing supply chains.
  • Meaningful volumes are processed under customer-owned, fee-for-service structures, reducing direct commodity-price exposure.

Cirba Solutions (The Heritage Group; EQT Infrastructure and Marubeni minority)

  • Operates battery collection, logistics and processing infrastructure across multiple battery chemistries and end markets.
  • Processes both manufacturing scrap and end-of-life batteries to recover valuable metals and reusable materials.
  • Expanding downstream capabilities to produce higher-value recycled inputs for domestic battery-material supply chains.

Blue Whale Materials (Ara Partners)

  • Processes end-of-life lithium-ion batteries and battery-manufacturing scrap through dedicated recycling infrastructure.
  • Produces concentrated recovered material containing lithium, nickel, cobalt and other economically valuable battery metals.
  • Supplies material to downstream refiners, with economics driven by feedstock access, recovery yields and metal values.

Textile & Composite-Material Recycling

  • Recovers difficult-to-recycle textile and composite materials into new industrial feedstocks.
  • Growth-oriented subsector; economics depend on technology scale-up, customer qualification and manufacturing substitution.

Syre (TPG Rise Climate and other investors)

  • Processes polyester textile waste that is difficult to recycle through conventional mechanical recycling systems.
  • Converts recovered polyester into specification-grade circular feedstock suitable for producing new textile fibers.
  • Builds long-term supply and offtake relationships with apparel customers while scaling dedicated recycling capacity.

Fairmat (Infinity Recycling and other investors)

  • Sources carbon-fiber composite scrap from aerospace and other advanced-manufacturing production processes.
  • Mechanically processes composite waste into engineered recycled materials with retained structural performance characteristics.
  • Supplies differentiated materials into industrial and consumer applications, replacing higher-cost virgin composite inputs.

Electronics & Specialty-Material Recycling

  • Recovers metals, plastics and components from retired electronics and other difficult specialty waste streams.
  • Economics combine processing fees, recovered commodities, secure destruction and equipment resale/refurbishment.

ERI (Closed Loop Partners-backed; other shareholders)

  • Operates physical electronics-recycling facilities receiving enterprise, consumer and regulated end-of-life electronic equipment.
  • Dismantles and processes devices to recover metals, plastics and other valuable materials for downstream reuse.
  • Also provides secure data destruction, refurbishment and resale, increasing recovery value before residual material recycling.

Cal Micro Recycling (Tide Rock; within Full Circle Electronics)

  • Processes electronics, glass, mattresses and other specialty waste streams through dedicated recycling and recovery operations.
  • Separates usable commodities and recyclable materials for resale into appropriate downstream manufacturing and recovery markets.
  • Also provides secure product destruction and material-management services for commercial, institutional and government customers.
