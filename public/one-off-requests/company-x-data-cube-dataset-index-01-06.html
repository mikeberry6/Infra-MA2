01. Customer & Site Master

Data Cube: Commercial & Customer Data Cube
Dataset Index: 01
Purpose: Customer, site and hierarchy data supporting concentration, end-market, geographic and site-level revenue analysis.

How the Data Connects

1. Ultimate Parent — Roll-up level
    Top of the customer hierarchy.
2. Customer Account — 1 row / customer
    Individual customer account.
3. Physical Site — 1 row / site
    Specific operating location.
4. Contract / Asset — Downstream joins
    Assets and contracts at the site.
