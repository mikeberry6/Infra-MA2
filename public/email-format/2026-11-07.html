Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • October 31 – November 6, 2026
KEY THEMES
J.P. Morgan AM clients agreed to buy DTE Vantage for ~US$3bn, while I Squared and ADIA agreed to form Constellation Aviation with a US$1.4bn seed fleet and ECP agreed to sell Harvestone for US$915mm.
U.S. deployment also included ECP’s acquisition of 892MW of operating cogeneration assets and Axium’s agreement to acquire a 95% stake in the University of Kansas Gateway District student housing project.
Power & ET5 Deals
DTE Vantage | J.P. Morgan AM
J.P. Morgan AM (Buyout) · Distributed Energy & Renewables · United States
Institutional investors advised by J.P. Morgan AM’s Global Infrastructure group agreed to acquire DTE Energy’s non-utility Vantage business for ~US$3bn. The sale includes renewable gas recovery, wholesale power and renewables, and on-site energy projects. DTE is retaining its coke operations.
Source
Harvestone Low Carbon Partners | ECP
ECP (Sale) · Low-Carbon Fuels · United States
ECP agreed to sell Harvestone Low Carbon Partners to The Andersons for US$915mm. Its three ethanol plants in North Dakota and Indiana can produce ~215mm gallons annually, and the Blue Flint facility operates a carbon capture and storage system.
Source
Aypa Power | NBIM / Brookfield
NBIM / Brookfield (Co-Investment) · Battery Storage · United States & Canada
NBIM disclosed a US$300mm direct investment for 10% of Aypa Power alongside Brookfield. The investment is separate from NBIM’s existing indirect exposure through Brookfield’s Global Transition Fund. Aypa has ~6.5GW of battery storage in operation, under construction or under contract.
Source
Puna Geothermal | Northleaf
Northleaf (Sale) · Geothermal Power · United States
Ormat disclosed a definitive agreement to buy Northleaf’s remaining interest in Hawaii’s Puna Geothermal plant for up to US$59.5mm, including a US$21.1mm payment tied to a repowering milestone. Ormat will own 100% of the plant at closing.
Source
892MW U.S. Cogeneration Portfolio | ECP / Fengate
ECP / Fengate (Buyout) · Gas-Fired Cogeneration · United States
ECP acquired three gas-fired cogeneration plants totaling 892MW from Fengate. The facilities in Texas and Illinois supply steam and electricity to two petrochemical complexes under long-term contracts.
Source
Digital3 Deals
Vizag Mega Data Center Park | Sixth Street
Sixth Street (Bolt-On) · Data Center Development · India
AdaniConneX, the joint venture between Adani Enterprises and Sixth Street-backed EdgeConneX, signed an agreement to buy Vizag Mega Data Center Park for ~INR10.86bn. The pre-operating company holds land and licenses for infrastructure development in Visakhapatnam.
Source
Speedcast Teleport Infrastructure | EQT Infrastructure
EQT Infrastructure (Bolt-On) · Satellite Ground Infrastructure · Global
EQT Infrastructure-backed SatPort acquired Speedcast’s network of 12 teleports across 11 countries. The acquisition expands SatPort’s carrier-neutral satellite ground network across five continents, and Speedcast will continue using the sites under a long-term services agreement.
Source
Ubiquity | Generate Capital
Generate Capital (Sale) · Fiber Networks · United States
1248 Holdings and affiliates acquired Generate Capital’s ownership interests in Ubiquity, whose fiber networks pass more than 200,000 addresses across Texas and Nebraska. The founding investor plans to provide additional capital to expand the network and FiberFirst internet service.
Source
Transportation1 Deal
Constellation Aviation | I Squared / ADIA
I Squared / ADIA (Joint Venture) · Aircraft Leasing · Global
I Squared and ADIA agreed to form an aircraft leasing venture with Avolon, which will retain a minority stake and manage the fleet. The platform is expected to buy 22 aircraft valued at US$1.4bn from Avolon, with leases to 17 airlines across 15 countries.
Source
Social Infra1 Deal
University of Kansas Gateway District Student Housing | Axium
Axium (Majority Stake) · Student Housing P3 · United States
An Axium-managed fund agreed to acquire 95% of a 482-bed student housing project under construction in Lawrence, Kansas. The two buildings are being developed under a 99-year air rights lease and are expected to open for the 2028–29 academic year.
