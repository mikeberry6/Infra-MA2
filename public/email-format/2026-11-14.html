Weekly Briefing – Infrastructure Sponsor M&A – 16 deals across Power & ET, Utilities, Midstream, Social Infra – November 7–13, 2026
GUGGENHEIM
INFRASTRUCTURE COVERAGE & ADVISORY
Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • November 7 – 13, 2026
KEY THEMES
PSP Investments’ C$1.6bn agreement for half of Enercare led disclosed deal values, while I Squared Capital and Allianz Global Investors joined a £100mm equity commitment to Aurora Utilities and Indonesia Investment Authority invested in GIP-backed Vena Group’s 2.8 GW renewables portfolio.
U.S. deployment centered on KKR and Diamondback Energy’s agreement for Deep Blue Midland Basin, Blackstone’s purchase agreement for Portland Natural Gas Transmission System and operating-asset acquisitions by TPG-backed Altus Power and Ember Infrastructure-backed H2O Innovation.
Power & ET8 Deals
Keppel Sakra Cogen | Keppel Infrastructure Fund Management / Keppel Asia Infrastructure Fund
Keppel Infrastructure Fund Management / Keppel Asia Infrastructure Fund (Majority Stake) · Gas-Fired Power · Singapore
Keppel Infrastructure Trust agreed to acquire a 70% interest from Keppel Asia Infrastructure Fund and a co-investor for up to S$111.6mm. The 600 MW hydrogen-compatible combined-cycle plant on Jurong Island will operate under a 30-year capacity tolling agreement.
Source
Belvedere di Aquileia | CIP
CIP (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Agrivoltaics and Storage · Italy
CIP-backed Elgin acquired its sixth Italian project, a 57 MW agrivoltaic development paired with 15 MW of battery storage in Friuli Venezia Giulia. Estimated construction cost is €46mm.
Source
Vena Group Southeast Asia Renewables | GIP
GIP (Co-Investment) · Solar and Wind · Indonesia / Philippines
Indonesia Investment Authority invested in a 2.8 GW portfolio from GIP-backed Vena Group spanning operating, construction, contracted and development-stage solar and wind projects. Vena will retain an ownership and operating role.
Source
Arevon U.S. Solar and Storage Portfolio | TPG
TPG (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Solar and Storage · U.S.
TPG-backed Altus Power acquired 92 operating projects across 13 U.S. states from Arevon. The assets add nearly 200 MW of solar and 10 MWh of battery storage to Altus’s operating portfolio.
Source
METLEN U.K. Solar Portfolio | IFM Investors
IFM Investors (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Utility-Scale Solar · U.K.
IFM Investors-backed Nala Renewables signed purchase agreements for four solar projects totaling approximately 119 MW from METLEN. The sites in Norfolk, Somerset and East Sussex are ready to build or under construction, with delivery expected in 2027 and 2028.
Source
Minerva Messina | Nuveen Infrastructure
Nuveen Infrastructure (Sale) · Onshore Wind · Italy
Dolomiti Energia acquired all shares of Minerva Messina, the owner of a 48.3 MW operating wind farm in Sicily, from Nuveen Infrastructure’s Glennmont platform.
Source
French Operating Solar Portfolio | J.P. Morgan Asset Management
J.P. Morgan Asset Management (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Utility-Scale Solar · France
Sonnedix, backed by J.P. Morgan Asset Management’s Infrastructure Investments Fund, acquired four operating solar plants totaling 37 MWp across southwestern and southeastern France. The purchases take its operating capacity in France above 430 MWp.
Source
Enerix Franchise | Igneo
Igneo (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Distributed Energy · Germany
Igneo-backed MVV took full ownership of Enerix, a German franchise network for rooftop solar, batteries and heat pumps, after investing in the business in 2016. Its approximately 90 franchise partners remain independent.
Source
Utilities3 Deals
Enercare | PSP Investments / Brookfield
PSP Investments / Brookfield (50% Stake) · Home Infrastructure · Canada
PSP Investments signed an agreement to acquire a 50% interest in Enercare from Brookfield and its institutional partners for approximately C$1.6bn. The platform provides water heating, HVAC and water purification equipment and related services to more than 1.4mm Canadian homes and businesses.
Source
Aurora Utilities | I Squared Capital / Allianz Global Investors
I Squared Capital / Allianz Global Investors (Primary Capital Raise) · Electricity Distribution · U.K.
I Squared Capital, Allianz Global Investors, Kåpan and M&G Investments committed an additional £100mm to Aurora Utilities, taking commitments to £250mm since 2024. The regulated distribution operator will use the new capital to expand electricity grid connections across Great Britain.
Source
Emory University WaterHub | Ember Infrastructure
Ember Infrastructure (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Water Reuse · U.S.
Ember Infrastructure-backed H2O Innovation acquired the WaterHub at Emory University in Atlanta. The facility treats up to 400,000 gallons per day and supplies reclaimed water under a long-term agreement, covering nearly 40% of the university’s water needs.
Source
Midstream3 Deals
Deep Blue Midland Basin | KKR / Five Point Infrastructure
KKR / Five Point Infrastructure (Majority Stake) · Produced Water Infrastructure · U.S.
KKR and existing minority owner Diamondback Energy agreed to acquire Five Point Infrastructure’s 70% stake in Deep Blue. The Texas platform handles more than 2.2mm barrels of water per day across its Midland Basin network of more than 2,000 miles of pipelines.
Source
Nowega | Igneo
Igneo (Buyout) · Natural Gas and Hydrogen Transmission · Germany
Igneo’s European Diversified Infrastructure Fund IV signed a binding agreement to acquire 100% of Nowega from Erdgas Münster. The regulated operator owns approximately 1,500 km of pipelines carrying natural gas and hydrogen in northwestern Germany.
Source
Portland Natural Gas Transmission System | Blackstone / GIP / Morgan Stanley Infrastructure Partners
Blackstone / GIP / Morgan Stanley Infrastructure Partners (Buyout) · Natural Gas Transmission · U.S. / Canada
Blackstone Infrastructure agreed to acquire Portland Natural Gas Transmission System from GIP and Morgan Stanley Infrastructure Partners. The 296-mile regulated pipeline connects New England and Atlantic Canada with North American gas supply.
Source
Social Infra2 Deals
Le Mas Campsite | InfraVia
InfraVia (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Outdoor Hospitality · France
InfraVia-backed Sandaya acquired Le Mas in Martigues, a 300-pitch campsite on five hectares on France’s Mediterranean coast. The site expands Sandaya’s network in southern France.
Source
Chianti Glamping Resort | InfraVia
InfraVia (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Outdoor Hospitality · Italy
InfraVia-backed Sandaya acquired Chianti Glamping Resort in Cavriglia, Tuscany. The 257-pitch property spans 23 hectares and expands the group’s Italian network.
Source
