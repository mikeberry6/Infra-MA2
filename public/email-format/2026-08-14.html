Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • August 8 – August 14, 2026
KEY THEMES
CPP’ US$10.2bn enterprise-value take-private of ReNew led the week, followed by Blackstone’s US$1.5bn MarineMax buyout and ReNew’s US$532mm solar-asset divestiture in India.
U.S. deployment centered on Blackstone’s MarineMax operating network and GIC / Macquarie AM’s Theseus data-center platform, alongside GSAM’s Tosca buyout and the SkyPlace and Hussung / HMC PortCo acquisitions.
Power & ET4 Deals
ReNew Energy Global | CPP / ADIA
CPP / ADIA (Buyout / Rollover) · Renewable Power Platform · India
CPP and founder Sumant Sinha signed a binding agreement to acquire ReNew’s remaining shares for US$7.02 per share, implying approximately US$2.8bn of equity value and US$10.2bn of enterprise value; ADIA-controlled Platinum Cactus will roll its stake. ReNew operates an approximately 20.2 GW clean-energy portfolio.
Source
ReNew Solar Portfolio | ADIA / CPP
ADIA / CPP / ReNew (PortCo Divestiture) · Solar Generation · India
ADIA- and CPP-backed ReNew signed a definitive agreement to sell six operating solar-project companies totaling 1,055 MW to Purvah Green Power for approximately US$532mm. The transaction advances ReNew’s capital-recycling program.
Source
Anesco | Ara
Ara / Nature Infrastructure Capital (Co-Investment) · Solar & Battery Storage · United Kingdom / Northern Europe
Nature Infrastructure Capital invested in Anesco alongside existing shareholder Ara to accelerate the renewable developer and operator’s growth. Anesco has delivered more than 1.3 GW and manages more than 1.3 GW across solar and battery assets, with a development pipeline above 7 GW.
Source
Hussung Mechanical / HMC Service | Bernhard Capital
Bernhard Capital / Optimum Energy (PortCo Acquisition) · Mechanical & Energy Infrastructure Services · Kentucky, United States
Bernhard-backed Optimum Energy acquired Hussung Mechanical Contractors and HMC Service Company, adding HVAC, plumbing, building-automation and mechanical-infrastructure capabilities. The businesses serve healthcare, education, industrial and other mission-critical facilities.
Source
Transportation4 Deals
MarineMax | Blackstone
Blackstone / Safe Harbor (PortCo Acquisition) · Marinas & Recreational Marine · United States
Blackstone Infrastructure-backed Safe Harbor agreed to acquire all outstanding MarineMax shares for US$53.00 per share in cash, valuing the transaction at approximately US$1.5bn. The combination adds MarineMax’s boat dealerships and marine-service operations to Safe Harbor’s marina platform.
Source
LEAP India | KKR
KKR (IPO) · Pallet Pooling & Supply-Chain Logistics · India
KKR-backed LEAP India began public trading on August 14 following a US$260mm IPO comprising newly issued shares and a larger offer for sale led by KKR-backed Vertical Holdings II. The pallet-pooling platform was valued at approximately US$725mm at debut.
Source
Tosca | GSAM
GSAM (Buyout) · Reusable Packaging & Supply-Chain Pooling · United States
Infrastructure at Goldman Sachs Alternatives agreed to acquire Tosca from Apax funds. The Atlanta-based platform owns and manages reusable containers, pallets and bulk bins across a global wash-and-service network serving perishable-food supply chains.
Source
SkyPlace FBO | Tallvine
Tallvine / Velocity FBO Network (PortCo Acquisition) · Fixed-Base Aviation Services · San Antonio, Texas
Tallvine-backed Velocity FBO Network acquired SkyPlace FBO, expanding its airport-services platform into San Antonio. The bolt-on adds fueling, hangar, ramp and passenger-support capabilities at San Antonio International Airport.
Source
Social Infra3 Deals
oOh!media Limited | I Squared
I Squared (Buyout) · Out-of-Home Media Infrastructure · Australia / New Zealand
I Squared entered into a binding scheme agreement to acquire all oOh!media shares, implying an A$898mm equity value and approximately A$1.04bn enterprise value. The platform operates roughly 30,000 digital and static assets across roadside, transit, airport and other locations.
Source
Capstec | EQT Infra
EQT Infra / SK Shieldus (PortCo Divestiture) · Integrated Security Services · South Korea
Genesis Private Equity signed to acquire 100% of Capstec, including EQT Infrastructure VI-backed SK Shieldus’s 80.02% interest. The sale divests an integrated security-services business from the broader SK Shieldus platform.
Source
Elysium Cymru | Ancala
Ancala / Iris Care Group (PortCo Acquisition) · Specialist Care & Supported Living · Wales, United Kingdom
Ancala-backed Iris Care Group acquired Elysium Cymru and its two supported-living services. The bolt-on expands Iris’s specialist complex-care pathway in Wales and adds services for adults with mental-health and learning-disability needs.
Source
Digital2 Deals
Theseus Infrastructure | GIC / Macquarie AM
GIC / Macquarie AM (Joint Venture) · Hyperscale Data Centers · United States
GIC and Macquarie-managed funds formed and will co-own Theseus Infrastructure to develop, operate and lease purpose-built U.S. data centers to Anthropic as anchor tenant. The sponsors will fund the majority of each project’s equity under the multi-site platform.
Source
firstcolo | CVC DIF / Cube Infra
CVC DIF / Cube Infra (Buyout / Sale) · Colocation Data Centers · Germany
CVC DIF agreed to acquire a significant majority stake in firstcolo through DIF Value Add IV from Cube Infrastructure Fund III. The Frankfurt-area operator hosts approximately 20,000 server systems and is developing an AI-ready Rosbach facility with up to 24 MW of capacity.
Source
Utilities1 Deal
Neumann Group | Igneo Infra
Igneo Infra (Majority Stake) · Waste Recycling & Resource Recovery · Germany
Igneo signed a binding agreement to acquire a majority stake in Neumann through its European Diversified Infrastructure strategy. Neumann operates six German sites and provides incinerator-bottom-ash recycling, logistics and related services to energy-from-waste facilities.
Source
