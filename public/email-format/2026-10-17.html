Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • October 10 – October 16, 2026
KEY THEMES
KKR’s US$1.2bn, 700MW U.S. solar portfolio led disclosed transaction value, alongside Ares’ 1.9GW Statera investment and CIP’s 25% acquisition in the up-to-1,080MW Five Estuaries project.
U.S. deployment centered on KKR’s Texas and Ohio solar acquisition and Ara Partners’ majority investment in Pacific Ag’s national logistics platform; I Squared’s Kinetik share disposals were secondary liquidity rather than new operating capital.
Power & ET8 Deals
Texas / Ohio solar portfolio | KKR
KKR (Joint Venture) · Solar · United States
KKR and TotalEnergies signed an agreement to add three U.S. solar assets totaling 700MW to their existing 50/50 JV at US$1.2bn enterprise value. The projects are expected to enter commercial operation by Q1 2027; TotalEnergies will remain operator and completion is expected in 2026.
Source
Five UK wind farm minority interests | Schroders Greencoat
Schroders Greencoat (Minority Stake) · Onshore Wind · United Kingdom
Greencoat UK Wind acquired EDF Invest’s remaining 20% interests in five operating wind farms with 96.4MW of total capacity for ~£10.5mm, increasing ownership to 100%. Full control supports life-extension and potential repowering work; the acquired capacity interest is 19.28MW.
Source
Statera operating energy portfolio | Ares
Ares (Minority Stake) · Storage & Flexible Generation · United Kingdom
Ares Infrastructure Equity funds acquired a significant minority stake in EQT Infrastructure VI-backed Statera’s ~1.9GW operating storage and flexible-generation portfolio. Assets include 680MW Carrington Storage and 750MW Thurrock Flexible Generation; the stake percentage and consideration were undisclosed.
Source
Five Estuaries | CIP / Macquarie AM
CIP / Macquarie AM (Minority Stake / Sale) · Offshore Wind · United Kingdom
CIP’s Infrastructure V acquired the Macquarie-led consortium’s 25% interest in Five Estuaries, an up-to-1,080MW offshore wind development off Essex and Suffolk. The project received development consent in December 2025; the acquired interest represents up to 270MW and consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Ixus Bugallon solar project | GIP
GIP (Minority Stake Sale) · Solar · Philippines
GIP-backed Vena Energy signed agreements for Norfund and Impact Fund Denmark to invest a combined 49% in its 500MWp Ixus Bugallon solar project. Vena retains ownership and operational involvement; completion requires Philippine competition approval and customary conditions, and equity consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Korean industrial rooftop solar JV | Stonepeak
Stonepeak (Joint Venture) · Solar · South Korea
Stonepeak-owned Peak Energy formed a JV with the Hyundai Motor Securities-backed Vencore Rooftop Equity Fund to develop industrial rooftop solar for automotive suppliers. The platform targets 100MW within two years and identifies five initial supplier projects; investment value was undisclosed.
Source
Swedish / Finnish wind portfolio | Ardian
Ardian (Bolt-On) · Onshore Wind · Sweden / Finland
Enordic Evergreen, wholly owned by Ardian Clean Energy Evergreen Fund, acquired three operating wind farms totaling 73.2MW. The portfolio comprises Anneberg and Sötterfällan in Sweden and Silovuori in Finland; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Hynamics | Hy24
Hy24 (Buyout) · Low-Carbon Hydrogen · France / United Kingdom
Hy24’s Clean Hydrogen Infrastructure Fund signed an agreement to acquire Hynamics from EDF Pulse Ventures. The platform develops and supplies low-carbon hydrogen for industry and heavy mobility in France and the UK; consideration was undisclosed and closing is expected in Q1 2027, subject to approvals.
Source

Kinetik — 2mm-share block | I Squared
I Squared (Sale) · Gas Gathering & Processing · United States
I Squared’s Buzzard Midstream sold 2mm Kinetik Class A shares on October 13 at US$54.77 each, generating US$109.54mm before expenses. October 15 filings first confirmed the completed block sale in the Delaware Basin midstream operator; the associated unit conversion is not counted separately.

DCC Energy — 61,481-share sale | UBS AM
UBS AM (Sale) · Energy Distribution & Storage · Ireland
UBS Asset Management disclosed the October 14 sale of 61,481 DCC Energy ordinary shares at £64.305 each, or ~£3.95mm. This is a secondary listed-equity disposal in an energy distributor with operating terminals and storage facilities; it provides no new capital to DCC.

I Squared’s Buzzard Midstream sold 58,439 Kinetik Class A shares on October 9 for ~US$3.17mm, calculated from reported weighted-average prices. The October 13 filing first confirmed completion; this tranche is separate from the October 12 and October 13 sales.

I Squared’s Buzzard Midstream sold 38,377 Kinetik Class A shares on October 12 for ~US$2.08mm at a reported weighted-average price of US$54.1851. The October 13 filings confirmed the completed secondary disposal in the gas gathering, processing and transportation operator.

Transportation2 Deals
Pacific Ag | Ara Partners
Ara Partners (Majority Stake) · Agricultural Feedstock Logistics · United States
Ara Infrastructure Fund I acquired a majority interest in Pacific Ag. The business operates a national crop-residue harvesting, storage and logistics network supplying dairies, animal-protein producers and other end markets; the investment supports expansion and consideration was undisclosed.
Source
De Gunst SSAN logistics operations | Macquarie AM
Macquarie AM (Bolt-On) · Specialist Bulk Logistics · Australia
Macquarie infrastructure-backed Qube acquired De Gunst Transport’s security-sensitive ammonium nitrate logistics operations, including a depot in Gracemere, Queensland. Completion on October 9 was first publicly confirmed in October 12 coverage, expanding Qube’s mine-resupply and dangerous-goods platform; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Social Infra2 Deals
Marius Pedersen Czech / Slovak operations | Vauban
Vauban (Bolt-On) · Waste Management · Czech Republic / Slovakia
Vauban-backed Paprec signed the acquisition of Marius Pedersen’s Czech and Slovak waste-management operations. The acquired network comprises 49 subsidiaries and JVs handling nearly 3mm tonnes annually and serving over 2,000 municipalities; consideration was undisclosed and competition approvals remain pending.
Source
Orchard Healthcare Group | Wren House
Wren House (Bolt-On) · Specialist Dementia Care · United Kingdom
Wren House-owned Voyage Care completed the acquisition of Orchard Healthcare Group, expanding its specialist dementia and complex mental-health care capabilities. Orchard will continue operating separately within the group; purchase price and acquired bed capacity were undisclosed.
Source
Digital1 Deal
PISCES subsea cable JV | Equitix
Equitix (Joint Venture) · Subsea Fiber · Ireland / France / Spain / Portugal
Equitix Infra Ireland formed PISCES Cable System Limited with MDM and Orange to deliver a ~3,125km, 16-fiber-pair subsea cable linking Ireland directly with continental Europe. The greenfield system targets deployment in 2028 and benefits from EU co-funding; the partners’ equity commitment was undisclosed.
