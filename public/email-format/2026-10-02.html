GUGGENHEIM

Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • September 26 – October 2, 2026
KEY THEMES
CPP's A$4.5bn Sydney toll-road exit and Vicinity Energy's US$2.92bn enterprise valuation led disclosed scale, while KKR backed India's Cisternina storage platform.
U.S. deployment included Harrison Street's Vicinity majority purchase, Antin Infra's HP Helicopters, Wafra's Liberty rail platform and CVC DIF's AJ Myers add-on, complemented by tax and preferred equity for solar and storage projects.
Transportation6 Deals
WestConnex / NorthWestern Roads Group | CPP
CPP (Sale) · Toll roads · Australia
CPP agreed to sell its 10.5% interest in WestConnex and 25% interest in NorthWestern Roads Group to Transurban for expected gross proceeds of A$4.5bn. The Sydney assets include Westlink M7 and NorthConnex.
Source
Hector Rail | InfraRed / Ancala
InfraRed (Buyout) · Rail freight · Scandinavia / Germany
InfraRed-managed funds agreed to acquire 100% of Hector Rail from Ancala. The independent rail-freight operator runs around 100 electric locomotives across Scandinavia and Germany.
Source
Liberty Development Partners | Wafra
Wafra (Platform Acquisition) · Rail infrastructure · U.S.
Wafra-advised funds invested in Liberty and its Gulf Inland Logistics Park and CMC Railroad assets near Houston. The 3,900-acre park connects to Union Pacific and BNSF through CMC, which provides switching and storage capacity for over 1,000 railcars.
Source
Omni Air International | Stonepeak
Stonepeak (PortCo Divestiture) · Passenger aviation · U.S.
Stonepeak-backed ATSG agreed to sell Omni Air International to OAI Holdings. The Tulsa-based airline provides passenger charter and aircraft, crew, maintenance and insurance services in over 80 countries annually; ATSG will focus on cargo aviation after closing.
Source
HP Helicopters | Antin Infra
Antin Infra (Majority Stake) · Specialty aviation · U.S.
Antin Infra's NextGen Infrastructure Fund I acquired a majority stake in HP Helicopters, with founder Brad Bauder retaining a minority interest. The operator provides heavy-lift services for utilities, firefighting and other customers across ten Western U.S. states.
Source
AJ Myers and Sons | CVC DIF
CVC DIF (PortCo Acquisition) · Student transportation · U.S.
CVC DIF-backed American Student Transportation Partners acquired AJ Myers and Sons through Krise Transportation. The acquisition adds an established student-transport operator in western Pennsylvania to ASTP's contracted school-bus platform.
Source
Power & ET5 Deals
Dolan / Kylertown Solar | True Green Capital
True Green Capital (Project Equity) · Solar · U.S.
True Green Capital-backed CleanChoice secured a US$61mm tax-equity commitment from Advantage Capital for Dolan in New York and Kylertown in Pennsylvania. The two solar projects, around 27 MWdc and 28 MWdc respectively, will expand CleanChoice's operating portfolio.
Source
Vector Renewables | J.P. Morgan AM
J.P. Morgan AM (PortCo Divestiture) · Renewable-energy services · Spain
IIF-backed Nadara sold its entire stake in Madrid-based Vector Renewables to Algebris Green Transition Fund. The global renewable-energy services business manages over 6 GW of assets and provides technical advisory, legal advisory and digital solutions.
Source
Pinnacle IV | Ares
Ares (PortCo Divestiture) · Wind / Solar · U.S.
Ares-backed Apex sold a 49% interest in Pinnacle IV to EGCO, retaining 51%. The 339 MW operating portfolio comprises 189 MW Timbermill Wind in North Carolina and 150 MW Coldwater Solar in Michigan.
Source
Mesa View BESS | EnCap
EnCap (Project Equity) · Battery storage · U.S.
EnCap-backed Linea secured a preferred-equity commitment from DESRI for the 250 MW / 500 MWh Mesa View battery project in Upton County, Texas. The project is expected online in 2027; the equity investment amount was not disclosed.
Source
Spring Creek Solar | Ares
Ares (Project Equity) · Solar · U.S.
Ares-backed Apex secured tax equity from Barclays for the 150 MW Spring Creek Solar project in Illinois. The project is expected to enter operation in 2027; the tax-equity commitment amount was not disclosed.
Source
Midstream4 Deals
Hardisty DRU / Port Arthur Terminal | ECP
ECP (PortCo Divestiture) · Crude-oil logistics · Canada / U.S.
A Jefferson Energy affiliate agreed to acquire a 50% stake in the Hardisty Diluent Recovery Unit from ECP-backed USD Group, alongside Port Arthur Terminal, for US$255mm total cash consideration. The bundled assets connect Alberta crude supply with Gulf Coast refining; the disclosed price covers the entire package.
Source
Cisternina Logistics | KKR
KKR (Majority Stake) · Liquid storage / Logistics · India
KKR signed definitive agreements for a majority investment in Cisternina through its Asia Pacific infrastructure strategy. The investment supports the proposed acquisition of GBL's terminal and rail businesses, with around 500,000 KL of operating and under-construction storage capacity, as the platform's anchor assets.
Source
Central Tank Terminal | KKR
KKR (Sale) · Tank storage · Japan
KKR agreed to sell Central Tank Terminal to an institutional investor. The Japanese platform operates 12 terminals, more than 450 tanks and over 420,000 KL of storage capacity; financial terms were not disclosed.
Source
Central Terminal Korea | KKR
KKR (Sale) · Tank storage / Grain logistics · South Korea
Under a separate agreement, KKR will sell Central Terminal Korea to an institutional investor. The business operates a tank-terminal complex in Ulsan and grain-silo facilities in Ulsan and Pyeongtaek; financial terms were not disclosed.
Source
Utilities2 Deals
Vicinity Energy | Harrison Street / Antin Infra
Harrison Street (Majority Stake) · District energy · U.S.
Antin Infra's Flagship Fund IV agreed to sell a majority interest in Vicinity Energy to Harrison Street at a US$2.92bn enterprise value for the whole company. Vicinity operates 19 district-energy systems across 12 U.S. cities; the valuation is distinct from the undisclosed majority-stake purchase price.
Source
Wärme Moselbogen | Asterion
Asterion (Joint Venture) · District heating · Germany
Asterion-backed Iqony Energies signed a 50:50 district-heating joint venture with evm in Koblenz. Wärme Moselbogen combines existing networks and plans to connect around 500 additional buildings by 2034, with operations starting October 1.
Source
