Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • August 22 – August 28, 2026
KEY THEMES
KKR’s SK Horizon investment and KKR / Apollo’s C$2.7bn Westcoast Pipeline joint venture led disclosed scale, alongside ArcLight’s US$1.0bn Anchor Point commitment.
U.S. deployment included ArcLight’s Anchor Point transmission platform, Harbert’s Mid-Georgia Cogen acquisition, Apollo / KKR’s Atlantic Aviation recapitalization and Antin-backed Sapphire’s EDGE LNG bolt-on.
Power & ET3 Deals
Mid-Georgia Cogen | Harbert
Harbert (Buyout) · Cogeneration · Georgia, United States
Harbert Power acquired Mid-Georgia Cogen, an operating 317 MW cogeneration plant backed by a long-term power purchase agreement. The acquisition adds a contracted generation asset to Harbert’s critical energy-infrastructure strategy.
Source
ECO POWER ONE / Bollingstedt BESS | Energy Infrastructure Partners
Energy Infrastructure Partners / Alpiq (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Battery Storage · Germany
EIP-backed Alpiq acquired ECO POWER ONE GmbH from ECO STOR, including the operating 103.5 MW / 238 MWh Bollingstedt battery facility. The asset expands Alpiq’s storage and flexibility portfolio in Germany.
Source
Olpe-Rehringhausen Wind Project | Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners
Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners / Perigus Energy (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Onshore Wind · Germany
CI V-backed Perigus Energy agreed to acquire the rights to the permitted 21 MW Olpe-Rehringhausen wind project. Perigus will assume responsibility for the project’s further implementation in North Rhine-Westphalia.
Source
Utilities3 Deals
Anchor Point Transmission | ArcLight
ArcLight (Platform Launch) · Power Transmission · United States
ArcLight committed US$1.0bn of fund equity to launch Anchor Point Transmission, a developer, owner and operator of high-voltage transmission infrastructure. The platform targets grid expansion needed to connect new generation and large power users.
Source
PLUS ES | DigitalBridge
DigitalBridge / Ausgrid (Buyout / Portfolio Company Divestiture) · Smart Metering · Australia
A DigitalBridge-managed vehicle agreed to acquire PLUS ES, Ausgrid’s ring-fenced smart-metering platform. The sale provides an exit for Ausgrid owners IFM Investors, APG and AustralianSuper from the metering business.
Source
Genus Power Infrastructures | GIC
GIC (Sale) · Smart Metering / Utilities · India
GIC affiliate Chiswick Investment sold approximately 3.58mm Genus Power shares, equal to a 1.17% stake, in an identifiable block transaction. The sale reduces GIC’s exposure to its Indian smart-metering infrastructure platform.
Source
Digital2 Deals
SK Horizon | KKR
KKR (Minority Stake) · AI Data Centers / Submarine Cables · South Korea / Asia-Pacific
KKR and the IMM consortium agreed to invest a combined KRW3.08tn in SK Horizon, SK Telecom’s data-center and submarine-cable platform. KKR will hold 29% through capital invested primarily from its Asia-Pacific infrastructure strategy.
Source
American Tower Canadian Portfolio | CVC DIF
CVC DIF / Aurora Towers (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Telecom Towers · Canada
DIF Infrastructure VIII-backed Aurora Towers agreed to acquire American Tower’s Canadian wireless-tower business. The bolt-on adds 255 operating sites to Aurora’s national communications-infrastructure platform.
Source
Midstream2 Deals
Westcoast Pipeline System | KKR / Apollo
KKR / Apollo (Joint Venture) · Gas Transmission · British Columbia, Canada
KKR and Apollo agreed to invest approximately C$2.7bn for a cumulative indirect 29% interest in Enbridge’s Westcoast Pipeline System. The joint venture funds direct ownership in essential Canadian gas-transmission infrastructure.
Source
EDGE LNG | Antin Infrastructure Partners
Antin Infrastructure Partners / Sapphire Gas Solutions (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Distributed LNG / CNG · United States
Antin-backed Sapphire Gas Solutions completed the acquisition of EDGE LNG. The bolt-on expands Sapphire’s distributed natural-gas infrastructure platform and its LNG and CNG delivery capabilities.
Source
Transportation1 Deal
Atlantic Aviation | Apollo / KKR
Apollo / KKR (Minority Stake / Partial Sale) · Fixed-Base Aviation · United States
Apollo-managed funds acquired a significant interest in Atlantic Aviation from KKR-managed funds, which will retain a substantial holding. The recapitalization provides new capital for the private aviation-infrastructure platform’s continued growth.
Source
