GUGGENHEIM

Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • October 3 – October 9, 2026
KEY THEMES
PSP's Sunrise exit covered a development pipeline with US$7.5bn of expected costs, while AustralianSuper's Perth Airport stake purchase was reported above A$600mm.
U.S. deployment included GIP / CPP's fiber JV, Harbert's 300MW Papago acquisition, Fengate's Accelergen platform and GIP-backed Clearway's US$119mm Utah storage purchase.
Power & ET7 Deals
Honeycomb 2 Utah storage portfolio | GIP
GIP (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Battery Storage · United States
GIP-backed Clearway Energy agreed to acquire Class A interests in three Utah battery storage projects totaling 210MW for ~US$119mm from an affiliated Clearway seller. The private agreement was signed October 1 and first publicly filed October 7; closing is expected in Q4 2027.
Source
Spanish solar development portfolio | Ardian
Ardian (Portfolio Company Divestiture) · Solar · Spain
Ardian-backed Akuo agreed to sell three early-stage solar projects totaling over 500MW to Dos Grados. Located in Castilla y León and Andalucía, the projects expand the buyer’s Iberian development portfolio; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Papago Storage | Harbert
Harbert (Buyout) · Battery Storage · United States
Harbert Power acquired the 300MW Papago battery storage project in Maricopa County, Arizona. The acquisition expands its contracted power infrastructure footprint alongside the 150MW Justice storage project in the same county; transaction value was undisclosed.
Source
Polish battery storage portfolio | Actis
Actis (Buyout) · Battery Storage · Poland
Actis acquired DRI’s 112MW / 518MWh Kozienice project at ready-to-build stage and signed the purchase of 132MW / 623MWh Trzebinia, with completion expected upon operation. The combined 244MW / 1,141MWh portfolio benefits from 17-year inflation-indexed capacity contracts; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Via Verde | CIP
CIP (Primary Capital Raise) · Biomethane · Italy
CIP’s Advanced Bioenergy Fund II invested in Via Verde, an Italian biomethane platform with 20 development projects across regions including Lombardy, Abruzzo and Campania. At full operation, the portfolio is expected to produce ~1TWh annually; the investment amount and ownership stake were undisclosed.
Source
Italian operating solar portfolio | J.P. Morgan AM
J.P. Morgan AM (Bolt-On) · Solar · Italy
Sonnedix, backed by the Infrastructure Investments Fund advised by J.P. Morgan AM, acquired ~54MWp of operating solar assets from EOS ReNewable Infrastructure Fund II. The two plants comprise 46.5MWp in Sardinia and 7.4MWp in Lazio, with most Sardinian capacity supported by a 20-year incentive tariff; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Accelergen Energy | Fengate
Fengate (Buyout) · Solar & Storage · United States
Fengate Infrastructure Fund V and affiliates acquired Accelergen Energy and seven solar-plus-storage and standalone battery development projects in SPP and WECC markets. The management team will advance the acquired portfolio and pursue further development and acquisitions; project capacity and purchase price were undisclosed.
Source
Digital4 Deals
Forged Fiber 37 / Gigapower JV | GIP / CPP
GIP / CPP (Joint Venture) · Fiber Networks · United States
GIP and CPP agreed with AT&T to combine Forged Fiber 37 and Gigapower into a U.S. wholesale fiber JV spanning nearly 5mm locations and over 1mm AT&T fiber subscribers across 16 states. AT&T will own 50%, with GIP and CPP collectively holding 50%; closing is expected in H1 2027 and investment value was undisclosed.
Source
Zerra DC | Stonepeak
Stonepeak (Primary Capital Raise) · Data Centers · Asia-Pacific
Macquarie Capital invested in Zerra DC, the hyperscale data center platform established by Stonepeak-backed AGP, which remains the majority shareholder. The funding supports a pipeline exceeding 2GW of planned IT capacity across Australia, Japan and India; the investment amount and new stake were undisclosed.
Source
Sabey Data Center Properties | Ares
Ares (Minority Stake) · Data Centers · United States
Ares made an additional equity commitment to Sabey Data Center Properties, expanding its July minority investment in the U.S. platform. Sabey owns and operates 275MW, with an expansion pipeline targeting ~737MW by 2033; Ares’ cumulative commitment exceeds US$500mm, while the new increment was undisclosed.
Source
IBM VMware enterprise customer business | Tiger Infrastructure
Tiger Infrastructure (Bolt-On) · Managed Cloud Infrastructure · United States / Global
Tiger Infrastructure-backed 11:11 Systems acquired selected IBM VMware Cloud Service Provider enterprise customers. Its eighth VMware-related acquisition broadens the platform’s cloud infrastructure and migration services across global markets; transaction terms and the acquired customer count were undisclosed.
Source
Transportation2 Deals
Perth Airport | AustralianSuper
AustralianSuper (Minority Stake) · Airports · Australia
AustralianSuper confirmed an increased investment in Perth Airport, reported as a 9.14% stake purchase for more than A$600mm, taking its ownership to ~29.4%. The airport is planning a new runway and terminal under a major expansion program; the stake and price figures are reported rather than officially quantified.
Source
C3 Limited | InfraRed
InfraRed (Buyout) · Port Logistics · New Zealand / Australia
An InfraRed value-add fund agreed to acquire C3 from Brookfield. The port logistics and materials-handling platform operates 15 strategic port and inland sites across New Zealand and Australia, handles around half of New Zealand’s log exports and about half of containers at Tauranga; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Utilities2 Deals
Xcel Michigan electric utilities | Axium
Axium (Bolt-On) · Regulated Electric Utilities · United States
Axium-owned UPPCO agreed to acquire Xcel Energy’s regulated electric transmission and distribution operations in Michigan. The assets serve ~9,000 customers and include 470 miles of distribution and 110 miles of transmission lines in Gogebic and Ontonagon counties; closing is expected in 2027 subject to regulatory approval.
Source
Leppäkosken Lämpö | CVC DIF
CVC DIF (Bolt-On) · District Heating · Finland
CVC DIF-backed Loimua signed the acquisition of Leppäkosken Lämpö from Leppäkoski Group. The target supplies district heating in six Finnish municipalities and also distributes natural gas, with ~250GWh of heat sales in 2025; consideration was undisclosed and completion is expected by year-end.
Source
Social Infra2 Deals
Sunrise Senior Living | PSP
PSP (Sale) · Senior Living & Care · United States / Canada
PSP agreed to sell Sunrise to BDT & MSD in partnership with management, with closing expected in 2027. The platform operates over 230 communities serving more than 22,000 residents and has over 50 planned communities with ~US$7.5bn of expected development cost; the purchase price was undisclosed.
Source
Degotte | GSAM
GSAM (Bolt-On) · Modular Social Infrastructure · Belgium / Luxembourg
GSAM-backed Adapteo signed the acquisition of Belgian modular-space provider Degotte, with closing expected at the end of October. Degotte has delivered over 6,000 modules across more than 2,000 projects in Belgium and Luxembourg, serving public-sector, defense, education and healthcare customers; consideration was undisclosed.
