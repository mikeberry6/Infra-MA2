Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • September 5 – September 11, 2026
KEY THEMES
Tallgrass's US$2.55bn crude-business sale to Enbridge led disclosed values, followed by Adani Airports' approximately US$1bn equity raise and the DigitalBridge / L&G / TD AM consortium's €669mm Dutch tower acquisition.
U.S. deployment centered on HASI's investment in CDPQ-backed Invenergy's 2.7 GW portfolio and Antin Infra-backed Sapphire's Spectrum LNG bolt-on; Tallgrass's crude-business sale represented a sponsor exit.
Transportation3 Deals
Adani Airport Holdings | BlackRock / Temasek
BlackRock / Temasek Consortium (Primary Capital Raise) · Airports · India
Adani Airport Holdings signed binding agreements to raise approximately US$1bn from BlackRock-managed funds, Temasek, Alpha Wave Global and Premji Invest for a combined approximately 5.54% stake. The eight-airport operator plans to fund airport expansion, airport-city development and ancillary businesses; subscriptions are scheduled in three tranches through July 2027.
Source
VTG Rail UK | USS
USS (Buyout) · Railcar Leasing · United Kingdom
USS agreed to acquire all shares in VTG Rail UK from VTG, whose infrastructure investors include GIP, ADIA Infra and OMERS Infra. The business leases approximately 4,500 freight rail vehicles in the UK; financial terms were not disclosed and completion is expected in Q4 2026.
Source
SNTA / Transports Auran | Cube Infra
Cube Infra / CFTR (PortCo Acquisition) · Bus & Coach Transport · France
Cube Infra-backed CFTR signed an agreement to acquire 100% of SNTA, expanding across southern France. The group operates nearly 450 vehicles under nine brands, providing scheduled, school and tourism services, and generated €46.5mm of revenue in 2025; the purchase price was not disclosed.
Source
Power & ET2 Deals
Invenergy 2.7 GW Renewables Portfolio | CDPQ
CDPQ / Invenergy (Joint Venture) · Solar, Storage & Wind · United States
CDPQ-backed Invenergy and HASI closed an equity partnership covering 10 solar, solar-plus-storage and wind projects totaling 2.7 GW across seven U.S. states. More than 850 MW is operating, with the remaining projects expected online by Q1 2027; Invenergy retains majority ownership and HASI funds its investments as projects reach commercial operation.
Source
Blackhillock Synchronous Condenser | Foresight
Foresight (Buyout) · Grid Stability · United Kingdom
A Foresight-managed fund acquired the operational Blackhillock synchronous condenser in northeast Scotland from GIP-managed funds. Operating since July 2025, the asset provides approximately 2 GVA of short-circuit capacity to support electricity-system stability as renewable generation expands; terms were not disclosed.
Source
Midstream2 Deals
Tallgrass Crude Transportation Business | Blackstone
Blackstone / Tallgrass (PortCo Divestiture) · Crude Pipelines & Storage · United States
Blackstone-backed Tallgrass agreed to sell its crude transportation business to Enbridge for US$2.55bn, including 75% of Pony Express, 51% of Powder River Gateway and approximately 8.4mm barrels of storage. The assets connect Rockies production to Cushing and include the contracted Pony Express expansion; closing is expected later in 2026, subject to approvals.
Source
Spectrum LNG Assets | Antin Infra
Antin Infra / Sapphire Gas Solutions (PortCo Acquisition) · Distributed LNG · United States
Antin Infra-backed Sapphire Gas Solutions acquired Spectrum LNG's assets, including the Desert Gas LNG plant in Ehrenberg, Arizona, and an expansion site in Stroud, Oklahoma. The operating plant produces up to 76,000 gallons per day and provides 100,000 gallons of storage, extending Sapphire's distributed LNG supply footprint; terms were not disclosed.
Source
Social Infra2 Deals
Secure Electronic Registries Victoria | Macquarie AM
Macquarie AM (Joint Venture) · Land Registry Concession · Australia
Macquarie AM's Global Infrastructure Fund agreed to acquire 50% of Secure Electronic Registries Victoria from Aware Super, which retains the remaining half. SERV administers Victoria's land register under a 40-year concession granted in 2018 and processes more than 9mm transactions annually; completion is expected in Q4 2026, subject to approvals.
Source
Westerleigh Group | ICG Infra
ICG Infra (Buyout) · Crematoria · United Kingdom
ICG Infra agreed to acquire Westerleigh Group from Ontario Teachers and USS, alongside the existing management team. The UK's largest private crematorium operator and developer has 42 sites, including 21 opened since 2016; financial terms were not disclosed and completion is expected in Q4 2026.
Source
Digital1 Deal
VodafoneZiggo Towers | DigitalBridge / L&G / TD AM
DigitalBridge / L&G / TD AM (Buyout) · Telecom Towers · Netherlands
Funds managed by DigitalBridge, L&G and TD AM agreed to acquire VodafoneZiggo's Dutch tower assets for €669mm. The consortium plans to combine the assets with Belgium Tower Partners, creating an independent platform with more than 6,600 sites across the Netherlands and Belgium, subject to regulatory approvals and consultation.
