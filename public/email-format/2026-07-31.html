Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • July 25 – July 31, 2026
KEY THEMES
Large-scale transactions set the pace, led by Blackstone / Brookfield / KKR’s US$16bn Kuwait pipeline partnership, BlackRock / GIP’s ~US$14bn Meta El Paso campus and ECP / KKR’s £5.75bn DCC Energy take-private.
U.S. deployment included BlackRock / GIP’s Meta El Paso venture, Ares’ US$800mm California solar-and-storage purchase, GSAM’s RWE distributed-generation acquisition, ArcLight’s 68 MW solar bolt-on and Tallvine’s US$75mm Crosby Marine bid.
Power & ET8 Deals
California Solar & BESS Portfolio | Ares
Ares (Acquisition) · Solar & Battery Storage · United States
Ares agreed to acquire an 80% common-equity interest in a California portfolio totaling 384 MW, comprising 200 MW of solar and 184 MW of battery storage. EDPR reported an approximately US$800mm enterprise value and US$450mm of expected proceeds.
Source
RWE U.S. Distributed Clean Energy | GSAM
GSAM / RWE (Acquisition / Divestiture) · Distributed Solar & Storage · United States
GSAM agreed to acquire RWE’s U.S. distributed clean-energy business, including 348 MWdc of operating assets and a 1.2 GW development pipeline. The transaction transfers an established commercial-and-industrial generation platform to GSAM’s infrastructure strategy.
Source
PCG Global | Temasek
Temasek / GenZero (Growth Investment) · Renewable Energy Platform · Asia
Temasek-owned GenZero led PCG Global’s first external, Pre-Series A capital raise to accelerate renewable-project origination across Southeast Asia, Oceania and the Middle East. The platform has approximately 1.8 GW of projects in development.
Source
U.S. Distributed Solar Portfolio | ArcLight
ArcLight / REC Power (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Distributed Solar · United States
ArcLight-backed REC Power completed the acquisition of 15 operating distributed-generation solar projects totaling 68 MW from Adapture Renewables. The portfolio broadens REC Power’s contracted operating base across U.S. power markets.
Source
Green Squares / Vertis Rooftop Solar | Igneo
Igneo / Vertis (Platform Launch / Portfolio Acquisition) · Rooftop Solar · Australia
Igneo launched Vertis Energy with the acquisition of Green Squares Energy and two follow-on portfolios totaling 46 MW of rooftop solar. The assets establish an operating distributed-energy platform serving Australian commercial and industrial customers.
Source
Fukushima Wind Project | Stonepeak
Stonepeak / TerraWind (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Onshore Wind · Japan
Stonepeak-backed TerraWind acquired an approximately 25 MW onshore-wind project in Fukushima Prefecture. The purchase expands TerraWind’s Japanese development pipeline to 376 MW.
Source
Múlavirkjun | Ancala
Ancala / HS Orka (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Hydropower · Iceland
Ancala-backed HS Orka acquired the 3.2 MW Múlavirkjun hydropower plant in Iceland. The bolt-on adds an operating renewable asset to HS Orka’s domestic generation portfolio.
Source
Wayu Energy | IFM
IFM / Nala / Kaffee Energy (Portfolio Company Divestiture) · Renewable Power Platform · Southeast Asia
IFM Net Zero Infrastructure Fund-backed Nala Renewables sold its 80% interest in Wayu Energy to Kaffee Energy Limited. The divestiture advances Nala’s geographic refocus while transferring the Vietnam- and Philippines-focused renewable-power platform to a new owner.
Source
Digital4 Deals
Meta El Paso Data Center Campus | BlackRock / GIP
BlackRock / GIP / Meta (Joint Venture) · Data Centers · United States
Meta formed a strategic venture with a BlackRock-led investor group to develop a 1 GW data-center campus in El Paso, Texas. The investor group will own 80% of the approximately US$14bn project, providing direct capital for hyperscale digital infrastructure.
Source
Westconnect | Igneo
Igneo (Equity Investment) · Fiber Networks · Germany
Igneo contributed new shareholder equity to Westconnect alongside a €1.2bn financing package supporting the company’s fiber rollout. Westconnect has passed more than 1mm households; the equity component was not separately disclosed.
Source
Yondr Slough Campus | La Caisse / DigitalBridge
La Caisse / DigitalBridge / GLIL (Sale / Joint Venture) · Data Centers · United Kingdom
La Caisse- and DigitalBridge-owned Yondr brought GLIL Infrastructure into its Slough data-center campus through an investment in two operational buildings. The buildings represent more than 60 MW within the campus’ greater-than-100 MW footprint.
Source
Greenergy Data Centers | Amber
Amber / 3SIIF (Minority Stake) · Data Centers · Estonia
Amber-managed 3SIIF admitted Tensor Ventures as a minority investor in MCF Group, the owner of Greenergy Data Centers, while retaining majority control. The investment adds growth capital to the operator of the Baltics’ largest data center.
Source
Transportation4 Deals
Cube Highways Trust | ADIA / BCI / I Squared
ADIA / BCI / I Squared (IPO) · Toll Roads · India
ADIA-, BCI- and I Squared-backed Cube Highways Trust began public trading following a ₹5,000 crore issue. The infrastructure investment trust owns 27 operational road assets and debuted with a reported market capitalization of ₹21,045 crore.
Source
Crosby Marine Transportation | Tallvine
Tallvine (Stalking-Horse Acquisition) · Marine Transportation & Dredging · United States
Tallvine affiliate Breakwater Buyer LLC signed a US$75mm stalking-horse agreement for Crosby Marine Transportation’s marine-transportation and dredging business. The proposed purchase remains subject to the bankruptcy auction process and court approval.
Source
Contracted Container Portfolio | Brookfield
Brookfield (Portfolio Company Divestiture) · Intermodal Containers · North America / Global
Brookfield Infrastructure disclosed the completed sale of a 67% interest across contracted intermodal-container programs representing more than one-quarter of the related fleet. The transaction generated US$60mm of proceeds at Brookfield Infrastructure’s share.
Source
Sølvtrans | Antin
Antin / GLIL (Minority Stake Sale) · Wellboat Transportation · Norway
Antin agreed to sell a 30% interest in Sølvtrans to GLIL Infrastructure while retaining majority control. The aquaculture wellboat operator has expanded from 21 vessels at Antin’s investment to a 48-vessel fleet.
Source
Midstream3 Deals
Kuwait Oil Pipeline Network | Blackstone / Brookfield / KKR
Blackstone / Brookfield / KKR (Joint Venture / Leaseback) · Crude-Oil Pipelines · Kuwait
A Blackstone-, Brookfield- and KKR-led consortium agreed a US$16bn partnership over Kuwait Oil Company’s 13-pipeline, 320-kilometer crude-oil network. The investors will own 49% of a newly formed infrastructure company, generating US$7.85bn of proceeds for KOC while operations remain with the state producer.
Source
SeaSeaS Amsterdam CO₂ Terminal | IMCO
IMCO / Exolum (Portfolio Company Minority Stake) · CO₂ Transportation & Storage · Netherlands
IMCO-backed Exolum acquired a minority interest in the SeaSeaS Amsterdam CO₂ terminal project. The planned terminal is designed to handle 3.5mm tonnes of CO₂ annually and is expected to begin operations in 2030.
Source
Epic Energy | MSIP / QIC
MSIP / QIC (Buyout / Sale) · Gas Pipelines · Australia
MSIP agreed to acquire Epic Energy from QIC Infrastructure. The transaction transfers an established Australian gas-pipeline and energy-infrastructure operator with a 50-year operating history; financial terms were not disclosed.
Source
Utilities2 Deals
DCC Energy | ECP / KKR
ECP / KKR (Buyout) · Energy Distribution · United Kingdom
ECP and KKR announced a recommended acquisition of DCC Energy valuing its equity at £5.75bn. The take-private would transfer a diversified energy-distribution platform serving customers across multiple European markets to infrastructure ownership.
Source
Tarchon Interconnector | CPP Investments / CIP
CPP Investments / CIP / WindGrid (Majority Stake) · Power Interconnector · United Kingdom / Germany
CPP Investments agreed to commit approximately C$1bn for a majority interest in Tarchon, a proposed 1.4 GW electricity interconnector between the United Kingdom and Germany. WindGrid will hold a 25% look-through minority interest, and Copenhagen Infrastructure V, managed by CIP, is the seller.
