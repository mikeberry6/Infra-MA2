KEY THEMES

Power & ET2 Deals
Mid-Georgia Cogen | Harbert
Harbert (Buyout) · Cogeneration Power Plant · Georgia, United States
Harbert Power Fund VII acquired the 317 MW Mid-Georgia Cogen dual-fuel combined-cycle facility in Kathleen. The plant operates under a power purchase agreement with Georgia Power, with a new 20-year agreement scheduled to begin in 2028.
Source
Olpe-Rehringhausen Wind Project | CIP
CIP / Perigus Energy (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Onshore Wind Development · Germany
Copenhagen Infrastructure V-backed Perigus Energy acquired the rights to the fully permitted Olpe-Rehringhausen onshore wind project in North Rhine-Westphalia. The three-turbine project has 21 MW of planned capacity and is targeted to enter operation in 2028.
Source
Digital2 Deals
SK Horizon | KKR
KKR (Minority Stake) · AI Data Centers & Subsea Cables · South Korea
Funds managed by KKR and an IMM Investment-Stonebridge consortium entered a definitive agreement for a combined KRW3.08tn equity investment in SK Horizon, a new SK Telecom digital-infrastructure platform. KKR will own 29%, and the platform will encompass eight operating data centers, projects under construction and subsea-cable assets totaling 318 MW.
Source
American Tower Canada Portfolio | CVC DIF
CVC DIF / Aurora Towers (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Wireless Towers · Canada
DIF Infra VIII-backed Aurora Towers agreed to acquire American Tower’s Canadian portfolio of approximately 255 communications sites. The bolt-on will expand Aurora’s portfolio to more than 650 towers across Canada.
Source
Utilities2 Deals
Anchor Point Transmission | ArcLight
ArcLight (Platform Launch) · High-Voltage Transmission · United States
ArcLight committed US$1bn of equity to launch Anchor Point Transmission, an independent platform formed to develop, finance, own and operate high-voltage transmission infrastructure. The company will partner with utilities to address grid investment required by electrification, manufacturing and AI load growth.
Source
PLUS ES | DigitalBridge / AustralianSuper
DigitalBridge / AustralianSuper / Ausgrid (Sale / Acquisition) · Smart Metering · Australia
AustralianSuper-backed Ausgrid entered an agreement to sell PLUS ES’s unregulated digital-metering business to DigitalBridge. PLUS ES has 2mm installed electricity and water meters and a further installation pipeline; its contestable-infrastructure business is excluded from the sale.
Source
Transportation1 Deal
Atlantic Aviation | Apollo / KKR
Apollo / KKR (Minority Stake) · Fixed-Base Aviation Services · United States
Apollo-managed infrastructure funds agreed to acquire a significant interest in Atlantic Aviation from KKR, which will remain a substantial investor alongside management. The transaction values the 100-plus-location fixed-base-operator platform at nearly US$10bn.
Source
Midstream1 Deal
EDGE LNG | Antin
Antin / Sapphire Gas Solutions (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Mobile LNG Infrastructure · United States
Antin-backed Sapphire Gas Solutions acquired EDGE LNG from Blue Water Energy, adding modular liquefaction, delivery and vaporization capabilities. EDGE contributes approximately 200,000 gallons of daily LNG liquefaction capacity serving customers across the southern United States.
Source
