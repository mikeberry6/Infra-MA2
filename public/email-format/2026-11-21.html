Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • November 14 – 20, 2026
KEY THEMES
Blackstone’s AWS data center transaction led disclosed scale at US$5bn of gross asset value, while Allianz GI and Schroders Greencoat joined the equity investors in Finland’s €300mm-plus Tampere e-methane project.
U.S. deployment included EQT Infra’s agreement to buy Flatiron Energy, East West Bank’s US$55.1mm tax-equity investment in Generate-backed esVolta’s City Breeze and Boxcar projects, and Kansai Electric’s minority investment in ArcLight’s operating Prospect Power Storage facility.
Power & ET6 Deals
Tampere E-Methane Project | Allianz GI / Schroders Greencoat
Allianz GI / Schroders Greencoat (Project Equity Investment) · Renewable Fuels · Finland
Allianz GI and Schroders Greencoat joined Tesi and Japan Hydrogen Fund as equity investors in Nordic Ren-Gas’s Tampere facility, which reached a final investment decision on more than €300mm of total project investment. The plant is designed to produce about 190 GWh of renewable e-methane annually.
Source
City Breeze and Boxcar Battery Storage | Generate
Generate (Project Tax Equity) · Battery Storage · U.S.
Generate-backed esVolta closed US$55.1mm of tax equity from East West Bank to support the City Breeze and Boxcar battery projects in Texas. The projects will add a combined 290 MW / 580 MWh of storage to ERCOT.
Source
Flatiron Energy | EQT Infra
EQT Infra (Buyout) · Battery Storage · U.S.
EQT Infra, through its Transition Infrastructure strategy, agreed to buy Flatiron Energy from Hull Street Energy. The Eastern U.S. battery storage developer has a 24 GWh portfolio, with 6 GWh awarded long-term contracts and 2 GWh under construction.
Source
Windyhill and Hunterston Battery Storage | Carlyle Infrastructure
Carlyle Infrastructure (Joint Venture) · Battery Storage · U.K.
Carlyle Infrastructure-backed Revera Energy formed a joint venture with Macquarie Capital, which will invest in the Windyhill and Hunterston battery projects now under construction in Scotland. The two projects total 600 MW / 1.2 GWh, and Revera will retain majority ownership.
Source
Prospect Power Storage | ArcLight
ArcLight (Minority Stake Sale) · Battery Storage · U.S.
Kansai Electric agreed to acquire a minority stake from an ArcLight fund in the 150 MW / 600 MWh Prospect Power battery facility in Virginia. Elevate Infrastructure developed and operates the project, which is already in commercial service in PJM.
Source
Ingka German Wind Portfolio | Partners Group
Partners Group (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Onshore Wind · Germany
Partners Group-backed Exus acquired four operating German onshore wind farms totaling 45 MW from Ingka Investments. Exus previously managed the 23-turbine portfolio and plans to pursue repowering permits.
Source
Digital4 Deals
AWS Data Center Campuses | Blackstone
Blackstone (Majority Stake) · Hyperscale Data Centers · U.S.
Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust agreed to acquire 75.1% interests in two joint ventures that will own 13 data centers in Indiana and Mississippi with 647 MW of IT capacity and US$5bn of aggregate gross asset value. The trust expects to contribute US$1.9bn of cash equity, and AWS will retain 24.9% and lease back the campuses.
Source
Data4 Greece | HIIF / Brookfield AM
HIIF / Brookfield AM (Minority Stake) · Data Centers · Greece
The Hellenic Innovation & Infrastructure Fund (HIIF) acquired a 23% stake in Brookfield AM-backed Data4’s Greek business to support its first data center in Paiania. The wider Athens campus is planned for up to 90 MW of IT capacity and more than €300mm of investment.
Source
One NZ Employee Shares | Infratil
Infratil (Secondary Share Purchase) · Telecommunications · New Zealand
Infratil disclosed NZ$2.4mm in purchases of employee-held shares in One NZ through liquidity events during the six months ended September 30. One NZ operates mobile, broadband and enterprise networks in New Zealand.
Source
AP WIP Tower Brasil | CPP / GIC
CPP / GIC (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Wireless Infrastructure · Brazil
CPP- and GIC-backed V.tal acquired 100% of AP WIP Tower Brasil, a Radius Global Infrastructure business. The acquisition adds 64 street-level wireless sites across 15 Brazilian cities.
Source
Utilities4 Deals
Acea 5% Stake | Meridiam
Meridiam (Portfolio Company Divestiture) · Water and Power Utilities · Italy
Meridiam-backed SUEZ sold about 5% of Italian utility Acea to institutional investors in an accelerated bookbuild for approximately €208mm in gross proceeds. SUEZ retained about 14.3% of Acea.
Source
Loviisan Lämpö District Heating | Ardian
Ardian (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · District Heating · Finland
Ardian-backed Nevel acquired CapMan Infra’s entire interest in Loviisan Lämpö’s district heating business. The networks sell 65 GWh of heat annually across five Finnish communities.
Source
Heatly | CBRE IM
CBRE IM (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Ground-Source Heating · Finland
CBRE IM-backed Geonova acquired 100% of Heatly in a carve-out from Loviisan Lämpö. Heatly adds 143 property-level geothermal plants, taking Geonova’s portfolio above 500 sites in Finland.
Source
MacKay Heating & Cooling | Basalt Infra
Basalt Infra (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Residential HVAC Services · Canada
Basalt Infra-backed Go Lime acquired MacKay Heating & Cooling, a St. Catharines residential HVAC provider serving Ontario’s Niagara Region. The purchase was Go Lime’s sixth acquisition of 2026 and gives it a local service base in Niagara.
Source
Transportation1 Deal
Zeppelin Rental Traffic Safety Business | I Squared
I Squared (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Traffic and Worksite Safety · Germany / Czechia
I Squared-backed Ramudden signed an agreement to acquire Zeppelin Rental’s construction site and traffic safety business, including METON and Levotec. The subsidiaries make and install temporary safety barriers and mounting systems.
Source
