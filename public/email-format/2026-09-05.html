Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • August 29 – September 4, 2026
KEY THEMES
Apollo’s US$9bn ONEOK investment and ONEOK’s US$4.425bn purchase of EnCap Flatrock-backed Brazos Midland led disclosed activity, alongside Apollo’s ~US$4.1bn Kelvion exit.
U.S. deployment combined Apollo’s minority equity supporting the Brazos acquisition and debt reduction with Tiger Infrastructure’s Safe-Way student-transportation bolt-on; I Squared’s Kinetik disposal was a secondary share sale.
Power & ET 3 Deals
Gawara Baya | CIP
CIP (Buyout) · Wind & Battery Storage · Australia
CIP’s CI V acquired 100% of Gawara Baya from Windlab, comprising 408MW of onshore wind and 104MW of battery storage in North Queensland. The project secured A$1.7bn of financing and targets full operations in 2030; the acquisition price was undisclosed.
Source
Azura Power | Actis
Actis (Sale) · Power Generation · Nigeria / Senegal / Mozambique
ePointZero agreed to acquire the combined 90% interest in Azura held by Actis and Africa50, with Amaya retaining 10%. The platform has 752MW of operating generation across Nigeria, Senegal and Mozambique and a development pipeline exceeding 1.5GW; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Silkstead | Equitix
Equitix (Bolt-On) · Battery Storage · United Kingdom
Equitix-backed Eelpower Energy acquired the 50MW/200MWh Silkstead battery-storage project near Winchester from Balanced Grid Works. Construction is expected to begin in 2027, with operations targeted for 2028; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Midstream 3 Deals
ONEOK Holdings | Apollo
Apollo (Minority Stake) · Midstream Infrastructure · United States
Apollo funds and affiliates agreed to invest US$9bn for a new non-voting minority equity interest in ONEOK Holdings. The investment supports ONEOK’s Brazos Midland acquisition and debt reduction, including plans to retire ~US$5bn of debt.
Source
Brazos Midland | EnCap Flatrock
EnCap Flatrock (Portfolio Company Divestiture) · Gas Gathering & Processing · United States
ONEOK agreed to acquire 100% of Brazos Midland from EnCap Flatrock-backed Brazos Midstream Holdings III for US$4.425bn. The Permian business has ~600,000 dedicated acres and fixed-fee contracts with an average remaining term exceeding 12 years.
Source
Kinetik Holdings Shares | I Squared
I Squared (Sale) · Midstream Infrastructure · United States
I Squared’s Buzzard Midstream vehicle sold 45,428 Kinetik Class A shares for ~US$2.5mm on September 1, as disclosed in a September 3 SEC filing. The partial exit left 797,136 shares held by the vehicle and represents a secondary share sale.
Source
Digital 2 Deals
Kelvion | Apollo
Apollo (Sale) · Data Center Cooling · Germany / Global
SLB agreed to acquire 100% of Kelvion from Apollo-managed funds for US$3.4bn in cash plus US$700mm of assumed debt, implying ~US$4.1bn enterprise value. The Germany-based heat-exchange business serves data centers and industrial markets, with closing expected in the first half of 2027.
Source
Tokyo DC4 / DC5 | GIC
GIC (Sale) · Data Centers · Japan
Keppel DC REIT and Keppel agreed to acquire a combined 90% of two fully occupied Inzai data centers at ¥190bn on a 100% basis. GIC reportedly exits its 80% interest while Equinix retains 10%; the two facilities provide 163,294 sq ft of net lettable area.
Source
Transportation 2 Deals
Peregrine–Sinar Primera JV | Stonepeak
Stonepeak (Joint Venture) · Cold Storage · Indonesia
Stonepeak-backed Peregrine Cold Logistics formed a JV with Sinar Primera to acquire an existing Pluit cold-storage facility and develop a second site in Narogong. The two sites are expected to provide ~35,000 sq m of combined capacity; consideration and ownership percentages were undisclosed.
Source
Safe-Way Bus / Safe-Way Wisconsin | Tiger Infrastructure
Tiger Infrastructure (Bolt-On) · Student Transportation · United States
Tiger Infrastructure-backed Reliant Transportation agreed to acquire all equity in Safe-Way Bus and Safe-Way Wisconsin, subject to STB approval. The businesses serve five school districts from four terminals across Minnesota and Wisconsin; the purchase price was undisclosed.
Source
Social Infra 2 Deals
Avisena Healthcare | KKR
KKR (Minority Stake) · Hospitals · Malaysia
KKR agreed to make a minority investment in Avisena Healthcare, which operates two multidisciplinary hospitals with more than 250 licensed beds in Shah Alam. The company plans over 300 additional beds, taking capacity toward ~600 by 2029; the investment amount and stake were undisclosed.
Source
WL Bau | Swiss Life
Swiss Life (Bolt-On) · Modular Buildings · Switzerland
Swiss Life-backed Condecta acquired modular-building specialist WL Bau, with completion on August 31 first disclosed on September 1. WL Bau’s 25-person team will move from Meggen to Condecta’s Wikon facility, expanding its Swiss modular-construction capabilities; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
