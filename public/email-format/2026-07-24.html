Weekly Briefing – Infrastructure Sponsor M&A – 11 deals across Power & ET, Utilities, Transportation, and Social Infra – July 18–24, 2026
GUGGENHEIM
INFRASTRUCTURE COVERAGE & ADVISORY
Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • July 18 – July 24, 2026
KEY THEMES
Power & ET accounted for 7 of 11 deals, led by Brookfield’s ~US$7bn Aypa Power acquisition and Cubico–CFE’s nearly US$1bn investment framework, backed by Ontario Teachers and PSP. Caruna’s €5bn valuation led utilities, while Mirova-backed Yanara and La Caisse / GO.FARM added material platform capital.
U.S. deployment centered on Brookfield’s Aypa Power acquisition, Ridgewood-backed MN8 / Greenbacker and TPG-backed Altus Power’s 32 MW Virginia community-solar portfolio acquisition.
Power & ET7 Deals
Aypa Power | Brookfield / Blackstone
Brookfield / Blackstone (Buyout / Sale) · Battery Storage · North America
Brookfield agreed to acquire Aypa Power from Blackstone for approximately US$7 billion of enterprise value, including US$3 billion of equity value. Aypa has approximately 6.5 GW of operating, under-construction and contracted battery-storage capacity and a development pipeline exceeding 20 GW.
Source
Cubico–CFE Mexico JV | Ontario Teachers / PSP
Ontario Teachers / PSP / Cubico (Portfolio Company Joint Venture) · Renewable Power & Battery Storage · Mexico
Ontario Teachers- and PSP-owned Cubico signed a 25-year joint venture agreement with CFE covering five renewable projects totaling 578 MWac plus 175.7 MW / 500 MWh of battery storage. The partnership is expected to unlock nearly US$1 billion of investment across Tamaulipas, Nuevo León, Campeche and the Yucatán Peninsula.
Source
Greenbacker Renewable Energy Company | Ridgewood
Ridgewood / MN8 Energy (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Renewable Power · United States
Ridgewood-backed MN8 Energy signed a definitive cash-and-equity agreement to acquire Greenbacker for up to approximately US$375 million. The combination would create a clean-power platform with more than 6 GW of operating and under-construction capacity across 33 states.
Source
Yanara | Mirova
Mirova (Growth Investment) · Renewable Power & Battery Storage · Australia
Mirova committed €150 million to Yanara to accelerate more than 2 GW of utility-scale renewable infrastructure across Victoria, New South Wales and Western Australia. The portfolio includes the 450 MW solar and 600 MW / 2,400 MWh Mortlake Energy Hub.
Source
Burton Wold & Winscales Moor Wind Farms | Tiger Infrastructure
Tiger Infrastructure / Forsa Energy (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Onshore Wind · United Kingdom
Tiger Infrastructure-backed Forsa Energy acquired the operational Burton Wold and Winscales Moor wind farms from GCP Infra for approximately £11 million. The 17-turbine portfolio totals approximately 26.5 MW and expands Forsa’s UK independent-power-producer platform to 324.5 MW.
Source
Vena Energy KN Wind Power | GIP
GIP / Vena Energy / Orient Cement (Portfolio Company Minority Stake Acquisition) · Onshore Wind · India
Orient Cement approved the acquisition of a 9.04% interest in both the ordinary and cumulative convertible preference share capital of GIP-backed Vena Energy KN Wind Power for ₹1.234 million. The target owns a 46 MW wind project in Karnataka that will supply contracted renewable power for captive use.
Source
Five Virginia Community Solar Projects | ECP / TPG
ECP / TPG / New Leaf Energy / Altus Power (Portfolio Company Acquisition / Divestiture) · Community Solar · United States
TPG-backed Altus Power acquired five under-development Virginia community-solar projects totaling 32 MW from ECP-backed New Leaf Energy. The projects will serve Appalachian Power’s shared-solar program and are expected to provide power equivalent to the needs of approximately 5,000 homes.
Source
Utilities2 Deals
Caruna | KKR / Ontario Teachers
KKR / Ontario Teachers / Iberdrola (Sale) · Electricity Distribution · Finland
Iberdrola agreed to acquire KKR’s and Ontario Teachers’ respective 40% holdings in Caruna for approximately €2 billion, valuing the Finnish electricity distributor at about €5 billion including debt. Caruna operates roughly 89,000 km of network and serves 1.5 million people.
Source
Uluğ Enerji | Actis
Actis / IFC / Proparco (Minority Investment) · Electricity Distribution · Türkiye
IFC and Proparco agreed to become minority equity investors in Actis-controlled Uluğ Enerji, with Actis retaining a majority stake. The electricity distribution and retail group serves more than three million customers across four provinces under an exclusive concession through 2036.
Source
Transportation1 Deal
OIL! Tankstellen | KKR
KKR / Enilive (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Fuel Distribution & Mobility Retail · Germany / Denmark / Austria / Switzerland
KKR-backed Enilive signed a binding agreement to acquire 100% of OIL! Tankstellen from Prax. The network comprises approximately 320 service stations across four European markets and expands Enilive’s retail platform for conventional and lower-emission fuels, electric charging and related mobility services.
Source
Social Infra1 Deal
GO.FARM Permanent Crops Platform | La Caisse
La Caisse / GO.FARM (Platform Launch / Minority Stake) · Agricultural Infrastructure · Australia
La Caisse committed A$300 million and GO.FARM A$30 million to launch an Australian irrigated permanent-crops platform, while La Caisse will also acquire a minority stake in GO.FARM. The manager oversees approximately A$1.6 billion of assets focused on institutional-scale land and water operations.
Source
