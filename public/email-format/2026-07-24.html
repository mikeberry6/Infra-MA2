Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • July 18 – July 24, 2026
KEY THEMES
Power & ET led with 7 of 14 deals, anchored by Brookfield’s approximately US$7 billion Aypa Power acquisition and the Cubico–CFE joint venture’s nearly US$1 billion investment framework. Other disclosed deployment included MN8’s up-to-US$375 million Greenbacker combination, Mirova’s €150 million Yanara commitment and La Caisse / GO.FARM’s A$330 million platform, while Caruna’s €5 billion valuation marked the largest utility ownership transfer.
U.S. deployment spanned platforms and development assets: Aypa Power would add approximately 6.5 GW of operating, under-construction and contracted battery storage; Ridgewood-backed MN8 / Greenbacker would create a clean-power operator with more than 6 GW across 33 states; and ECP-backed New Leaf Energy’s sale of five Virginia community-solar projects to TPG-backed Altus Power added a 32 MW development portfolio. Outside the U.S., Gabia, OIL! Tankstellen, Chargepoly and Uluğ Enerji broadened deployment across digital, mobility and regulated utility infrastructure, while Atlantic Towing added a long-term-contracted Canadian vessel fleet.
Power & ET7 Deals
Aypa Power | Brookfield / Blackstone
Brookfield / Blackstone (Buyout / Sale) · Battery Storage · North America
Brookfield agreed to acquire Aypa Power from Blackstone Energy Transition Partners for approximately US$7 billion of enterprise value, including US$3 billion of equity value. Aypa has approximately 6.5 GW of operating, under-construction and contracted battery-storage capacity and a development pipeline exceeding 20 GW.
Source
Cubico–CFE Mexico JV | Ontario Teachers / PSP Investments
Ontario Teachers / PSP Investments / Cubico (Portfolio Company Joint Venture) · Renewable Power & Battery Storage · Mexico
Ontario Teachers- and PSP-owned Cubico signed a 25-year joint venture agreement with CFE covering five renewable projects totaling 578 MWac plus 175.7 MW / 500 MWh of battery storage. The partnership is expected to unlock nearly US$1 billion of investment across Tamaulipas, Nuevo León, Campeche and the Yucatán Peninsula.
Source
Greenbacker Renewable Energy Company | Ridgewood Infrastructure
Ridgewood Infrastructure / MN8 Energy (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Renewable Power · United States
Ridgewood Infrastructure-backed MN8 Energy signed a definitive cash-and-equity agreement to acquire Greenbacker for up to approximately US$375 million. The combination would create a clean-power platform with more than 6 GW of operating and under-construction capacity across 33 states.
Source
Yanara | Mirova
Mirova (Growth Investment) · Renewable Power & Battery Storage · Australia
Mirova committed €150 million to Yanara to accelerate more than 2 GW of utility-scale renewable infrastructure across Victoria, New South Wales and Western Australia. The portfolio includes the 450 MW solar and 600 MW / 2,400 MWh Mortlake Energy Hub.
Source
Burton Wold & Winscales Moor Wind Farms | Tiger Infrastructure Partners
Tiger Infrastructure Partners / Forsa Energy (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Onshore Wind · United Kingdom
Tiger Infrastructure Partners-backed Forsa Energy acquired the operational Burton Wold and Winscales Moor wind farms from GCP Infrastructure Investments for approximately £11 million. The 17-turbine portfolio totals approximately 26.5 MW and expands Forsa’s UK independent-power-producer platform to 324.5 MW.
Source
Vena Energy KN Wind Power | Global Infrastructure Partners
GIP / Vena Energy / Orient Cement (Portfolio Company Minority Stake Acquisition) · Onshore Wind · India
Orient Cement approved the acquisition of a 9.04% interest in both the ordinary and cumulative convertible preference share capital of GIP-backed Vena Energy KN Wind Power for ₹1.234 million. The target owns a 46 MW wind project in Karnataka that will supply contracted renewable power for captive use.
Source
Five Virginia Community Solar Projects | Energy Capital Partners / TPG
Energy Capital Partners / TPG / New Leaf Energy / Altus Power (Portfolio Company Acquisition / Divestiture) · Community Solar · United States
TPG-backed Altus Power acquired five under-development Virginia community-solar projects totaling 32 MW from Energy Capital Partners-backed New Leaf Energy. The projects will serve Appalachian Power’s shared-solar program and are expected to provide power equivalent to the needs of approximately 5,000 homes.
Source
Transportation3 Deals
Chargepoly | Meridiam
Meridiam (Growth Investment) · Heavy-Duty EV Charging · France
Meridiam Green Impact Growth Fund led a €23 million investment in Chargepoly alongside existing shareholder Fideve Groupe. The company operates hundreds of DC fast-charging points for truck and bus fleets across France, the United Kingdom and Canada, supporting more than one million freight kilometres each month.
Source
OIL! Tankstellen | KKR
KKR / Enilive (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Fuel Distribution & Mobility Retail · Germany / Denmark / Austria / Switzerland
KKR-backed Enilive signed a binding agreement to acquire 100% of OIL! Tankstellen from Prax. The network comprises approximately 320 service stations across four European markets and expands Enilive’s retail platform for conventional and lower-emission fuels, electric charging and related mobility services.
Source
Atlantic Towing Offshore Division | J.P. Morgan Asset Management
J.P. Morgan Asset Management (Buyout) · Offshore Support Vessels · Canada
Institutional investors advised by J.P. Morgan Asset Management’s Global Transportation Group acquired Atlantic Towing’s offshore division from J.D. Irving and renamed it Sterling Atlantic Offshore. The fleet comprises four platform supply vessels and one anchor-handling tug supply vessel, all under long-term contracts.
Source
Utilities2 Deals
Caruna | KKR / Ontario Teachers
KKR / Ontario Teachers / Iberdrola (Sale) · Electricity Distribution · Finland
Iberdrola agreed to acquire KKR’s and Ontario Teachers’ respective 40% holdings in Caruna for approximately €2 billion, valuing the Finnish electricity distributor at about €5 billion including debt. Caruna operates roughly 89,000 km of network and serves 1.5 million people.
Source
Uluğ Enerji | Actis
Actis / IFC / Proparco (Minority Investment) · Electricity Distribution · Türkiye
IFC and Proparco agreed to become minority equity investors in Actis-controlled Uluğ Enerji, with Actis retaining a majority stake. The electricity distribution and retail group serves more than three million customers across four provinces under an exclusive concession through 2036.
Source
Digital1 Deal
Gabia | Macquarie Asset Management
Macquarie Asset Management / MKOF6 / DCK Investment (Buyout / Take-Private) · Data Center, Cloud & Internet Exchange · South Korea
Macquarie Asset Management’s DCK Investment vehicle, backed by Macquarie Korea Opportunities Private Equity Fund No. 6, disclosed a share purchase agreement and tender offer to acquire control of Gabia. Gabia and its KINX subsidiary operate cloud, internet data center and neutral internet-exchange infrastructure for business customers.
Source
Social Infra1 Deal
GO.FARM Permanent Crops Platform | La Caisse
La Caisse / GO.FARM (Platform Launch / Minority Stake) · Agricultural Infrastructure · Australia
La Caisse committed A$300 million and GO.FARM A$30 million to launch an Australian irrigated permanent-crops platform, while La Caisse will also acquire a minority stake in GO.FARM. The manager oversees approximately A$1.6 billion of assets focused on institutional-scale land and water operations.
