Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • September 12 – September 18, 2026
KEY THEMES
Hy24, the Ardian/FiveT Hydrogen joint venture, led a quiet week with its entry into the >€1bn Onuba hydrogen project and a 20% stake in Germany’s 470 km OPAL pipeline.
U.S. activity was limited to Harbert’s sale of a 45% stake in the 615 MW Astoria Energy II plant, an operating-asset exit rather than new fund deployment.
Power & ET2 Deals
Onuba Green Hydrogen Project | Hy24
Hy24 (Minority Stake) · Green Hydrogen · Spain
Hy24, a 50:50 joint venture between Ardian and FiveT Hydrogen, and COFIDES agreed to acquire a combined 29% stake in Moeve’s Onuba project in Huelva, with Hy24 investing through its Clean Hydrogen Infrastructure Fund. The 300 MW first phase involves more than €1bn of total project investment, with a further 105 MW expansion option; that figure is project cost, not the price of the minority stake.

Astoria Energy II | Harbert
Harbert (Sale) · Gas-Fired Power · U.S.
Harbert-managed Gulf Pacific Power agreed to sell its 45.0549% indirect interest in Astoria Energy II to EGCO New York, with terms undisclosed. The 615 MW combined-cycle gas plant in Queens supplies energy, capacity and ancillary services under a tolling agreement with the New York Power Authority; the sale remains subject to closing conditions.
Source
Digital2 Deals
Risti Kampus | Mirova
Mirova (Joint Venture) · Data Centers · Estonia
Mirova-backed Sunly agreed to sell 50% of the Risti Kampus project to Magnora Data Center, forming a 50:50 development joint venture. The campus targets 180 MW in its first phase and up to 400 MW following substation upgrades, with access to Sunly’s adjacent renewable-energy park; transaction value was undisclosed.
Source
Epic Communications | GSAM
GSAM (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Telecom Networks · Malta
GSAM-backed Melita agreed to acquire 100% of Epic Communications from Monaco Telecom, combining two of Malta’s leading fixed and mobile network operators. The transaction, whose value was undisclosed, aims to support further network investment and remains subject to merger-control and other regulatory approvals.
Source
Midstream1 Deal
OPAL Pipeline | Hy24
Hy24 (Minority Stake) · Hydrogen Transmission · Germany
Hy24, a 50:50 joint venture between Ardian and FiveT Hydrogen, agreed through its Clean Hydrogen Infrastructure Fund to acquire Uniper’s 20% interest in OPAL through the purchase of Lubmin-Brandov Assets. The 470 km pipeline corridor is being repurposed for Germany’s hydrogen core network, connecting the Baltic coast with the Czech border; financial terms were undisclosed.
Source
Social Infra1 Deal
BauWatch | CVC DIF
CVC DIF (Majority Stake) · Security Infrastructure · Europe
CVC DIF’s Value-Add IV fund and Carlyle Europe Partners agreed to acquire BauWatch from Haniel, which will retain 10%. The Netherlands-based security platform operates more than 15,000 surveillance systems across 12 European markets, serving construction sites and critical infrastructure; closing is expected in Q1 2027, subject to regulatory approvals.
