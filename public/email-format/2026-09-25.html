Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • September 19 – September 25, 2026
KEY THEMES
Mirova’s €65mm Nordic battery investment led disclosed new commitments, alongside Patria’s sale of its stake in 360 MWp Puerta de Oro and Stonepeak’s investment in AMPYR Distributed Energy’s 250 MW contracted portfolio.
U.S. deployment centered on GIP-backed Vanguard Renewables’ acquisition of Generate Upcycle’s five facilities in upstate New York and Ontario, adding 1.1bn cubic feet of annual renewable natural gas production to an established operating platform.
Power & ET4 Deals
Nordic Storage Platform | Mirova
Mirova (Equity Investment) · Battery Storage · Finland / Sweden
Mirova committed €65mm of equity to two Ingrid Capacity battery projects totaling 145 MW / 290 MWh. The 45 MW Finnish project is ready to build; the 100 MW Swedish project is expected to reach that stage in 2027, with Ingrid developing, operating and optimizing both systems.
Source
Puerta de Oro | Patria
Patria (Sale) · Solar · Colombia
Patria agreed to sell its entire stake in Puerta de Oro to ISAGEN through Infrastructure Fund V. The operating 360 MWp solar project in Cundinamarca is expected to generate 728 GWh annually; the sale remains subject to regulatory approvals and financial terms were not disclosed.
Source
AMPYR Distributed Energy | Stonepeak
Stonepeak (Growth Investment) · Distributed Renewables · United Kingdom / Europe
Stonepeak agreed an equity investment in AMPYR Distributed Energy, with AGP retaining an interest. The platform has contracted 250 MW across 200 commercial and industrial sites and has a pipeline exceeding 1 GW, providing a base for further onsite renewable-energy deployment across the UK and Europe.
Source
Enerfarm | Macquarie
Macquarie (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Bioenergy · Italy
Macquarie-backed VORN Bioenergy acquired Enerfarm from Equitix, adding five biogas and biomethane plants and a composting facility in northern Italy. The portfolio is expected to produce more than 215 GWh annually once fully operational, with conversion permits secured for four plants; financial terms were not disclosed.
Source
Social Infra3 Deals
Generate Upcycle UK | Asterion
Asterion (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Bioenergy · United Kingdom
Asterion-backed ABIO, through Pinta Energy, acquired Generate Upcycle’s seven UK anaerobic-digestion facilities from Generate Capital. The transaction transfers the UK operating portfolio to a European biogas platform; consideration was not disclosed.
Source
Generate Upcycle North America | GIP
GIP (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Renewable Natural Gas · United States / Canada
GIP-backed Vanguard Renewables agreed to acquire Generate Upcycle’s five operating facilities in upstate New York and Ontario from Generate Capital. The portfolio adds 565,000 tons of annual food-waste processing capacity and 1.1bn cubic feet of annual renewable natural gas production, expanding Vanguard’s footprint to 19 sites; closing is expected October 1.
Source
Burgess Diagnostics | CVC DIF
CVC DIF (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Diagnostic Imaging · United Kingdom / Ireland
CVC DIF-backed Medneo UK acquired Burgess Diagnostics, extending its imaging platform into veterinary services across the UK and Ireland. Burgess brings more than 20 years of specialist experience, adding a new customer segment to Medneo’s existing diagnostic-imaging operations; consideration was not disclosed.
