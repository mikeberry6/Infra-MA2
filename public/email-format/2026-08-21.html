Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • August 15 – August 21, 2026
KEY THEMES
GIP’s US$940mm Lackawanna divestiture led disclosed activity, alongside Macquarie AM’s Fiberhost / INEA sale and the >£58mm UK schools portfolio transaction.
U.S. deployment centered on Ardian’s 460 MW Horse Creek / Electra consolidation, EIG’s Power Planet investment, Pantheon-backed InfraMed, and Igneo / Bernhard Capital PortCo acquisitions.
Power & ET3 Deals
Lackawanna Energy Center | GIP
BlackRock / GIP (Sale) · Gas-Fired Power Generation · Pennsylvania, United States
Funds managed by GIP, a part of BlackRock, signed a definitive agreement to sell 87.71% of Lackawanna’s Class A shares to Equinor for US$940mm. The 1,483 MW combined-cycle plant serves PJM and will continue to be managed and operated by Invenergy.
Source
Horse Creek / Electra Wind Farms | Ardian / MEAG
Ardian / MEAG (Minority Stake Acquisition / Sale) · Onshore Wind Generation · Texas, United States
MEAG, on behalf of Munich Re, completed the sale of its minority interests in Horse Creek and Electra to existing co-owner Ardian, giving Ardian full ownership. The two operating ERCOT wind farms have 460 MW of combined installed capacity.
Source
Power Planet | EIG
EIG (Growth Investment) · Enhanced Geothermal Systems · Nevada, United States
EIG Geothermal Catalyst Partners completed its inaugural investment in Power Planet to advance the Star Peak enhanced-geothermal project and a broader Nevada portfolio. Power Planet expects Star Peak to begin delivering power in 2027 / 2028.
Source
Social Infra3 Deals
Nine UK School PPP Projects | Amber / APG / InfraRed
Amber Infrastructure / InfraRed / APG (Sale / Acquisition) · Education PPPs · United Kingdom
Amber-advised INPP agreed to sell interests in nine UK PPP projects comprising 15 schools to an InfraRed-managed account backed by APG on behalf of ABP. Gross proceeds are expected to exceed £58mm, representing a premium to the portfolio’s June valuation.
Source
InfraMed Properties | Pantheon
Pantheon (Platform Launch / Seed Equity) · Medical Outpatient Facilities · United States
Pantheon led the institutional LP support for One Orchard’s investment in newly launched InfraMed Properties. The healthcare-infrastructure platform was seeded with 25 medical-outpatient assets across 16 states and is targeting more than US$1bn of investments.
Source
Bryant Hammett & Associates | Bernhard Capital
Bernhard Capital / Aventia (PortCo Acquisition) · Civil Engineering & Infrastructure Services · Louisiana, United States
Bernhard-backed Aventia acquired Bryant Hammett & Associates, adding civil engineering, surveying and hazard-mitigation capabilities across four Louisiana offices. The combination extends Aventia’s 400-plus-person infrastructure-services platform, which serves clients in more than 40 states.
Source
Digital1 Deal
Fiberhost / INEA | Macquarie AM
Macquarie AM / MEIF5 (Sale) · Fibre Network & Telecommunications · Poland
Macquarie AM, on behalf of MEIF5, agreed to sell Fiberhost and INEA to Deutsche Telekom for integration into T-Mobile Polska. Fiberhost’s open-access network passes 1.4mm homes across more than 9,700 towns and villages and serves more than 160 providers.
Source
Transportation1 Deal
FlightServ FBO | Igneo
Igneo / Infinity Aviation (PortCo Acquisition) · Fixed-Base Aviation Services · Trenton, New Jersey
Igneo-backed Infinity Aviation acquired FlightServ’s fixed-base operations at Trenton-Mercer Airport, its third location and second bolt-on. The acquired assets include fueling services, approximately 80,000 square feet of modern hangarage and a development parcel for expansion.
Source
