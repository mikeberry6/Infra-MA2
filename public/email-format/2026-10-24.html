Power & ET5 Deals
Ashtead Technology | Ember Infrastructure
Ember Infrastructure (Buyout) · Subsea Technology · United Kingdom
Ember Infrastructure agreed to acquire Ashtead Technology for 615p per share, valuing its equity at ~£505mm. The subsea equipment and services platform supports offshore energy infrastructure; the recommended cash offer follows September’s non-binding approach and is subject to shareholder and other approvals.
Source
Oklahoma wind portfolio — 49% stake | Ares
Ares (Minority Stake) · Onshore Wind · United States
Ares Infrastructure funds acquired 49% of TransAlta’s 500MW Oklahoma wind portfolio for US$305mm. The three operating wind farms have long-term contracts with investment-grade counterparties, while TransAlta retains operational oversight under an asset-management agreement.
Source
Electrip Global — Zorlu stake | Wren House
Wren House (Minority Stake) · EV Charging · Europe / Turkey
Wren House and Kuwait Investment Authority signed an agreement to acquire Zorlu Enerji’s remaining Electrip holding for US$147.6mm. The interest represents 41.28% of voting A ordinary shares in the EV-charging infrastructure business; Zorlu will fully exit its shareholding.
Source
Ełk / Turośń Kościelna battery projects | KKR
KKR (Portfolio Company Divestiture) · Battery Storage · Poland
KKR infrastructure-backed Greenvolt Power agreed to sell two completed Polish battery projects totaling 400MW / 1.6GWh to A&R Investments. Each asset has a 17-year capacity-market contract starting in 2028 and a 10-year tolling agreement; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Lundsby Renewable Solutions business | CIP
CIP (Co-Investment) · Biogas Engineering · Denmark
CIP’s Advanced Bioenergy Fund joined majority investor CoreCapital in acquiring Lundsby’s business from its bankruptcy estate. The re-established Danish platform preserves more than 30 years of experience developing and building Nordic biogas plants; the investment amount was undisclosed.
Source
Transportation3 Deals
Infinity Transportation | Ares
Ares (Buyout) · Railcar Leasing · North America
Ares Infrastructure Equity and Insurance Solutions funds agreed to acquire Infinity Transportation from KKR’s Global Atlantic for ~US$2.9bn enterprise value. Infinity owns and manages ~50,000 railcars serving more than 350 lessees across the United States, Canada and Mexico; closing is expected in Q1 2027.
Source
Associated British Ports | IFM Investors / CPP Investments / OMERS
IFM Investors / CPP Investments / OMERS (Majority Stake / Sale) · Ports · United Kingdom
An IFM Global Infrastructure Fund subsidiary signed a binding agreement to acquire CPP Investments’ and OMERS’ combined 63.88% interest in Associated British Ports. The UK’s largest ports group operates 19 facilities handling ~25% of UK seaborne trade; financial terms were undisclosed and approvals remain pending.
Source
Monson Logistics | Igneo
Igneo (Bolt-On) · Freight Logistics · Australia
Igneo’s ADIF-backed Strait Link agreed to acquire Monson Logistics from founder Bruce Monson. Monson adds 65 prime movers, four depots and ~100 employees across Tasmania and Victoria, extending Strait Link’s integrated Bass Strait freight network; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Digital1 Deal
Pilot Fiber / Boldyn NY–NJ fiber network | Fengate
Fengate (Primary Capital Raise / Acquisition) · Fiber Networks · United States
Fengate Infrastructure Fund V agreed to invest in Pilot Fiber and fund its acquisition of Boldyn’s New York / New Jersey metro fiber business, providing over US$330mm in total financing. The combined owned network will span more than 1,600 route miles, 1,200 buildings and 50 data centers; the equity amount was not separately disclosed.
Source
Social Infra1 Deal
Cleanaway Waste Management | EQT
EQT (Buyout) · Waste Management · Australia
EQT Infrastructure VI signed a binding scheme implementation deed to acquire Cleanaway for A$3.095 per share, implying ~A$9.3bn enterprise value. Cleanaway’s national network spans collection, recycling, treatment and disposal; the board-recommended transaction remains subject to shareholder, court and other conditions.
Source
