Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • October 24 – October 30, 2026
KEY THEMES
EIG’s Plenum Power JV targets ~US$4bn of launch equity, alongside La Caisse’s C$2.45bn Bell tower investment and Asterion’s ~£2.3bn enterprise-value Mobico take-private.

Power & ET9 Deals
Plenum Power | EIG
EIG (Joint Venture) · Power Generation & Storage · United States
EIG and Acwa entered definitive agreements to establish Plenum Power, a U.S. power investment platform targeting ~US$4bn of initial equity at launch. The partnership plans to pursue acquisitions and new development across conventional generation, renewables and storage; the launch target is not capital already deployed.
Source
Six UK biogas plants — remaining 49% | 3i Infrastructure
3i Infrastructure (Bolt-On) · Biogas · United Kingdom
3i Infrastructure-backed Future Biogas acquired Foresight Environmental Infrastructure’s remaining 49% interest in six gas-to-grid biogas facilities for £104mm, taking full ownership. The purchase follows its initial 51% investment in 2024 and allows capacity expansion and operational improvements across the portfolio.
Source
Qwello | Tiger Infrastructure
Tiger Infrastructure (Primary Capital Raise) · EV Charging · Europe
Tiger Infrastructure participated alongside ABN AMRO Sustainable Impact Fund and other existing investors in up to €55mm of growth equity for Qwello. The public charging operator has more than 19,500 charge points across eight European countries; the funding supports network expansion and selective acquisitions, and Tiger’s individual contribution was undisclosed.
Source
550+ MW Greek solar project | Mubadala
Mubadala (Bolt-On) · Solar Generation · Greece
Mubadala-backed Masdar, through TERNA ENERGY, signed an agreement to acquire and develop a 550+ MW solar project from Lightsource bp, including two substations. The project is expected to operate by year-end 2026, with battery storage planned; consideration was undisclosed and closing remains subject to approvals.
Source
Wind2 | Brookfield
Brookfield (Bolt-On) · Onshore Wind Development · United Kingdom
Brookfield infrastructure-backed Boralex acquired Wind2, a UK renewables developer with ~350MW of advanced wind projects across Scotland, England and Wales. The acquisition also brings teams in two UK offices, expanding Boralex’s development capacity; transaction value was undisclosed.
Source
Baixas / Coquainvilliers battery projects | BlackRock
BlackRock (Portfolio Company Divestiture) · Battery Storage · France
BlackRock infrastructure-backed Recurrent Energy sold two French battery projects totaling 200MW / 800MWh to BW ESS. Recurrent will advance remaining development milestones before BW ESS takes the projects through construction and long-term operation; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Bettyhill Phase 2 Wind Farm | J.P. Morgan AM
J.P. Morgan AM (Bolt-On) · Onshore Wind · United Kingdom
IIF-backed Nadara acquired the consented 48MW Bettyhill Phase 2 wind project in Scotland from Eden Renewables. Nadara will finance, build and operate the project, with construction expected in Q1 2027 and operation in Q4 2028; consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Tallgrass community-solar portfolio | Brookfield
Brookfield (Bolt-On) · Community Solar · United States
Brookfield-backed Standard Solar acquired 13 Illinois community-solar projects totaling ~43.3MW from Trajectory Energy Partners. Standard Solar will construct, own and operate the portfolio, with commercial operation expected in phases during 2027–2028; financial terms were undisclosed.
Source
Kore HVAC Services | Basalt
Basalt (Bolt-On) · HVAC & Energy Services · Canada
Basalt-backed Go Lime acquired Kore HVAC Services, extending its home and commercial energy-services platform into Greater Sudbury and Northern Ontario. Kore’s local brand, technicians and existing service agreements will continue; transaction terms were undisclosed.
Source
Digital2 Deals
Bell wireless TowerCo — 49.9% stake | La Caisse
La Caisse (Minority Stake) · Wireless Towers · Canada
La Caisse agreed to acquire 49.9% of Bell Canada’s new wireless TowerCo for C$2.45bn, implying a ~C$4.91bn valuation. The platform will own more than 5,000 sites, with Bell retaining control and leasing tower capacity under long-term agreements; closing is expected in Q1 2027, subject to approvals.
Source
Gabia / KINX shareholdings | Macquarie AM
Macquarie AM (Acquisition) · Digital Infrastructure · South Korea
Macquarie Asia-Pacific Infrastructure Fund 4 signed agreements to acquire major shareholders’ Gabia and KINX stakes at KRW53,000 and KRW190,000 per share, respectively, with management rolling part of its holdings. The transaction aims to simplify the dual-listed ownership structure; further tender offers are intended after approvals, and aggregate consideration was undisclosed.
Source
Transportation2 Deals
Mobico Group | Asterion
Asterion (Buyout) · Passenger Transport · United Kingdom / International
Asterion Fund III-backed AdL, jointly held with CMC Group, agreed a 46p-per-share cash offer for Mobico through Leita Mobility, implying ~£308mm equity value and ~£2.3bn enterprise value. The coach, bus and rail operator serves 40 cities across 11 countries; the recommended scheme is expected to complete in H1 2027, subject to approvals.
Source
Cernaval | CVC DIF
CVC DIF (Majority Stake) · Ship Repair Infrastructure · Spain
CVC DIF acquired a majority stake in Cernaval, a ship-repair business operating under a long-term concession at the Port of Algeciras Bay. Its facilities include a 400-metre dry dock and a 230-metre floating dock; CVC DIF plans to modernise the shipyard, with consideration undisclosed.
Source
Midstream1 Deal
Iron Horse Midstream | Sixth Street
Sixth Street (Sale) · Gas Gathering & Processing · United States
Sixth Street agreed to sell Iron Horse Midstream to Howard Energy Partners. The Oklahoma business includes ~400 miles of gas pipelines and 425mm cubic feet/day of processing capacity, supported by multidecade customer contracts; the transaction is expected to close by year-end 2026, with consideration undisclosed.
