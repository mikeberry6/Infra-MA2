ArcLight Capital — North American direct infrastructure fund census
Conclusion
The census identifies 11 current qualifying vehicles under ArcLight Capital Partners: nine closed-active funds or co-investment vehicles and two vehicles still raising. Four qualify through an explicit North American mandate and seven through a verified current North American holding. ArcLight's current official materials describe the firm as a value-added infrastructure investor focused on critical electrification infrastructure, including power, renewables and strategic gas. (arclight.com)
DigitalBridge and ArcLight announced a definitive strategic combination in May 2026, but the transaction remained subject to completion conditions and the announcement stated that ArcLight would continue to operate as a distinct business. Accordingly, DigitalBridge is recorded as an overlapping supplied manager rather than a completed successor or merged fund identity. (businesswire.com)

Exclusions and unresolved issues
ArcLight VI Upstream Annex Fund is excluded because ArcLight's regulatory and ESG materials associate it with production investments rather than direct infrastructure. ARC Infra SMA is excluded because its March 2026 Form D reported no first sale and $0 sold, and no fund-specific North American mandate or current direct holding was located.

Completeness
Research covered ArcLight's official investment list, portfolio-services page, fund-closing announcements, transaction-closing sources, 2025 ESG report, SEC Form D filings and the July 1, 2026 Form ADV. Fund VIII-A/B/C were consolidated into the single commercial Fund VIII, while the GCX Fund VIII A/B entities were consolidated because their SEC filing treats them as parallel issuers in one aggregated offering. Feeder entities were not counted separately.
Legacy Funds I-IV were checked for current fund-attributed North American holdings. Funds I/II remain subject to the Lodi Holdings II conflict; no qualifying current Fund III or Fund IV evidence was found. No program exception was used. The review opened 29 distinct sources and ran 42 search queries; primary offering documents were not located for the APIP and Aransas mandate descriptions, which is why both remain NEEDS_REVIEW.
