Conclusion

Exclusions and unresolved issues
Infrastructure debt and credit products are excluded because they do not satisfy the direct-equity test. Infrastructure secondaries, fund-of-funds, LP-interest, and access sleeves are also excluded. Climate and emerging-markets vehicles without an explicit United States, Canadian, or Mexican mandate or a fund-attributed current North American holding are outside this census.
The GEPIF-to-GIF naming transition is treated as a series evolution, not as evidence that historical GEPIF I-III names should be rewritten or duplicated as GIF I-III. The official historical fund names are preserved.
The three Mexican vehicles are included as listed-program exceptions because the completed evidence supports three distinct directly investing commercial vehicles with Mexican mandates. Their exact trust names, tickers, vintage dates, and feeder or parallel legal entities remain subject to final BMV filing transcription.
The AI Infrastructure Partnership is counted once as a distinct program. It is not merged into GIF IV, GRP IV, Evergreen, or a GIP flagship fund. BlackRock and GIP overlap is preserved, while undisclosed partner economics and acquisition SPVs are not treated as separate funds.
