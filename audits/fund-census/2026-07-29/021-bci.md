BCI — North American direct infrastructure fund census
Conclusion
As of July 29, 2026, the census identifies one current qualifying BCI vehicle: the Infrastructure & Renewable Resources Program, included as a PROGRAM_EXCEPTION rather than a conventional third-party named fund. BCI describes I&RR as an active global program focused primarily on core infrastructure and renewable-resource assets, while its audited statements confirm that it primarily invests directly in privately held companies and held C$24.531 billion of direct private equity at December 31, 2025. Because the statements also say the combined Program is not itself a legal entity, its overall confidence is capped at MEDIUM. (bci.ca)
The program qualifies through VERIFIED_CURRENT_NA_HOLDING: BCI stated in April 2026 that I&RR has been a shareholder in Global Container Terminals since 2017 and continues to support the Canadian operator of two Port of Vancouver container terminals. (bci.ca)
The canonical manifest identity remains BCI. Its legal name is British Columbia Investment Management Corporation, its historical abbreviation was bcIMC, and its 2017–2018 annual report records the rebrand to BCI. Exclusions and unresolved issues
Investment Grade Private Credit Fund is excluded under DEBT_OR_CREDIT. BCI describes it as a dedicated investment-grade corporate-credit and asset-backed-financing strategy, primarily focused on North America and Europe; that geographic scope does not make it direct infrastructure equity. (bci.ca)
The audited I&RR statements list Bolsena Fund, Bolsena (Australia) Fund, Bolsena (Public) Fund, Renewable Resources Investment Fund, Orto (S-NPC) Fund, and various structured entities and individual investments as the entities comprising the combined program. They are not counted as separate commercial rows because the same statements present them within one combined program, say the Program is not itself a legal entity, and do not provide each constituent with an independently evidenced commercial strategy, capital base, lifecycle, and North American holding attribution. (bci.ca)
Two disclosure issues remain:


BCI reports C$36.5B AUM, while its audited statements report C$35.337B of program net assets and C$35.404B of investments at December 31, 2025. These are differently defined measures, and no public reconciliation was located. The census therefore preserves C$36.5B strictly as AUM, rather than recasting it as net assets, investments, commitments, or a close amount. (bci.ca)


The 2024 statements said BCI intended to wind up the Renewable Resources Investment Fund, with the date unconfirmed. The 2025 statements still list it as an I&RR constituent without expressly confirming whether the legal wind-up occurred. This does not alter the one-row program census but warrants confirmation if internal pooled entities are separately maintained in the repository. (bci.ca)
