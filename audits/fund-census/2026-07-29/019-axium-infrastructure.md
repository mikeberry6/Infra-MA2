Axium Infrastructure — North American direct infrastructure fund census
Conclusion
The census includes three current direct-equity vehicles: (1) the 2011-vintage, Canada-only Axium Infrastructure Canada L.P.; (2) the continuing open-end Axium Infrastructure North American Fund, whose numbered feeder, country and parallel entities are treated as one commercial fund platform; and (3) the separately named B2C ES Coinvest LP, a transaction-specific co-investment vehicle with a current California solar-and-storage investment. Axium's own materials and 2025 institutional diligence describe two North American flagship funds, while the B2C vehicle is independently supported by an institutional commitment disclosure, Axium's transaction announcement, year-end 2025 holdings and a 2026 adviser filing.
The current manager is Axium Infrastructure. Institutional diligence records the firm's original Fiera Axium identity and July 2015 name change, and Axium's January 2016 announcement documents the completion of its acquisition of Fiera Capital's remaining 35% interest.

Exclusions and unresolved issues
Axium Infrastructure Global Fund is excluded under SECONDARIES_OR_FUND_OF_FUNDS: Axium describes it as investing in its North American and European regional funds, not directly in operating infrastructure. The Europe regional fund is outside the requested geography and is not placed in the structured exclusion array because the supplied reason-code set has no geography-only code.

They are not counted without an LPA, PPM, audited statement or investor commitment showing a separately marketed strategy and capital base.


Trade press reported a planned first globally focused open-end co-investment fund, but the reviewed evidence does not establish an official name, launch or close, North American qualification, or current fund-attributed North American asset by July 29, 2026.


B2C ES Coinvest LP's identity, commitment, direct deployment and current status are supported, but its public materials do not state the contractual term or expressly label it open-end or closed-end. Closed-End is therefore a provisional schema classification for a fixed, transaction-specific co-investment LP pending review of its LPA or PPM.


Completeness
The review covered official launch and final-close releases, corporate-history materials, current ESG/SFDR/PAI disclosures, transaction announcements, institutional investment-committee diligence, LP holdings, fundraising reporting and the latest reviewed U.S. and Canadian adviser filings. Manager aliases and the Fiera lineage were tested; numbered AINA, AIC II and AxInfra US feeder/country/parallel entities were reviewed for double counting; and current holdings were checked for the older Canada fund and the B2C co-investment. Twenty-eight sources were opened across thirty targeted searches. No access failure blocked a meaningful result, although private contractual documents for the identified co-investment and asset-holding entities remain unavailable.
