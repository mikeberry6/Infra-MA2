APG Infrastructure — North American direct infrastructure fund census
Conclusion
The census identifies eight current qualifying vehicles under the canonical manager APG Asset Management: seven separately named APG Infrastructure Pools and one infrastructure-equity co-investment vehicle, APG Infrastructure Asset Owner Fund III C.V. APG’s current product materials list the seven pools individually, and its fund-specific restrictions expressly include the United States, Canada and Mexico.
The seven pools are CLOSED_ACTIVE, are represented as Closed-End with Financial Close status under the required snapshot taxonomy, and qualify through EXPLICIT_NA_MANDATE. No pool-level capital amount was established from the opened evidence, so each size is reported as Undisclosed. Fund III is also CLOSED_ACTIVE, but remains NEEDS_REVIEW: its legal APG name is documented, Pattern Energy verifies current North American deployment, and the commercial program is active, but public sources do not fully reconcile the APG legal vehicle with the Record-managed co-investment vehicle and its latest reported capital base.

Exclusions and unresolved issues
APG Infrastructure Asset Owner Fund I C.V. is excluded as FULLY_REALIZED_OR_DISSOLVED: APG reported no investments, and NZ Super Fund states that the partnership was dissolved in December 2024 without having invested.
APG Infrastructure Asset Owner Fund II C.V. is excluded as GLOBAL_WITHOUT_NA_EVIDENCE. It is a current €1.0 billion, 15-year program, but its disclosed mandate is only developed markets or global. Those generic labels do not establish North American qualification, and no current North American holding was attributed to Fund II.
The single unresolved conflict concerns Fund III. De Brauw identifies the exact APG legal fund, APG describes the partnership as a co-investment program, and Record identifies the participating vehicle through APG’s Asset Owner Partnership. The opened materials do not establish that Record’s latest €1.23 billion commitment figure belongs entirely to APG Infrastructure Asset Owner Fund III C.V. An audited report, constitutional document or official APG–Record vehicle map is required before assigning that latest amount or final commercial-manager identity.
