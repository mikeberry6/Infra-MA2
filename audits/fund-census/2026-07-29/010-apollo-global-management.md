Apollo Global Management — North American direct infrastructure fund census
Conclusion
Four current Apollo vehicles satisfy the fund-specific inclusion test: AIOF II, AIOF III, Apollo Infrastructure Company and the Apollo Starmoon CEPF co-investment vehicle formerly named Apollo Core Infrastructure Fund. Three match repository rows but require corrections; Starmoon is proposed as a new row. The two repository ACT Equity rows remain review items because the available evidence does not establish both direct-infrastructure scope and North American qualification. Repository reconciliation
AIOF II is corrected to a 2020 vintage and $2.542 billion of commitments; AIOF III to a 2024 vintage and $2.399 billion of commitments. Both receive Apollo's core-plus/value-add classification. AIC is corrected to its official name, 2023 inception and core/core-plus operating-company program classification. Its displayed net asset value is not presented as fund commitments or AUM.
ACT Equity I and II are each reconciled once as repository-only NEEDS_REVIEW records. The Form D for ACT Equity II establishes an offering entity, but does not establish a final close, infrastructure mandate or North American qualification. Purmo evidence concerns a European investment. (sec.gov)
Exclusions and unresolved issues
AIOF I is excluded as fully realized. ACT Capital is excluded as credit or structured solutions. ACT Equity I and II, Digital Origin Partners and the ACT Equity ELTIF fail the fund-specific inclusion standard; parallel AIOF legal entities are consolidated.
Starmoon remains medium confidence because the public legal chain supports the rename and equity ownership but does not disclose the complete current LP structure. Apollo's signed acquisition of Argo is preserved as an overlap rather than a merger of manager results because the official announcement described closing as prospective and no fund-level successor evidence was located. (apollo.com)
Completeness
Official Apollo fund materials, SEC filings, fundraising and close sources, current holding records, manager aliases, legal renames, feeder and parallel entities, AIC share series and the Argo overlap were reviewed. Thirty-three sources were opened across 29 searches. Critical inclusion findings rely on primary Apollo, SEC or institutional evidence; the secondary AIOF III final-close report is corroborative rather than the source of the exact commitment amount.
