Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners — North American direct infrastructure fund census

Conclusion
Seven current direct-equity vehicles qualify through explicit North American mandates: CI II, CI III, CI IV, CI V, CI Energy Transition Fund I, and Advanced Bioenergy Funds I and II. CI IV and CI V match the two repository rows and require only the addition of North America to their region classifications; five other current commercial funds are proposed new census records under the existing manager.

Repository reconciliation
FUND-056A and FUND-056 are each reconciled exactly once. Their commercial identities and rounded display amounts are retained. A later CIP summary shows slightly higher fund-size figures, so amount precision remains an unresolved review item rather than an inferred correction.

Exclusions
CI I is no longer current; Artemis I and II are Germany-only; Growth Markets Funds I and II target markets outside North America; Green Credit Funds I and II are debt; CIP GET is a fund of funds; and feeder, AIV and co-investment legal entities are not counted separately. No public fund-specific evidence established CI VI by the census date.

Completeness
Official fund pages, the 2024 annual report, current sustainability and periodic-disclosure pages, fundraising and close materials, current-lifecycle evidence, manager identity, and parallel-vehicle structure were reviewed. Twelve official sources were opened across eight targeted searches. No production fund record or ownership link was modified.
