Ardian — North American direct infrastructure fund census
Conclusion
The census identifies five current qualifying vehicles: three directly managed Ardian infrastructure funds already represented in the repository, one additional Ardian nature-based infrastructure fund, and one hydrogen-infrastructure fund managed through Ardian’s Hy24 joint venture. Ardian is the canonical current manager name; AXA Private Equity completed its spin-out and became Ardian in September 2013. (ardian.com)
The three repository vehicles require field corrections rather than new identities. Averrhoa Nature-Based Solutions is a proposed addition under Ardian. Clean H2 Infra Fund qualifies substantively but remains NEEDS_REVIEW because primary sources identify Hy24—a 50/50 Ardian/FiveT Hydrogen joint venture—as its regulated manager. Exclusions and unresolved issues
Outside North America: Ardian Infrastructure Fund V is expressly a European fund. ACEEF has a global label but an explicit European focus and documented current deployment in Europe and South America rather than the US, Canada or Mexico. TSH’s identified holding is the France-based Geosel platform. (ardian.com)
Outside the direct-equity vehicle definition: ASF VIII/IX are infrastructure-secondaries or fund-of-funds vehicles; the Clean Hydrogen Equipment Fund targets equipment and technology companies and made its first disclosed investment through a manufacturer’s convertible bond; and Ardian’s separately listed Infrastructure Debt Fund is excluded as credit. (ardian.com)
Insufficient fund-specific evidence: Ardian Access Infrastructure combines direct-infrastructure and secondaries exposure, but the opened materials do not attribute a qualifying current North American direct holding to the access vehicle itself. Ardian also disclosed a future mid-cap infrastructure strategy combining US and European opportunities, but no official fund name, close, capital base or investable lifecycle was available. (ardian.com)
Two conflicts remain unresolved:


Ardian’s September 2023 release announces the launch of Averrhoa, while its June 2026 article states that the Article 9 fund launched in 2024. The snapshot uses the later official source’s stated 2024 fund launch, while the conflict remains pending legal-fund or audited-report evidence. (ardian.com)


Clean H2 Infra Fund is associated with Ardian but managed by Hy24. A manifest-level decision is required on whether jointly owned specialist managers receive separate canonical manager records. (hy24partners.com)


Completeness
The review covered Ardian’s official infrastructure and Nature-Based Solutions pages, fund close releases, the 2025 Integrated Report, the 2025 Article 29 product list, current portfolio evidence, acquisition-completion sources and Hy24’s fund materials. Legal feeders and parallel entities were consolidated rather than counted separately: AAIF V and its Euro feeder; AIF VI, AIF VI B and its feeders; Averrhoa compartments 1 and 2; TSH and its feeder; and ASF IX variants. The $20 billion AIF VI platform total was not treated as a separate fund because Ardian states that it comprises AIF VI plus co-investments alongside the fund. (ardian.com)
No program exception was required, no repository vehicle was left unreconciled, and no inaccessible primary source prevented a disposition.
