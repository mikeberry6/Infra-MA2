CDPQ — North American direct infrastructure fund census
Conclusion
The census identifies one qualifying current direct-equity infrastructure program: the Infrastructure Specialized Portfolio. It is included as a PROGRAM_EXCEPTION because it is La Caisse's evergreen sovereign/pension specialized portfolio rather than a third-party commingled fund. The 2025 annual report identifies the formal portfolio name, reports C$74.506 billion of net assets under management at December 31, 2025, and states that the portfolio was created on July 1, 2010. Its mandate consists principally of interests in listed and unlisted companies operating infrastructure and utility-type assets. (lacaisse.com)
North American qualification rests on verified current deployment, not merely a generic global label. The annual report reports 30% United States and 21% Canadian exposure at December 31, 2025. An April 22, 2026 release also confirms that La Caisse remained an equity sponsor and long-term partner of Vertical Bridge, a United States wireless-infrastructure owner and operator. (lacaisse.com)
The manager's current public brand is La Caisse, formerly CDPQ, while its governing legal entity remains the Caisse de dépôt et placement du Québec. The supplied manifest identity, La Caisse de dépôt (CDPQ), is retained as the canonical repository manager rather than treated as a separate organization. (lacaisse.com)
