Exclusions and unresolved issues
Astatine Infrastructure Fund III (Euro), SCSp has an active EEA marketing notification dated May 11, 2026, and Astatine's current team page refers to Fund III's investment committee. Those facts establish the vehicle's existence but not a North American mandate, current North American deployment, direct-equity terms, first close or complete commercial-family boundary. It is therefore excluded pending fund-specific evidence. (fi.se)
Logistics Partners Co-Investment A and Ribble Co-Invest Vehicle A are current infrastructure private funds in adviser filings and remain present in pension holdings. No opened source, however, identifies either underlying asset or establishes U.S., Canadian or Mexican deployment. Their names and domiciles are insufficient to qualify them. (reports.adviserinfo.sec.gov)
ATA I and ATA 2 are excluded because Astatine presents the ATA strategy within its separate global equipment-leasing and tangible-assets business, rather than its mid-market infrastructure-equity business. The broader Form ADV category assigned to ATA 2 does not override the manager's strategy boundary. (astatineip.com)
The legacy Alinda Infrastructure Fund II main, parallel and AIV entities are excluded because the current adviser filing expressly states that they were formally dissolved in 2025. (reports.adviserinfo.sec.gov)
The Fund I and Fund II main, Euro, parallel and AIV legal entities are consolidated into their respective commercial families. Fund II's current SFDR disclosure expressly treats those entities collectively as “Fund II,” so separate census rows would double-count the same strategy and capital family. (fundrock-lis.com)
The proposed listed AT85 Global Mid-Market Infrastructure Income plc is excluded as a retail access wrapper designed to invest in Astatine funds and co-investments, not as a qualifying direct-investment program of the manager. No current launch evidence was found. (quoteddata.com)
