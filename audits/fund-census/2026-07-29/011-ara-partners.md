Ara Partners — North American direct infrastructure fund census
Conclusion
As of 2026-07-29, the census includes two commercially distinct vehicles: Ara Infrastructure Fund I and Ara Energy Fund I. Infrastructure Fund I is closed and active, with multiple active North American holdings attributed to the fund. Energy Fund I is a live named fund in market with an explicit North American mandate, but no signed acquisition was counted as a current holding without closing evidence. Ara Fund III is excluded because fund-specific primary evidence classifies it as the manager's third private-equity buyout and growth fund, not the separate Infrastructure or Energy strategy. Ara Partners is the canonical current manager name; Argus Energy Managers is the documented 2018 predecessor name. (arapartners.com)

Exclusions and unresolved issues
Ara Fund III is excluded under the OTHER reason code because Ara describes it as an oversubscribed third private-equity fund focused on buyout and growth investments, and the current portfolio labels Fund III holdings as Strategy Private Equity. (arapartners.com)
Two items remain for human follow-up. First, Ara Energy Fund I's target, committed capital, vintage and final-close status remain undisclosed in primary fund materials: the Form D reported an indefinite offering and zero sold at filing; StepStone and Liberty Mutual subsequently evidence commitments, while Infrastructure Investor reported an up-to-$1.5 billion figure and later reported that Ara declined to disclose the target. Second, the Gate City Power, Gate City Renewable Fuels and JET acquisitions were announced as conditional; the June 2026 New York ruling still described the transfer as proposed and required notice after completion, and no completion notice was located by the as-of date. (sec.gov)
There is no program exception and no overlapping supplied manager. The I-A issuers and named co-investment entities are parallel or associated vehicles within the two commercial fundraises and are not separately counted.
