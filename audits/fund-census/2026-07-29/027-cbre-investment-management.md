CBRE Investment Management — North American direct infrastructure fund census
Conclusion
As of 2026-07-29, one commercially distinct current vehicle qualifies: CBRE Global Infrastructure Fund (CGIF). The manager lineage is Caledon Capital Management, founded in 2006; CBRE Caledon after Caledon joined CBRE Global Investors in 2017; and CBRE Investment Management after the CBRE Global Investors, CBRE Clarion and CBRE Caledon brands merged in 2021.
CGIF is documented as a diversified global open-end fund focused on mid-market core and core-plus infrastructure in OECD countries, while CBRE Investment Management's current direct strategy page describes direct investments through pooled vehicles and separately managed accounts. North American qualification is not inferred from the generic Global or OECD labels: Pennsylvania and Georgia utility filings attribute the U.S. fiber operator WANRack and the related GW Operating ownership chain to CBRE Global Infrastructure Fund Holdings I, LP, and the Georgia filing states that the 2026 internal restructuring does not change ultimate ownership or control.

Exclusions and unresolved issues
Five legal vehicles remain excluded for INSUFFICIENT_FUND_SPECIFIC_EVIDENCE: CBRE Bright Star Infra Co-Invest LP, CBRE Starlight Infra Co-Invest LP, CBRE Musselshell Infrastructure Investments, LP, CBRE Minke Infrastructure Investments, LP, and CBRE Andromeda Infrastructure Investments IV, LP. The opened filings establish vehicle existence—and, for Musselshell, an institutional commitment—but do not establish every required element of direct-equity strategy, current North American mandate or holding, current lifecycle, and distinct commercial identity.
CBRE's Global Indirect Private Infrastructure strategy is excluded because it builds exposure through funds and joint ventures rather than qualifying as the requested direct-equity vehicle. The CBRE Global Infrastructure Securities Fund and CBRE Global Infrastructure SMA are excluded because they invest in listed infrastructure securities.
Three conflicts remain open: the underlying assets, North American exposure and relationship between Bright Star and Starlight; the direct-versus-indirect nature, asset attribution and commercial identity of Musselshell, Minke and Andromeda IV; and the precise valuation date underlying CGIF's Form ADV gross asset value, because the filing states the amount but not a separate valuation date. No program exception is used, and no overlapping supplied manager was identified.
