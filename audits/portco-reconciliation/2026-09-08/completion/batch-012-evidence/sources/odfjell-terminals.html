Odfjell Terminals offers safe and efficient storage for vital liquids, chemicals, and oil products at international shipping hubs.

The four tank terminals in the US, Belgium, and Korea provide a diversified service offering and product mix. By combining shipping and tank terminals, Odfjell is uniquely positioned to deliver increased safety, stronger product stewardship, greater efficiency, and improved arrival accuracy for customers.

Terminal expansions: Increased capacity to meet growing demands

As global trade patterns evolve and supply chains rationalize, tank terminals remain vital infrastructure in the chemical value chain. Operating high-barrier assets in key hubs, Odfjell combines long-term partnerships, safe operations, and local execution that together sustain value. To strengthen the platform and create additional value, we are growing our terminal business.
