Chris Beall

Founder & Managing Partner

Chris Beall is a Founder and the Managing Partner of NOVA. Chris is an experienced infrastructure investor with 28 years of experience in private equity, investment banking, infrastructure operations, and engineering. He serves as Chair of NOVA’s Investment Committee and on the Executive and Valuation Committees. Currently, Chris serves on the Boards of Directors for Fund I/II’s portfolio companies Integrated Waste Solutions Group, Nopetro Energy Holdings, and UGE.

Since 2017, Chris has built NOVA with Allison Kingsley, forming the NOVA Team and leading the execution of infrastructure investments in NOVA’s Target Sectors. Previously, from 2014 to 2017, Chris served as a Managing Director and Co-Portfolio Manager for the Oaktree Capital Group LLC (Oaktree) Infrastructure Investing strategy, acquired by Oaktree from Highstar Capital L.P. (Highstar) in 2014. Prior to Oaktree’s acquisition, he was one of four owners and a Partner of Highstar, the manager of a fourth-generation infrastructure fund responsible for a more than $6.3 billion portfolio of infrastructure investments. Prior to joining Highstar in 2004, Chris was CFO of the Aslan Group, an environmental services and communications firm. From 2000 to 2003, he also worked as an Investment Banking Associate in the Global Natural Resources Group at Lehman Brothers, Inc. Prior to business school, Chris worked in operations and engineering at Koch Gateway Pipeline Company and Koch Refining Company.

From 2013 through 2022, Chris was appointed to and served on the Board of Directors of the National Passenger Rail Corporation (Amtrak), the largest provider of passenger rail service in the U.S.

Chris earned an M.B.A. from Harvard Business School and a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Oklahoma State University.

 
 

Allison Kingsley

Founder & Partner

Allison Kingsley is a Founder of NOVA and a Partner. Allison is an experienced infrastructure investor with 26 years of professional experience, including work in infrastructure investing, investment banking, and global strategy and risk. She serves on NOVA’s Investment, Valuation, and Executive Committees. Currently, Allison serves on the Boards of Directors for Fund I/II’s portfolio companies Bold Ocean (realized), Harbor Logistics, and DartPoints.

Since 2017, Allison has built NOVA with Chris Beall, forming the NOVA Team and leading the execution of infrastructure investments in NOVA’s Target Sectors. Since 2001, Allison has sourced, structured, and invested in numerous transactions across global infrastructure sectors and geographies as Founder & Partner at NOVA Infrastructure, Director at PartnerRe and its sponsored asset manager Reformation Group, Vice President in the Emerging Markets and Project Finance Groups at Ambac, and Investment Banking Associate in the M&A Group at Lehman Brothers.  Allison is also a political economist and former tenured Associate Professor, teaching and publishing research at New York University, Yale University, and University of Vermont. 

Currently, Allison also serves on the Board of Directors for Phillips Infrastructure Holdings.

Allison earned a Ph.D. in political economy from Columbia University, an M.S.L. from Yale Law School, and a B.A. with honors from Rice University.
