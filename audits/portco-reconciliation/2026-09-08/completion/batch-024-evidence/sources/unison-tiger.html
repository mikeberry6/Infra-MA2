www.unisonenergy.com

Unison Energy is a fully integrated Energy-as-a-Service (EaaS) platform that designs, builds, owns, and operates behind-the-meter microgrids under long-term contracts for commercial and industrial customers in North America. Unison’s solutions enable customers to achieve savings on energy bills and decreases in carbon footprint, while ensuring increased energy security with no capital outlay. Unison owns and operates projects across essential end-markets including healthcare, data centers, hospitality, food & beverage, biofuels and other industrials.
