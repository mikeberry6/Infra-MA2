Portfolio

QSP

QSP is a joint venture containing a portfolio of three state-of-the-art data centre facilities in the #1 data center market globally

Key Information

Investment Date 2024
Geography USA
Sector Digital
Website https://qtsdatacenters.com

QSP is a joint venture between Wren House, QTS and another investor. The JV includes a portfolio of three fully constructed state-of-the-art hyperscale data centres in Northern Virginia, the largest data centre market in the world. The assets are fully contracted to investment grade tenants and will continue to be operated by QTS, one of the fastest growing data centre operators in the world. The strategic location of the assets in Northern Virginia coupled with the demand for data centres make them a highly defensible investment.
