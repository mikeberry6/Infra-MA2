Where We Excel

The rapid expansion of AI is driving extraordinary demand for digital infrastructure capacity and massive requirements for reliable power to support it. Vinson & Elkins is a leading law firm advising at the center of this new era — guiding developers, investors, hyperscalers, lenders, and other market participants across the full lifecycle of data center projects and transactions.

Why Clients Choose Vinson & Elkins for
Data Center and Power Infrastructure

Our lawyers bring deep, cross-disciplinary experience to the transactions driving the AI data center business: joint ventures, acquisitions and dispositions, equity investments, debt financings, engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) contracts, master lease and service agreements, build-to-suit arrangements, site management agreements, service-level agreements, and more.

No other firm is better positioned to advise on these complex, highly technical buildouts — from project development and finance, to conventional and renewable energy, to M&A, tax, regulatory compliance, environmental permitting, and real estate.

Business leaders worldwide trust Vinson & Elkins to guide them through their most complex data center projects and transactions. Contact us to learn how we can help with yours.
