Key sectors

Digital
Infrastructure

As the data revolution continues to unfold, driven by advancements in AI, Internet of Things (IoT), and increased connectivity, we are focused on supporting and building the future's information infrastructure by providing the physical layer that supports mobile and data networks.

Transport and Logistics Infrastructure

Transport and logistics infrastructure is integral in our fast-paced and interconnected world. Our investments are transforming the movement of people and goods, with expertise in aviation through regional airports, rail through rolling stock and terminals, and logistics. We seek high-quality opportunities in strategic locations with significant growth potential, staying at the forefront of industry trends such as the increasing electrification of transit, rising global urban populations, and heightened economic activity in emerging markets.

Energy Transition and Sustainability

Energy transition is critical to accelerating the path to net zero. We maximise impact by investing in both emerging technologies and legacy industries driving change. This includes buy-and-build platform investments applicable to development pipelines or distributed energy opportunities led by strong management teams. Our sustainable investments utilise digitalisation to enable energy transition, leveraging digital tools and technologies that transcend energy sectors.

InfraHealth

We invest in both adult and aged care businesses which are core to the social fabric of our communities. These are people businesses with operational and political complexities which demand thoughtful and comprehensive asset management approaches and close collaboration with the public realm. We utilise our functional human capital expertise, operating partner model and deep understanding of regulated industries to its full potential in these investments. Our private equity rigour drives better quality for residents, drives efficiencies and growth which provide attractive investment opportunities.

ZeMobility

Investment Date: August 2020

ZeMobility is a project established by InfraBridge to invest in public and private vehicle electrification across the Americas. The platform’s initial focus is on Latin America, a geographically attractive area with a robust pipeline of new electric bus fleet investment opportunities.

Invenergy JV

Investment Date: August 2018

ConGlobal

Investment Date: October 2017

ConGlobal operates rail terminals and intermodal equipment depots in North America, holding approximately 35% share of the market. Its portfolio spans 90 locations across the US, Mexico, and Costa Rica.

Telecom Infrastructure Partners

Investment Date: September 2021

Telecom Infrastructure Partners (TIP) is an acquiror and aggregator of long-term mobile telecommunication site leases globally. TIP is currently active across 12 countries and has more than 150 employees, with near-term plans to further grow and diversify on an international basis.

VX Fiber

Investment Date: September 2020

VX Fiber, founded in 2014, is a technology company providing managed software and services for fibre-to-the-premises (FTTP) networks. Its modular platform supports network owners and service providers in simplifying operations, scaling efficiently, and delivering high-performance connectivity on open access networks.

Achieve Together

Investment Date: January 2018

Achieve Together is a UK specialist care provider, offering support to adults with learning disabilities and complex needs. The organisation was formed through the successful merger of The Regard Group and Care Management Group in 2019.

Australia Pacific Airports Corporation Limited (APAC)

Investment Date: June 2017

Australia Pacific Airports Corporation Limited is the holding company of Melbourne Airport and Launceston Airport. Prior to the Covid-19 pandemic, Melbourne Airport served over 37 million domestic and international passengers per year and was the second busiest airport in Australia, while Launceston Airport served over one million domestic passengers per year and was the second busiest airport in Tasmania. APAC holds 99-year concessions for both airports, having acquired them in 1997 and 1998 respectively.

Leeds Bradford Airport

Investment Date: November 2017

Leeds Bradford Airport (LBA) is a non-price regulated, freehold regional airport located in the North of England. The airport has a large and wealthy catchment area, with more than 11 million passengers annually.

London Luton Airport

Investment Date: June 2018

London Luton Airport is the fifth largest airport in the UK, located 29 miles north of London. The airport is non-price regulated and operates under a concession agreement with Luton Borough Council serving a large catchment area of approximately 23 million people living within a two-hour drive.

Millennium Garages

Investment Date: May 2016

Millennium Garages is a portfolio of four underground parking garages comprised of over 9,000 parking spaces located directly beneath Millennium, Grant and Maggie Daley Parks in the East Loop area of downtown Chicago, Illinois. The system is the largest underground downtown parking system in the U.S. and is operated under a 99-year concession with the City of Chicago.

Opal HealthCare

Investment Date: November 2017

Opal HealthCare is an Australian for-profit provider of residential aged care, operating a geographically diverse portfolio of 91 care homes with approximately 9,000 beds located across New South Wales, Victoria, Queensland and Western Australia.

Freedom Fibre

Freedom Fibre is a UK-based FTTP wholesaler specializing in the development of a full-fibre network across the North West and Midlands. Founded by industry pioneer Neil McArthur, Freedom Fibre has become a leader in the Altnet sector, driving the rollout of gigabit-capable infrastructure. The company supports ultra-fast broadband delivery to around 300,000 premises and is expanding through strategic initiatives, including a 2024 merger with LilaConnect as its retail subsidiary.
