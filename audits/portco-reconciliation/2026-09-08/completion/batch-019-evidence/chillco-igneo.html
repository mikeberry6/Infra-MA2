    ChillCo

    ChillCo is a post production, multi temperature cold storage platform anchored by a 275,000 square foot facility in Zanesville, Ohio, serving a diversified base of food focused customers across the Midwest. The facility provides mission critical cold storage and value added services that are closely integrated into customers’ production and distribution processes, supporting high utilization and long standing customer relationships. ChillCo focuses on post production storage near food producers, delivering essential infrastructure with stable demand driven by food consumption and regional agricultural supply chains, while benefiting from limited exposure to speculative overbuilding and broader economic cycles.
