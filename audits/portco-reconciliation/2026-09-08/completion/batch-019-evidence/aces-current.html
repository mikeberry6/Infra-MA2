DELIVERING HYDROGEN FOR THE WESTERN U.S.

At ACES Delta, we’re expanding America’s next frontier of energy with  green hydrogen. A joint venture between Chevron U.S.A. and Mitsubishi Power, with Chevron as a majority owner, ACES Delta is driving the energy transition across the United States to transform intermittent renewables into reliable, safe, and affordable energy.

Enabling previously unattainable utility and industrial scale storage of renewable energy, we are transforming intermittent renewables into reliable, and affordable energy. With ACES Delta, the energy possibilities are limitless.

OUR VISION

Through our planned network of hydrogen hubs across the U.S., we will move the boundaries of renewable energy to achieve safe, reliable lower carbon solutions.

OUR MISSION

ACES Delta develops, owns, and operates a utility and industrial scale green hydrogen and storage site to empower our customers with a longer term solution to affordably and reliably reach net zero, providing dispatchable and long-duration energy storage.

OUR INITIAL SITE

Located in Delta, Utah, the Advanced Clean Energy Storage will be one of the world’s largest renewable energy storage facilities, providing a complete end-to-end solution to store and convert renewable hydrogen, enabling lower carbon strategies for the Western U.S.

OUR LEADERSHIP

Our expertise in energy solutions is a result of our deep expertise and decades-long experience in development of energy infrastructure, engineering, financing and operations.

Jonathan Perry, ACES Delta President & CEO
ACES Delta Board Chair
Chevron General Manager ACES Asset

Jonathan Perry is the President & CEO of ACES Delta and serves as General Manager where he will oversee the strategic direction of ACES. Over his career, Jonathan has overseen operations in over 33 countries, where he was accountable for safe and efficient execution of Chevron upstream asset development and exploration, enterprise asset retirement and environmental portfolios, major capital project delivery, and real estate. Most recently, Jonathan served as Vice President of Operations and Wells for Chevron Canada.

Jonathan joined Chevron in 2007 as a drill site manager and throughout his career, Corporate Social Responsibility has been a core tenant and focus in being a good partner in industry, with communities and governments. As a result, he’s led investment in lower carbon and environmental initiatives, while also serving and supporting social and responsible business community organizations.

Jonathan received his degree in civil engineering from Louisiana State University. His spouse and two children have enjoyed the unique experiences gained through having the opportunity to live and work all over the world in such diverse locations, landscapes and cultures.

William "Bill" Myers, ACES Delta Chief Operations Officer
Chevron Director of Projects & Operations

Bill is a Director of Projects and Operations in Chevron New Energies and is the Chief Operations Officer for ACES Delta. He spent the first 32 years of his career working on traditional power and energy projects from the Architect/Engineer side, Owner side (IPP and Utility), and finally the OEM side. Bill has spent the past 5 years on the ACES Delta team developing and then executing the Advanced Clean Energy Storage project, in Delta, UT, a utility scale electrolytic hydrogen conversion and storage facility. Initially as a Mitsubishi Power Americas employee and since January 2024 as a Chevron Employee.

Prerna Jain, ACES Delta Chief Commercial Officer
Chevron Integration & Growth Manager

Prerna has a decade and half of international experience in business development and asset commercialization, focusing on origination, deal structuring and contract negotiations in oil & gas, renewable fuels, CCUS and clean hydrogen sectors, in the US, Asia, Africa and South America.

Prerna co-led the transaction for Chevron to acquire majority interest in ACES and in her current role, she is leading the integration of ACES in Chevron and advancing future development opportunities for ACES assets.

Prior to Chevron, Prerna worked with McKinsey & Company as a consultant and has an MBA from the University of Chicago and MS Data Science from Texas A&M.

Donald V. Daniels, ACES Delta Board Member
Mitsubishi Power Americas, Inc.
Senior Vice President & Chief Strategy Officer

Donald “Don” Daniels is senior vice president and chief strategy officer of Mitsubishi Power Americas, Inc., a world leader in power generation and energy storage solutions. Don is responsible for all aspects of the company’s strategic planning and oversees many of the business’s staff functions including Marketing, Business Intelligence, Government Relations, Business Transformation and Environment, Health & Safety.

Don also leads a number of key strategic initiatives that include driving the company’s progress toward enabling our customers to combat climate change and advance human prosperity.

Mr. Daniels earned a bachelor’s degree in business administration from the University of Cincinnati, and has studied telecommunications in the Master of Science Program at Southern Methodist University.

Chip Toth, ACES Delta Board Member

Chip Toth has been an ACES Delta board member since 2020 and ACES 1 since July of 2025. He was part of the original team that supported Mitsubishi’s initial partnership with Magnum in the ACES Delta investment. He joined Mitsubishi Power Americas in 2019 to lead their commercial finance efforts and has helped Mitsubishi’s customers finance almost $1BB of equipment purchases. He was most recently the CFO of Mitsubishi’s Hydrogen Infrastructure business. Prior to joining Mitsubishi Mr. Toth lead the underwriting team at Czech Asset Management and spent 20 years with GE Capital in various divisions including Energy Financial Services. He has served on several other boards including Geometric Power (Nigeria’s first independent power company). Mr. Toth has an MBA from University of North Carolina Chapel Hill and a BA from the College of the Holy Cross.

Tyson Sharp, ACES Delta Chief Financial Officer

Tyson Sharp brings over a decade of experience in accounting and finance to his role as Chief Financial Officer at ACES Delta. He began his career as an Accounting Manager for a law firm, followed by three years in public accounting specializing in tax. Tyson then spent five years in accounting roles within the renewable energy sector before joining Magnum Development in June 2021 as Controller.

His experience in energy storage and project finance positioned him well for leadership as CFO, where he now oversees financial strategy, capital planning, and risk management.

Tyson holds both a B.S. and a Master’s in Accounting from the University of Utah, reflecting his strong foundation in financial principles and commitment to excellence.

POWERING CHANGE TOGETHER

The world is at a critical juncture in our energy transition. Renewables are becoming more available and affordable, but we must find ways to solve the intermittency challenges of these resources and enable them to fuel industries outside of the power sector. At ACES Delta, we work with organizations across all sectors — utilities, developers, companies, industry experts, and governments — to move the boundaries of renewable energy and provide reliable lower carbon solutions at scale.

ACES Delta is your trusted collaborator in energy. Our expertise, innovation, and proven technology are transforming how people think about renewable energy for today and for our future.
