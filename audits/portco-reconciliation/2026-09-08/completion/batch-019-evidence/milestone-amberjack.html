Amberjack Capital Partners Completes the Sale of Milestone Environmental Services to SK Capital Partners

Amberjack Capital Partners is pleased to announce the sale of Milestone Environmental Services, a portfolio company of Amberjack Capital Fund I, to SK Capital Partners.
October 13, 2023

Milestone was founded in 2014 via a partnership between the Company’s executive management team and Amberjack to pioneer a proprietary and environmentally superior method of disposing mission-critical and challenging energy waste streams.  Since inception, Milestone has grown into the leading independent energy waste management platform throughout the United States’ most productive energy regions.  The Company provides a secure means for its customers to permanently sequester hydrocarbon-containing energy waste, thereby preventing greenhouse gas emissions and groundwater contamination.  More recently, the Company created a new business segment, Milestone Carbon, and emerged as a leader in permanent carbon sequestration services.  Leveraging the Company’s core competencies in energy waste sequestration, Milestone Carbon is focused on developing and operating injection sites that permanently sequester CO2 to enable a broad range of emitters to achieve their decarbonization goals.

The team at Amberjack could not be prouder of the Milestone team for building the innovative and market-leading company Milestone has become.  We are grateful to the Company’s management team for the many years of partnership and we commend CEO Gabriel Rio and CFO Frank Schageman for their stewardship of the business since the Company’s founding.  Amberjack is honored to have supported the Company during its formative years of development and looks forward to the Company’s continued growth and development.

About Milestone Environmental Services

Milestone Environmental Services, headquartered in Houston, Texas, is a leading environmental services and carbon management company and the largest independent energy waste sequestration company in the United States.  Milestone operates a leading integrated network of waste infrastructure that reduces the carbon impact of its customers by permanently sequestering hydrocarbon waste over a mile below the surface, preventing greenhouse gas emissions and water and ground contamination.  Since 2014, Milestone has sequestered more than 2 million tons of CO2e through its injection process.  Its subsidiary, Milestone Carbon, is devoted to helping multiple types of emitters meet their decarbonization goals by developing and operating injection sites that permanently and securely sequester CO2 below the surface.  For more information, please visit www.milestone-es.com.

About Amberjack Capital Partners

Amberjack Capital is a specialized private equity firm that invests in and partners with entrepreneurs and business owners to build market leaders serving the industrial, infrastructure and environmental end markets.  Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the firm has raised $1.9 billion of committed capital since its inception in 2006 and has invested in over 50 companies.
