Pocahontas Parkway
USA
Visit website

Toll road located southeast of Richmond, Virginia (USA) that connects Interstate 95 and State Route 150 and the Richmond Regional Airport. It is the only immediate crossing of the James River, being a solution for saving travel time and distance. From Globalvia we are in charge of the financing, development, operation and maintenance of Pocahontas Parkway, as well as the construction and subsequent maintenance of the connection with the Richmond airport.
