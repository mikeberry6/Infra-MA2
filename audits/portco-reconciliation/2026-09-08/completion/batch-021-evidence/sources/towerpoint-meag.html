15.12.2025MEAG finances two major electric train fleet projects for regional rail networks in Germany

MEAG, acting on behalf of Munich Re and other third-party institutional investors, has signed long-term financing agreements for two major projects that advance the decarbonization and modernization of Germany’s regional rail transport.

The first transaction supports the procurement of Stadler FLIRT Akku battery-electric multiple units for deployment in the Mittelthüringesches Akkunetz regional rail network in Thuringia. These trains will operate on partially electrified and non-electrified routes around Erfurt, replacing diesel-powered trains and offering low-emission mobility, improved accessibility, energy efficiency, and passenger comfort.

The second transaction finances the acquisition of a fleet of new Mireo electric multiple units manufactured by Siemens Mobility GmbH. The trains will be deployed in the “Elektronetz Nord-Magdeburg” regional rail network serving the federal states of Saxony-Anhalt, Lower Saxony, and Brandenburg.

Both fleets are scheduled to enter service in 2028 and will be initially operated by DB Regio AG under long-term operating leases. The combined financing volume provided by MEAG managed investors for both projects is approximately €450m. Both transactions are structured as long-term, fixed-rate infrastructure debt instruments, with MEAG acting as sole arranger. The closings showcase MEAG’s leading role as arranger of rolling stock financings in the German regional passenger market.

Benjamin Hemming, Head of Illiquid Assets Debt, MEAG: "Battery-electric and electric trains are key enablers of sustainable transport on regional networks. We thank our partners for the excellent collaboration and are proud to contribute to the modernization of Germany’s public transport system."

Işıl Tanrıverdi Versmissen, Head of Infrastructure Debt Transactions, MEAG: "As a long-term infrastructure investor, MEAG is committed to financing projects that combine environmental benefits with reliable returns. These transactions underline our expertise in structuring complex rolling stock financings in Germany and beyond."

1.8.2025MEAG provides long-term financing for Portugal’s high-speed rail line between Lisbon and Porto

  • MEAG, on behalf of Munich Re and further institutional investors, participates in the financing of Portugal’s first high-speed rail line, a major infrastructure project connecting Lisbon and Porto.
  • The project is delivered under a 30-year concession awarded to the Portuguese consortium Avan Norte, with public support from Infraestruturas de Portugal and co-financing from the European Investment Bank and InvestEU.
  • The new rail line will cut travel times to just over one hour, enhance regional connectivity, and significantly reduce transport emissions – supporting EU climate and mobility goals.

MEAG, acting on behalf of Munich Re and further institutional investors, has successfully signed a long-term financing agreement to support the construction of the first section of Portugal’s high-speed rail line connecting Lisbon and Porto.

The financing supports Phase 1 of the new high-speed railway, beginning with the 71-kilometre Porto–Oiã section. This stretch will be delivered via a public-private partnership (PPP) under a 30-year concession granted by Infraestruturas de Portugal (IP) to the Avan Norte consortium, formed by leading Portuguese construction firms.

The high-speed line marks the largest infrastructure investment in Portugal in recent decades. Once complete, it will reduce travel time between the country’s two largest cities to just over one hour, improve regional connectivity, and significantly cut transport emissions – supporting national and EU climate goals.

MEAG’s investment complements a broader funding package including the European Investment Bank, InvestEU, European grants, and other private sector lenders. The project benefits from strong public support and is backed by a robust PPP framework.

Benjamin Hemming, Head of Illiquid Asset Debt, MEAG: "We are proud to support this nationally significant and future-oriented transport project. The investment aligns well with our long-term infrastructure strategy and enables our clients to participate in sustainable economic development. It also demonstrates the strong track record of Portuguese infrastructure developers and institutions in delivering complex projects."

Işıl Tanrıverdi Versmissen, Head of Infrastructure Debt Transactions, MEAG: "The Lisbon-Porto high-speed line is a transformative initiative. We are pleased to contribute to its financing alongside experienced partners and thank all stakeholders for their trust and excellent collaboration throughout the process."

15.07.2025MEAG acts as sole arranger for a €120m bond structure to PAUL Tech AG

  • MEAG managed investors provide €120m in long-term financing to PAUL Tech AG to support the nationwide roll-out of its Heating-as-a-Service model, PAUL Net Zero.
  • The solution combines AI-driven energy optimisation with heat pumps to improve energy efficiency in existing residential buildings — without upfront investment from property owners.
  • The financing is structured as amortising project bonds with a maturity of up to 10 years, disbursed in instalments tied to clearly defined performance milestones.

MEAG managed investors provide €120m financing to the prop-tech company PAUL Tech AG. The financing backs the national roll-out of the company’s heating-as-a-service model “PAUL Net Zero”. Funds will be disbursed in multiple instalments, each contingent on meeting specific contractual conditions. The bonds issued under the structure are amortising over up to 10 years.

PAUL Net Zero combines energy optimisation through an adaptive hydraulic balancing and a highly efficient heat pump systems, specifically designed for older multi-dwelling residential buildings. Property owners benefit from a solution that requires no upfront investment and significantly improves the energy efficiency of their properties.

Işıl Tanrıverdi Versmissen, Head of Infrastructure Debt Transactions, MEAG: “We are pleased to support PAUL Tech’s continued growth with this innovative financing structure. We are particularly impressed by the company's ability to significantly improve the energy efficiency of existing buildings, including older stock. Our sincere thanks go to the PAUL Tech team for the excellent collaboration, as well as to our advisors for their support throughout the process.”

Benjamin Hemming, Head of Illiquid Asset Debt, MEAG: “We are delighted to offer our investors access to a new and promising segment within the infrastructure space. Heating-as-a-Service is poised to become an integral component of the broader energy transition. The granular structure of the underlying contracts ensures a stable and well-diversified credit exposure.”

4.6.2025MEAG acted as sole arranger for approximately €300m in financing for Spain's largest hybrid renewable energy project

We are pleased to announce that we have provided financing totaling approximately €300m for the Gecama Hybridisation Project in Spain, a pioneering renewable energy project that will integrate wind, solar and battery energy storage facilities to support the delivery of electricity at any time.

The project, developed by Enlight, will have a combined capacity of 554 MW and 220 MWh, making it Spain's largest hybrid renewable energy complex. BNPP acts as Enlight’s exclusive financial advisor and the financing is structured as a bond issuance for institutional investors, with maturity dates in 2045 and 2046. DLA Piper acts as legal advisor to Enlight and Linklaters as the legal adviser to MEAG and the bondholders.

This transaction marks a significant milestone in the development of Spain's renewable energy sector, and we are proud to have played a role in supporting this innovative project. The Gecama Hybridisation Project will not only provide renewable energy to the grid but also enhance power supply stability and contribute to Spain's national plan to combat climate change.

The hybrid project will deliver renewable energy around the clock by combining technologies that generate electricity at different times of day with a battery storage system that enables optimized use of energy resources. The storage system will support peak shifting - storing electricity when prices are low and discharging during periods of high demand - thereby enhancing the project’s profitability. It will also provide essential grid services, such as frequency response, helping to stabilize the power system through rapid charge and discharge capabilities.

Benjamin Hemming, MEAG’s Head of Illiquid Assets Debt: "We are thrilled to have supported Enlight in this groundbreaking project, which showcases the potential for hybrid renewable energy solutions to transform the way we generate and consume energy. The Gecama Hybridisation Project is a testament to the innovative spirit of our partners and the growing demand for sustainable energy solutions. We are proud to have worked alongside Enlight and other stakeholders to bring this project to life, and we look forward to seeing its impact on Spain's energy landscape."

8.5.2025MEAG finances Parmaco’s dynamic building portfolio

MEAG is participating in the debt refinancing of Parmaco, a provider of dynamic buildings with modular structure for educational, healthcare, and other public services in the Nordics, together with a consortium of leading banks and institutional investors. MEAG is thereby the cornerstone debt investor with a three-digit million-euro stake funded by several Munich Re entities and a separately managed account in the debt financing totaling ca. €700m.

Parmaco designs, builds, owns and rents high-quality modular buildings. The buildings are rented to highly rated public sector customers in Finland and Sweden for both temporary and permanent use. Parmaco’s portfolio is mainly used for educational, healthcare and other public services based on a dynamic, circular ‘space-as-a-service’ model. Within the transaction parameter, Parmaco has currently over 300 lease contracts in place with public sector customers, providing home for around 38,000 children in school and day cares across Finland and Sweden.

Dynamic buildings represent an innovative and sustainable approach to construction and operation. They promote efficient use of space by being scalable and adaptable as required and can be flexibly repurposed and relocated. They further use standardized building materials and generate less waste both during construction and at the end of their service life. These characteristics are well-suited to the Nordic market with its high demand for sustainable, fit-for-purpose, immediate building solutions – driven by population volatility (urbanization, demographics) and renovation need of the aging educational and healthcare building stock.

While retaining high-quality aspects (incl. fire safety, sound proofness, air ventilation, triple glazed windows) of ‘traditional’ buildings made of concrete, Parmaco’s modular buildings have a low carbon footprint in manufacturing through wood and (incoming) CO2-free steel building materials, while ensuring a high level of energy-efficiency once in operation.

Benjamin Hemming, Head of Illiquid Assets Debt at MEAG: “MEAG builds on its long-lasting successful relationship with Partners Group across various alternative asset classes. As one of the leading infrastructure debt asset managers in Europe, we are proud to underpin our contribution to social infrastructure projects with our strong participation in this refinancing.”

Isil Tanriverdi Versmissen, Head of Infrastructure Debt Transactions: “We are delighted to support the refinancing of Parmaco, a leading provider of innovative modular buildings in the Nordics. I'm proud of our team's efforts in bringing this complex transaction to a close. This achievement is a testament to our team's dedication, expertise, and commitment to supporting sustainable and impactful projects.”

About Parmaco

Parmaco, which is supported by Partners Group, is a social infrastructure company providing high-quality, cost- and energy-efficient modular buildings for educational and care services. Its business focus is on medium-to-long-term dynamic buildings as a circular economy (‘space-as-a-service’) model. With roots deep in Finland, Parmaco’s journey as a pioneer of dynamic sustainable spaces began in the 1960s and has become the market leader in Finland.

Parmaco’s headquarter is in Tampere, Finland, with further offices in Espoo, Finland, and in Stockholm, Sweden. The production facilities are split across Finland: Leppävirta, Pyhäjoki, Hämeenlinna, as well as Varkaus. The company employs 250 people.

7.4.2025Stor-Skälsjön wind farm handed over to MEAG

Stor-Skälsjön wind farm has been handed over to the owners MEAG (75 %) and Hydro Rein (25 %). Stor-Skälsjön wind farm is located in Sundsvall and Timrå municipalities in northern Sweden (SE2) and comprises 42 turbines (260 MW). The expected annual production of 800 GWh is handled commercially by Hydro Energy.

“We have set out to be a leading provider of renewable energy to assist industrials in the Nordics with their decarbonization efforts. Reaching full commercial operation marks an important milestone for Hydro Rein. With the Stor-Skälsjön wind farm development we have also proven our strong commitment to sustainability and stakeholder engagement,” said Head of Hydro Rein in Europe, Lisa Haukaas.

Philipp Deisler, Head of Infrastructure Equity Asset Management at MEAG: "We are delighted to announce the take-over of Stor-Skälsjön wind farm, one of our key wind power projects in Sweden. This strategic investment further strengthens the stability and resilience of our 1.8 GWp renewable energy portfolio, which is an integral part of our Infrastructure Equity portfolio. As a major investor in clean energy, we are proud to support the transition to a lower-emission future. We would like to extend our sincere gratitude to Eolus and Hydro Rein for the collaborative spirit and trust throughout this project, and we look forward to our continued partnership."

Stor-Skälsjön has been jointly constructed by Eolus, who divested its 51 percent share of the project in 2022, and Hydro Rein, under a Construction Management agreement. In 2022, the construction of roads, crane pads, foundations, and the internal electrical grid started. In 2023, Siemens Gamesa began installing the wind turbines, and all turbines were erected by November the same year. Electricity production at Stor-Skälsjön started in September 2023. Since then, it has gradually increased.

Engaging with local stakeholders, such as Sami, hunting teams and cultural associations was prioritized during the development and construction of the wind farm. The first local community support grants have already been given out according to a model that was established, which will run through the lifetime of the wind farm. Among the first beneficiaries were a local school, community center and sports and outdoor associations.

Telenor Infra AS and Hydro Extrusion Sweden have signed power purchase agreements (PPA) with Hydro Energy for a supply from Stor-Skälsjön. Eolus will provide technical, operational, and administrative services for the wind farm on a 15-year contract.

Stor-Skälsjön is the first wind farm co-developed by Hydro Rein reaching full commercial operation in Sweden, where Hydro Rein has more than 30 wind farm projects under development.

 

About Hydro Rein

Hydro Rein is a leading provider of renewable energy solutions to industrials, co-owned by the renewable energy and aluminium company Hydro and infrastructure investor Macquarie Asset Management. The company has a diversified portfolio of more than 60 wind, solar and energy efficiency projects under development in core markets in the Nordics and Brazil. After three years of growth in renewable energy, Hydro Rein has a gross capacity of 8.4 GW in its portfolio and long-term contracts signed for a production of 5.3 TWh per year.
