About us

Yusen Terminals LLC (YTI) was established in 1991 and operates a conventional marine container terminal located in the Port of Los Angeles at Berths 212-218. Our services include stevedoring and terminal operations for the Premier Alliance (ONE, HMM, and Yang Ming), Wan Hai, and Hapag Lloyd, as well as slot charter services for Evergreen, CMA-CGM, APL, COSCO, and OOCL. As a full-service marine terminal, YTI utilizes modern equipment, offering ship-to-shore container handling, on-dock rail services, storage facilities, and Alternative Maritime Power (AMP) services to our customers. We currently operate ten Post Panamax Gantry cranes, including six state-of-the-art Super Post Panamax Gantry cranes. Our highly experienced management team is dedicated to efficient cargo handling with a focus on service delivery and customer satisfaction. YTI is committed to providing its customers with reliable “Best-in-Class” service delivery as well as a complete suite of marine terminal services to the shipping and transportation community. Yusen Terminals LLC is wholly owned by Ocean Network Express (ONE), with its business management company headquarters located in Singapore.

    Congratulations to Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of The Pacific Companies, Joseph S. Gregorio, on being named a 2026 Admiral of the Ocean Sea (AOTOS) Award honoree by the United Seamen's Service (USS). Widely regarded as the most prestigious honor in the American maritime industry, the AOTOS Award recognizes leaders whose contributions have strengthened the U.S.-flag maritime industry. USS promotes the welfare of American seafarers and their families, seafarers of all nations, and U.S. military personnel through a broad range of programs and services. Mr. Gregorio has has spent nearly 50 years building a career defined by entrepreneurial vision and a commitment to the International Longshore and Warehouse Union (ILWU), making him a fitting recipient of this distinguished honor. Mr. Gregorio will be recognized at the 57th annual AOTOS Awards Gala on November 13, 2026, at the Sheraton New York Times Square Hotel, alongside fellow honorees Robert F. Sappio, Chief Executive Officer of SeaCube Container Leasing, and David Kim, CEO of Hanwha Philly Shipyard. For more information, visit www.uss-aotos.org.
