Last Updated: June 2026


Helix Digital Infrastructure LLC (“Helix,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates this website. Helix is a wholly owned subsidiary of KKR & Co. Inc. (“KKR”).

Helix relies on KKR to provide certain services, including technology infrastructure, website hosting, analytics, and other operational support, pursuant to a shared services arrangement.

As a result:

  • Personal data collected through this website may be processed by Helix and KKR
  • In certain cases, Helix and KKR may act as independent controllers or joint controllers, depending on the processing activity

Helix’s website generally follows the same approach to data collection and use as KKR’s websites, including:

  • Collection of technical and usage data (e.g., IP address, browsing behavior)

For a detailed description of the categories of data collected, purposes of processing, and legal bases (where applicable), please refer to KKR’s Privacy & Cookies Notice (linked below).


Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This website uses cookies and similar technologies. A cookie consent mechanism is implemented where required by law.

For more information about the types of cookies used, their purposes, and your choices, please see KKR’s Cookie Notice.


Data Sharing

Personal data collected via this website may be shared:

International Data Transfers

Helix operates globally. Personal data may be processed in the United States and other jurisdictions where KKR or its service providers operate, in accordance with applicable law.


Your Rights
