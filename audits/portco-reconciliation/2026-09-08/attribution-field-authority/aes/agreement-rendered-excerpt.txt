SECTION 4.10 Ownership of Company Common Stock. None of Parent, Merger Sub or any of their respective “affiliates” or “associates” (in each case as such terms are defined in Section 203 of the DGCL) is or has been at any time during the three (3) years prior to the date of this Agreement an “interested stockholder” of the Company (as defined in Section 203 of the DGCL). Neither Parent nor any Affiliate of Parent “beneficially owns” (as such term is defined for purposes of Section 13(d) of the Exchange Act) any shares of Company Common Stock or other Equity Securities.
