OHA Blocker and OHA Aggregator

        OHA Blocker and OHA Aggregator (collectively, “OHA Member”) are special purpose
vehicles formed and wholly owned by Oak Hill Advisors, L.P. (“OHA”), to hold 100% of
OHA’s indirect interests through units held upstream of CVEC. OHA is a wholly owned
subsidiary of T. Rowe Price Group, Inc. (“TRPG”). 3 To the best of OHA’s knowledge, as of
September 30, 2025, neither TRPG nor any of its asset management subsidiaries, nor any of its
managed investment funds, own, directly or indirectly, 10% or more of the voting securities of
any company that has direct or indirect ownership or controls interests in electric generation or
transmission assets, intrastate natural gas transportation assets, intrastate natural gas storage,
distribution facilities, or physical coal supplies or access to transportation of coal supplies in
New York.

Federal Energy Regulatory Commission has ruled that such ownership is non-controlling. 4

networks and intrastate pipelines, including within the PJM states of Delaware, Maryland, and
Ohio.

Kiwoom US

        Neither Kiwoom US nor any of its affiliates owns, operates, or controls any electric
generation facilities, electric transmission facilities (aside from, limited and discrete

CVEC

       CVEC is a limited liability company formed under the New York Limited Liability
Company Law. The Commission granted CVEC a Certificate of Public Convenience and
Necessity for the construction of an approximately 1,000 MW combined cycle, natural gas-
powered electric generating facility in the Town of Dover, New York. 5 Other than the CVEC
Partners, no other entities hold a direct or indirect ownership or controlling interest greater than
10% of CVEC. The electric generating facilities and other energy-related assets affiliated with
CVEC are identified above.
