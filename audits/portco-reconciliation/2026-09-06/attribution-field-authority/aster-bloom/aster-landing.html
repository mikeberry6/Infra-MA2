JANUARY 8, 2020 – AGECARE AND AXIUM INFRASTRUCTURE FORM A PARTNERSHIP TO SHARE OWNERSHIP OF FIVE CONTINUING CARE FACILITIES IN ALBERTA

Calgary – Axium Infrastructure (“Axium”) is pleased to announce that it has entered into a partnership with Age Care Health Services Inc. (“AgeCare”) to share ownership of five of AgeCare’s continuing care facilities in Alberta. The portfolio is comprised of 1,402 beds and includes both long-term care and designated supportive living services.
