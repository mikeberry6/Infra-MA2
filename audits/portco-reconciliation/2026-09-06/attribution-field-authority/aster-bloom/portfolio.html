Passiflore Wind Portfolio

Passiflore Wind Portfolio

Energy

Asset Type
Energy Infrastructure
Status
Operational (COD between August 2015 and July 2022)
Asset Description
174.2 MW operational portfolio of 22 wind projects located in the French regions of Hauts-de-France (13 projects / 79.4 MW), Nouvelle‑Aquitaine (5 projects / 52.8 MW), and Grand Est (4 projects / 42.0 MW).
Asset Revenues
The Portfolio revenues are secured by French government backed Feed-in-Tariff (FiT) and Contract-for-Difference (CfD) schemes. These contracts provide an effectively fixed tariff on 100% of the energy produced by each project, with annual inflation-linked indexation. Under FiT contracts, 60% of the tariff is indexed to inflation, while under CfD contracts, 30% of the strike price is indexed. Contract durations range from 15 to 20 years from COD and are administered by EDF OA, whose payment obligations are fully backed by the Government of France (rated Aa3 by Moody’s).
