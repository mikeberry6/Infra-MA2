Keep `DataBank` / `DataBank Holdings, Ltd.` as one company. Add the omitted current manager-universe owners Northleaf Capital Partners, Ardian and CBRE Investment Management; correct Swiss Life to Swiss Life Asset Managers; retain DigitalBridge, IMCO and AustralianSuper. TIAA, EDF Invest and TJC are current co-owner context outside the supplied manager universe.

The January 2025 secondary reduced DigitalBridge but did not eliminate it. Because the public announcement did not identify every seller, no other owner is retired by inference. No full sale or signed pending exit was found through August 18, 2026.

Primary evidence: DataBank's live investor page. Supporting evidence includes the 2016 acquisition, 2020 secondary, 2022 and 2023 recapitalizations, 2024 AustralianSuper raise, 2025 secondary, current TIAA SEC filing and May 2026 company update.
