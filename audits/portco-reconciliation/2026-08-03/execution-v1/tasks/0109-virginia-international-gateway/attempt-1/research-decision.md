# Virginia International Gateway - proposed new canonical company

Create one active **Virginia International Gateway** record. Preserve **Astatine Investment Partners** as the current manager attribution, add **PSP Investments** as a directly evidenced current owner association, and retain the separate **Alinda Infrastructure Fund II** period as realized in 2019.

Astatine's site lists VIG as Active and separately identifies its Fund II investment as exited in 2019. The Virginia Port Authority's August 2025 final official statement says the underlying current owners are USS and PSP. This supports one company with overlapping manager and underlying-owner relationships rather than duplicate active and exited companies.

Virginia International Gateway, VIG, the VIG Terminal and APM Terminals Virginia resolve to one canonical company. The Virginia Port Authority is the lessee, Virginia International Terminals is the operator, and lease interests, bonds, cranes and expansion projects remain inside the company boundary.

Nonblocking disclosure limits: the active Astatine fund or managed-account vehicle, manager-level stake and entry date are not public; PSP's entry date and percentage are also not public. USS is named in the narrative because it is outside the supplied 100-manager universe.
