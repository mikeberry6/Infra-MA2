# Hill Top Energy Center — proposed correction

Direct FERC evidence shows that Blackstone's acquisition closed on March 26, 2026. The existing company should remain one canonical Hill Top record, with Blackstone current from the close and Ardian recorded as the former owner from July 2019 through the close. The queued pending-transaction action is obsolete.

- Current owner: Blackstone through affiliated vehicles, including ETP IV AIV and BXE IV; Hill Top is indirectly wholly owned by the Blackstone funds.
- Former owner: Ardian Americas Infrastructure Funds, 41.9% from July 2019 and 100% from April 2025 until March 26, 2026.
- Operating asset: 620 MW natural-gas combined-cycle plant in Carmichaels, Pennsylvania, operating in PJM since July 2021.
- Exit check: no later sale or signed pending transfer found through August 15, 2026.
- Primary evidence: the direct FERC consummation notice, accession 20260429-5359.
