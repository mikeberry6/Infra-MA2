# Waihonu Solar Farm — proposed correction

Archive the standalone Waihonu record without a redirect, retire Argo's ownership period in 2025, and retain Hawaiʻi Gas as the manager-level Argo/APG PortCo.

Waihonu is the 6.5 MW Waihonu North and South solar project, not a separate manager-level platform. Argo acquired it with Hawaiʻi Gas on July 21, 2022. Hawaiʻi Gas now says the project was acquired in 2025 by a solar energy provider; EIA's 2025 filing identifies ERCAM LLC as operator/reporting utility for both plants and contains no separate owner row. The plants remain operational, but ERCAM is not supported as an infrastructure-fund manager. The exact 2025 closing date, price and ERCAM parent are not publicly disclosed and do not block the list correction.

No redirect is proposed because Waihonu and Hawaiʻi Gas are distinct identities. The archived project and former Argo ownership period remain available for audit history.
