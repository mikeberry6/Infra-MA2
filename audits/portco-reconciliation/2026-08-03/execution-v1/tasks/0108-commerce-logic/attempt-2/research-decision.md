# Commerce Logic — exclusion decision

**Decision:** Exclude Commerce Logic from the current PortCo list; make no production, seed, ownership, pending-transaction or redirect change.

Astatine's Fund III retained Commerce Logic after CVO's 2022 divestiture, but Fleetworthy announced its acquisition of Commerce Logic's operating assets on July 15, 2025 and integrated the technology into Bestpass. That later transaction supersedes Astatine's stale “Active” page for current-ownership purposes.

Commerce Logic, LLC is the historical company; Tollogic is its product, CVO is its former parent, and Fleetworthy/Bestpass are the acquirer and integration platform. None should be created as a duplicate Commerce Logic PortCo.

Primary evidence: https://fleetworthy.com/resources/news/fleetworthy-commercelogic-acquisition/
