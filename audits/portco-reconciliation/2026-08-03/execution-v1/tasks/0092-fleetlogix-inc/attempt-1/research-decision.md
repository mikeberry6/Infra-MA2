# FleetLogix, Inc. — Phase 1 decision

**Decision:** Proposed correction. Archive FleetLogix from the standalone manager-level PortCo list while preserving it as an active LAZ-controlled operating company.

LAZ acquired a majority interest in FleetLogix in 2025, and FleetLogix continues to operate under the “FleetLogix Powered by LAZ” brand. Argo’s directly evidenced infrastructure platform is LAZ Parking. FleetLogix is a bolt-on beneath LAZ, not another legal name for LAZ, so the safe correction is archival without a company merge or redirect.

The protected after-image will retain one Argo audit period as indirect through LAZ, retire the unsupported duplicate Argo row, preserve the existing LAZ acquisition milestone, and record no Argo exit or pending transaction. The precise LAZ stake, FleetLogix closing date, Argo indirect percentage and fund vehicle are not publicly disclosed.

Primary evidence: [LAZ Parking’s FleetLogix acquisition announcement](https://www.lazparking.com/our-company/about/news/2025/04/22/laz-parking-acquires-majority-interest-in-fleet-management-staffing-services-provider-fleetlogix).
