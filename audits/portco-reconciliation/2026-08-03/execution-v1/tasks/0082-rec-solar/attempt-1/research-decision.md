# REC Solar / REC Power reconciliation decision

**Decision:** Correct the existing company in place; do not add another owner.

REC Solar formally rebranded as **REC Power** on June 30, 2026. The same production company ID remains canonical, with REC Solar and supported historical or legal names retained as aliases. The acquired Duke commercial distributed-generation portfolio is part of the REC Power platform; individual projects and intermediate holding entities are not separate PortCos.

The census label `ArcLight Capital` resolves to the existing `ArcLight Capital Partners` organization, so the queued `ADD_OWNER` action would duplicate the existing owner. Keep one active ownership period, normalize the fund to `ArcLight Energy Partners Fund VII, L.P.`, clear the unsupported legal vehicle, and leave the platform stake undisclosed. Duke's October 4, 2023 closing remains the supported entry year.

Update the website, San Luis Obispo headquarters, distributed-power description and milestones. Remove the unsupported 2025 scale date; add the June 2026 rebrand and July 2026 operating-portfolio acquisition. No REC Power-level sale or signed pending exit was identified through August 15, 2026.
