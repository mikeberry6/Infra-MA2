# Pike County Light & Power — Phase 1 decision

**EXCLUDED — HIGH confidence.** Pike County Light & Power is a regulated operating subsidiary of Corning Energy Corporation, not a separate manager-level Argo investment. The standalone Pike production and seed identity should be consolidated into one canonical Corning Energy record, with the Pike name retained as an alias and subsidiary context.

Argo acquired 100% of the Corning platform on July 6, 2022 through the ACP Crotona chain. Argo therefore remains the current owner, and no pending ownership transaction should be published.
