Operations

Crescent Midstream is a net carbon neutral midstream company providing the most competitive and reliable option for producers and shippers in the U.S. Gulf Coast to transport their crude oil, operating through three segments: Crescent Gulf (Offshore pipelines), Crescent Louisiana Midstream (Onshore pipelines) and Cameron Highway Oil Pipeline System (CHOPS). Crescent owns over 1,200 miles of pipelines, both onshore and offshore, that transport nearly 200 million barrels of crude oil each year on behalf of its customers. Our unique asset footprint, operating expertise, and fee-based commercial approach enable us to work quickly to expand our services and create value for customers and stakeholders.
