Michael Coleman
Managing Director
New York

Michael Coleman is a Managing Director at Wafra where he focuses on real assets and infrastructure investments. He is responsible for all aspects of the investment process, including origination, transaction execution and asset management.

Michael currently serves on the boards for Ascension FBO Network, the Hinckley Company, Intermountain Infrastructure Group, Beyond Data Centers, Mission Clean Energy, and Crescent Louisiana Midstream.

Previously, he worked at American Infrastructure Funds where he focused on middle market infrastructure strategies across various industries. Mr. Coleman started his career with the Energy & Power investment banking team at Wells Fargo Securities and earned a BA in Economics from Yale University.
