# Review

**Decision:** `PROPOSED_CORRECTION`.

Phoenix Renewables remains an active, in-scope ArcLight Fund VIII operating-renewables portfolio. It is distinct from SkyVest Renewables, which performs management and operating functions, and from Thunderbird Renewables, Mesquite Sky and any underlying Phoenix projects.

The existing ArcLight Capital Partners ownership row should be retained. **No second owner should be added** for the shortened `ArcLight Capital` label.

Two list-level corrections are required:

- Change `investmentYear` / `entryYear` from **2024** to **null / `NOT_PUBLICLY_DISCLOSED`**. The July 2024 announcement proves the SkyVest management relationship and Fund VIII association, not Phoenix's acquisition or closing date.
- Change headquarters from **United States** to **null**. The United States country classification is supported, but a specific headquarters is not disclosed.

ArcLight's current investments page provides direct evidence that Phoenix remained an active Fund VIII investment as of August 14, 2026. No reviewed direct source disclosed a sale, exit, superseding rename or signed pending ownership transaction.
