# Phoenix Renewables reconciliation decision

**Decision:** Correct the existing Phoenix Renewables record; do not add another owner.

ArcLight's current portfolio materials identify Phoenix as a current Fund VIII operating-renewables portfolio managed through SkyVest. `ArcLight Capital` is the census's shortened label for the existing `ArcLight Capital Partners` organization, so the queued owner addition would duplicate the ownership period already in production.

Two ownership-list claims lack direct support. The July 2024 SkyVest launch does not prove that ArcLight acquired Phoenix in 2024, and no reviewed source discloses a Phoenix headquarters. Set both `investmentYear` and `headquarters` to null. Retain the U.S. country classification, Fund VIII vehicle and `CLOSED_ACTIVE` state.

The historical wording is also tightened: ArcLight's current ESG report supports establishment of Phoenix under SkyVest and reports 467 MW, while the July 2024 release supports SkyVest's launch but does not itself name Phoenix. No Phoenix-level sale or pending exit was identified through August 14, 2026.

Primary evidence: <https://arclight.com/portfolio-services/>
