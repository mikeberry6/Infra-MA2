# OnPoint Energy - List Reconciliation Decision

**Decision:** Exclude  
**Confidence:** High  
**As of:** August 14, 2026

OnPoint Energy resolves to ArcLight's Houston-based retail-electricity platform, not a generation fleet or another physical-infrastructure operator. ArcLight still lists OnPoint as a current Power investment, but direct regulatory records show that OnPoint sold its Texas customer contracts to Good Charlie, completed the transfer of all customers in February 2024, ceased retail-electricity service, cancelled its remaining federal market-based-rate tariff, and had Texas REP certificate 10326 revoked in November 2025.

The correct list outcome is therefore **EXCLUDED** as a non-operating wind-down with no demonstrated direct infrastructure assets. This does not assert that ArcLight completed a parent-level exit: the evidence supports possible residual legal ownership, and no parent-company sale or dissolution was found.

No production company, seed record, ownership period, redirect, or Deal Database record will be created or changed. The immutable census holding and this decision remain as the audit lineage.
