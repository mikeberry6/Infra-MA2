# Orca reconciliation decision

**Decision:** Create one canonical `Orca Acquisitions, LLC` manager-level power platform.

ArcLight's live portfolio and 2025 ESG report identify Orca as a current Fund VI power investment. Official FERC filings establish the legal chain from ArcLight Fund VI through ArcLight Orca Holdings and Orca Acquisitions to Walleye Power, while the current 2026 filing confirms Walleye still owns and operates the Ohio generating units. Olympus identifies Orca as its joint venture with ArcLight.

ArcLight owns 95% of Orca's **Class A managing interests** and controls day-to-day decisions. That figure is not a disclosed 95% total economic stake because Olympus affiliates also hold the remaining Class A interests and all Class B interests. The exact overall economic split is not publicly disclosed.

Count Orca once. Walleye Energy Holdings, Walleye Power, Bay Shore and the individual units remain below the canonical platform boundary. No Orca-specific sale or pending change of control was identified through August 14, 2026.
