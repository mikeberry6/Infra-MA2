## Recommendation

Create one Aerostar Airport Holdings, LLC record for the private operator of Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport. The Puerto Rico Ports Authority retains the airport asset, while Aerostar holds the long-term operating lease. ASUR owns 60% and PSP Investments owns 40% through AviAlliance; the Highstar/Oaktree interests exited on May 26, 2017. No later Aerostar ownership transfer or signed pending sale was found through September 1, 2026.
