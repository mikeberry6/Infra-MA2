Edward Tsai
Managing Director
New York

Edward Tsai is a Managing Director and member of the Management Committee at Wafra.  Mr. Tsai is the Head of Real Assets and Infrastructure and is responsible for leading investment activities within the real assets and infrastructure business as well as portfolio oversight and management.

Mr. Tsai joined Wafra in 2014 and currently serves on the boards of Contract Leasing Corporation, Aquila Air Capital, The Hinckley Company, Signal Rail Holdings and American Inland Marine Holdings.  He formerly served on the board of Global Container International.

Mr. Tsai earned an AB from Georgetown University and a JD from New York University School of Law.
