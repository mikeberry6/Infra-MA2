# TramCité — research decision

**Decision:** EXCLUDED (HIGH confidence)

TramCité is a public Québec City tramway project, not a CDPQ PortCo. Québec is project owner; Québec City and the RTC are the intended asset owners. CDPQ Infra and wholly owned Infra CITÉ Inc. are State mandataries serving as project manager/procurement lead and prospective maintainer. Although the framework contemplates future CDPQ Infra financing and return, realization requires Government approval and a future definitive agreement. No direct project equity, concession, definitive investment commitment, financial close or private ownership transfer had closed.

This is a research artifact only and does not mutate the database, seed, scorecard, or release state.
