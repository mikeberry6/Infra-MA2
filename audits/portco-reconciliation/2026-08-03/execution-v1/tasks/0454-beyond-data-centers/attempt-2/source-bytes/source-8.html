JSA Podcasts | Telecom, Digital Infrastructure and Data Center News, Insights + More

From onsite coverage and exclusive interviews at global telecom and data center industry conferences to special feature mini-series that showcase digital infrastructure trends, JSA Podcasts delivers content that can’t be found anywhere else.


Programs include:

>> JSA TV Live: Onsite Conference Coverage - JSA TV Live broadcasts from the expo floors of premier digital infrastructure conferences around the world – delivering event highlights and exclusive onsite interviews.

 

>> JSA Fast Forward: News Wrap-Up - A monthly review of the month's top headlines in digital infrastructure, tech, telecom and data centers – from product announcements and new hires to the latest on top industry conferences.


>> Know Before You Go: A Conference Briefing - The show that covers everything attendees should have on their radar – from insider talking points to conference logistics – before attending that next big industry event.


>> DATA MOVERS: The Digital Future - JSA's newest series focuses on the next generation moving into the digital infrastructure industry — the future of our sector. How do we attract, retain, and engage the brightest minds in the world, generating excitement about one of the most critical, influential industries on the planet? How do we diversify the industry while ensuring a sustainable influx of talent — from the boardroom to the data center hall?
