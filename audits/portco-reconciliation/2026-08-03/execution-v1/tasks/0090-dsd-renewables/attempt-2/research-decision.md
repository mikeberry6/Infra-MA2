# DSD Renewables — proposed correction

DSD remains one current U.S. distributed-energy platform. Correct its ownership to show Ares as majority owner, BlackRock and Cox as retained minority owners, and GE Renewable Energy as the former owner. GIP is not a DSD owner, and reported sale exploration does not qualify as a signed pending exit.

- Former owner: GE Renewable Energy, which retained 20% after the 2019 spinout and exited when BlackRock acquired the remaining interest on November 10, 2020.
- Identity: preserve the familiar canonical name DSD Renewables; add legal name Distributed Solar Development, LLC and predecessor name GE Solar as aliases.
- Operating platform: Schenectady-based developer, financier, owner and operator of U.S. distributed solar, storage and EV-charging infrastructure.
- Exit check: no signed or completed platform sale found through August 15, 2026.
