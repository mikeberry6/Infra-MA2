# Pike Holdings — proposed new canonical PortCo

Create **TransMontaigne Partners LLC** as the single canonical PortCo for ArcLight's “Pike Holdings” census holding.

Pike Holdings is Pike Petroleum Holdings, LLC, an intermediate sponsor shell. TransMontaigne is the operating U.S. midstream platform beneath it. ArcLight acquired control in February 2016; Fund VI completed the take-private in February 2019. Current filings establish 100% ownership of TransMontaigne through TLP Finance Holdings, an indirect wholly owned Fund VI subsidiary.

The proposal creates one active ArcLight Capital Partners / ArcLight Energy Partners Fund VI ownership period beginning in 2016. Its stake wording distinguishes initial control from full ownership since 2019 and does not infer the current capitalization of the Pike shell.

Pike Corporation and Pike County Light & Power are unrelated. Neither will be merged, redirected, superseded or changed. Subsidiaries, holding vehicles, terminals, pipelines and individual assets beneath TransMontaigne are not counted separately.

No platform-level exit or signed pending ownership transfer was identified through August 15, 2026. The legal founding year remains null because public materials use different operating-lineage and legal-formation reference points; this is nonblocking for the Phase 1 list decision.
