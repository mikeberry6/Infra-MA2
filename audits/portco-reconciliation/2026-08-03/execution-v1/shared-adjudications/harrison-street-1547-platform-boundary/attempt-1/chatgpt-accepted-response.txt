Review

KEEP_PLATFORM_MERGE_ALL_FACILITIES is the consistent result. The applicable rule is to count the continuing Harrison Street–1547 manager-level investment and operating program once, then merge every facility and property SPV publicly placed within that program unless direct evidence establishes a later exit or a genuinely separate manager-level platform. An umbrella legal entity is not required under the stated methodology.

The direct record does not support the prior mixed distinction. Harrison Street described Wells as an important step in growing the digital platform; Pittock was expressly added to the connectivity-focused platform with Harrison Street; and the 2021 purchase containing Orangeburg and Cheyenne expanded the Harrison Street–1547 portfolio to six assets. A 2022 joint release expressly identified Pittock and Wells as facilities in that platform. South Bend was subsequently brought under 1547’s umbrella in partnership with Harrison Street. Most decisively, an October 2024 joint release described the arrangement as Harrison Street and 1547’s joint national data-center and connectivity platform, totaling more than one million square feet.
