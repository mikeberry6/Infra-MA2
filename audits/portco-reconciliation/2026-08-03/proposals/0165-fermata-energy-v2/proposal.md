## Recommendation

Preserve Fermata Energy LLC as the historical Carlyle PortCo but retire Carlyle's ownership effective April 25, 2025 and mark the company realized. The executed asset purchase agreement and Nuvve's Form 8-K show that substantially all operating assets, contracts, intellectual property, names and goodwill transferred at closing to the newly formed Fermata Energy II LLC, which is controlled 51% by Nuvve. This was an operating-business asset sale rather than a disclosed transfer of Carlyle equity, but the operating PortCo left Carlyle and no current infrastructure-fund owner remains. Fermata Energy II is a separate successor and is not merged into the legacy Carlyle record. Direct sources do not identify the specific Carlyle fund, vehicle, stake or legal closing of the 2021 investment, so the unsupported CRSEF II attribution is removed and the 2021 entry year is retained from Carlyle's 2022 disclosure that it invested the prior year.
