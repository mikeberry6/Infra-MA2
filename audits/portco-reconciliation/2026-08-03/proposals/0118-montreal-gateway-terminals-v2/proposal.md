## Recommendation

Create one canonical Montreal Gateway Terminals Partnership company. Axium's live portfolio identifies Montreal Gateway Terminals as an operational transportation-infrastructure investment, while current company and Port of Montreal pages confirm that the business operates the Cast and Racine marine container terminals as one platform and that Termont Montreal is a separate operator. The Axium-led consortium completed its 100% acquisition from Morgan Stanley Infrastructure Partners on March 5, 2015. The disclosed Axium vehicle was Fiera Axium Infrastructure Canada II L.P.; Axium's individual percentage was not disclosed. Morgan Stanley's official release establishes its former 2007-2015 ownership through MGT Holdings S.a.r.l. Public sources last named Desjardins, Manulife, Fonds de solidarité FTQ and Industrial Alliance as acquisition consortium members, but no sufficiently current direct evidence was found to carry them forward as active 2026 owner rows. A 2025 refinancing identified FA MGT Limited Partnership as the parent but did not constitute an equity transfer. No later sale, secondary, concession termination, bankruptcy or signed pending exit was identified through August 18, 2026.
