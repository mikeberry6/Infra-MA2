## Evidence

- [APG beneficial investment and vehicle confirmation](https://assetmanagement.apg.nl/media/03xbpmt3/apg-infrastructure-pool-2020-2021-sfdr-periodic-report-2022.pdf) — APG Infrastructure Pool 2020-2021 listed Hawaiʻi Gas as an infrastructure investment in its 2022 portfolio disclosure, Hawaiʻi Gas represented 4% of the pool's assets in the disclosed 2022 investment table, The investment was classified in the energy sector and United States
- [Regulatory approval evidence](https://puc.hawaii.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/PUC-Annual-Report-FY-2022.pdf) — Docket No. 2021-0098 was closed after approval, Hawaiʻi PUC Decision and Order No. 38478 approved the transfer of control on June 29, 2022, The approved buyer was AMF Hawaii Holdings, LLC
- [Company identity, founding, and operating profile](https://www.hawaiigas.com/about-us-new) — Hawaiʻi Gas was founded in 1904 as Honolulu Gas Company, Its current operating base includes approximately 1,100 miles of pipeline and service across the state, The company is Hawaiʻi's only franchised gas utility
- [Argo acquisition closing and current legal-manager basis](https://www.hawaiigas.com/posts/acquisition-of-hawai-i-gas-paves-way-for-clean-energy-transformation) — Hawaiʻi Gas is an infrastructure utility owned by Argo, Ownership transferred to an affiliate of Argo Infrastructure Partners on July 21, 2022, The acquisition closed after Hawaiʻi Public Utilities Commission approval on June 29, 2022
- [Current Argo-managed ownership and 2025 infrastructure expansion](https://www.hawaiigas.com/posts/argo-acquires-ugis-gas-storage-and-supply-assets-in-hawai-i) — AMF Hawaiʻi Investment Holdings, LLC is the Argo-managed holding company, As of June 2025, Hawaiʻi Gas and Isle Gas were both wholly owned by funds managed by Argo, Isle Gas agreed to acquire approximately 750,000 gallons of propane storage and related delivery fleet assets
- [Acquisition investor and infrastructure-strategy evidence](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1289790/000110465921083888/tm2120341d1_defa14a.htm) — Argo described Hawaiʻi Gas as a utility infrastructure investment, Argo stated that it had committed capital from APG and CalSTRS for the Hawaiʻi Gas acquisition, The acquisition materials identify the utility's customers, services, and clean-energy infrastructure
- [September 2025 bolt-on closing confirmation](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884614/000088461425000053/ugi-20250930.htm) — AmeriGas completed the sale of its Hawaiʻi propane business in September 2025, The closing converted the June 2025 announced transaction into a completed acquisition, The transaction included approximately 750,000 gallons of propane storage and multiple delivery fleet assets
