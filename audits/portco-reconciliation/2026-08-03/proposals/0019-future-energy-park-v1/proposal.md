## Recommendation

Retire Amber Infrastructure's unsupported active ownership period and mark Future Energy Park realized in the PortCo database. GIP's first-quarter 2023 financial statements, however, state that FEP LP's Class A units remained 100% held by GIP and that Amber's expected 50% interest was subject to definitive documents, project debt financing, return thresholds and other conditions. No official evidence was found that Amber's conditional Future Energy Park investment ever closed. The retired period therefore records a proposed 50% interest that did not close and retains a null exit year; this is an administrative correction of erroneous active ownership, not a historical divestiture.
