## Recommendation

Correct Duquesne Light Company's ownership ledger to the current three-vehicle structure disclosed by the company in August 2026 and in its 2025 Corporate Responsibility Report. DQE Holdings' equity is held through Epsom Investment Pte. Ltd. (GIC, 44.4%), Three Rivers Utility Holdings, LLC (Manulife/John Hancock and PGGM, 30.4%), and AIA Montana LLC (owned by APG Americas Infrastructure and CalSTRS and managed by Argo Infrastructure Partners, 25.2%). The company does not identify Macquarie as a current investor, and a New York City Comptroller infrastructure-market report records Macquarie Infrastructure and Real Assets' 2016 sale of its 30.4% interest to PGGM and John Hancock. Retire the Macquarie ownership period in 2016, preserve both the APG economic-owner and Argo manager relationships without treating the shared 25.2% vehicle interest as two separate economic stakes, and clarify that Manulife's record represents the shared Three Rivers vehicle. Also correct the website, Pittsburgh headquarters, current operating description, verification date, and supporting milestone evidence. Current-company and current-report exit searches found no later sale or pending ownership transaction involving the three disclosed vehicles.
