## Recommendation

Archive Waihonu Solar Farm from the manager-level PortCo census and retire its Argo ownership period in 2025, while preserving the separately published Hawaiʻi Gas platform. Waihonu is the collective name for the Waihonu North and Waihonu South solar project assets, not a separate operating platform. Argo acquired the assets with Hawaiʻi Gas on July 21, 2022. Hawaiʻi Gas now states that Waihonu was acquired by a solar energy provider in 2025, and Form EIA-860 reporting changed from the two Waihonu project entities in 2024 to ERCAM LLC as the reporting utility and operator for both plants in the 2025 early release. The projects remain operational, but no reviewed source identifies ERCAM as an infrastructure-fund manager or supports adding a new manager ownership period. A redirect from Waihonu to Hawaiʻi Gas would be false because the project assets and utility platform are distinct identities. The exact 2025 closing date, transaction value and ERCAM parent are not publicly disclosed; those disclosure gaps do not block the list correction. No later sale or signed pending ownership transaction was identified through August 16, 2026.
