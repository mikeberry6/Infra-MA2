## Recommendation

Create one canonical Orca Acquisitions, LLC manager-level joint-venture platform. ArcLight Energy Partners Fund VI owns ArcLight Orca Holdings, LLC, which holds 95% of Orca's Class A managing interests, appoints two management-committee members and controls day-to-day decisions. Olympus Power affiliates hold the remaining 5% of Class A interests and all non-managing, non-voting Class B interests; therefore the Class A split must not be presented as the overall economic ownership split. Orca indirectly owns Walleye Power, LLC through Walleye Energy Holdings, LLC. Walleye owns and operates the Bay Shore generating and industrial-steam facility in Oregon, Ohio. Count Orca once; Walleye, Walleye Energy Holdings, Bay Shore and its individual generating units are subsidiaries or assets beneath the platform. ArcLight's current investments page, its 2025 ESG report and a June 2026 FERC filing support continuing Fund VI ownership. No Orca-specific sale, signed exit or pending change of control was identified through August 14, 2026.
