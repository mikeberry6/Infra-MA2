## Recommendation

Retain CAG Holdings as Carlyle's manager-level airport infrastructure platform and correct the ownership history rather than retiring it. Ferrovial acquired 96% of Carlyle's former 51% indirect interest in the New Terminal One at JFK at the June 10, 2022 financial close, leaving Carlyle with approximately 2% indirect ownership. Ferrovial's 2026 SEC materials continue to report that 2% interest, and no later Carlyle full exit or signed transfer was identified through August 19, 2026. CAG Holdings is the platform; JFK NTO LLC and The New Terminal One are the concessionaire and associated project and are not separate PortCos. The platform's precise equity percentage, legal investment date and current headquarters are not publicly disclosed, so they are not inferred.
