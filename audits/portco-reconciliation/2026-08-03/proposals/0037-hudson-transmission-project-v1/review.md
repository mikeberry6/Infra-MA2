## Evidence

- [APG beneficial investment and vehicle confirmation](https://assetmanagement.apg.nl/media/xuvbwxt3/apg-infrastructure-pool-2014-sfdr-periodic-report-2022.pdf) — APG Infrastructure Pool 2014 listed Hudson Transmission Partners among its largest investments for reference year 2022, Hudson Transmission Partners represented 6% of pool assets, The investment was classified in the energy sector and United States
- [Regulatory ownership chain and investment-vehicle evidence](https://documents.dps.ny.gov/public/Common/ViewDoc.aspx?DocRefId=%7B5288AF6D-AFB8-4165-86A4-81723FB9725E%7D) — AMF Oratam Investments LLC was formed solely to invest in the Hudson Transmission Project, AMF Oratam would acquire the 94% Class C economic interest in Hudson Transmission Partners, APG Infrastructure Pool 2014 wholly owned Sogra Investment Holding B.V., which was allocated a 37.7% indirect interest in AMF Oratam at closing, Argo Infrastructure Partners is the managing member of AMF Oratam
- [Project identity, construction, and operating profile](https://hudsonproject.com/project/) — Construction began in May 2011 and commercial service began in June 2013, Hudson is a 660 MW underground and underwater HVDC link between PJM and New York City, The route runs from Ridgefield, New Jersey to Manhattan
- [Current Argo principal-investor evidence](https://hudsonproject.com/team/owners-investors/) — PowerBridge continues to operate and manage the project, The current Hudson Transmission Project website identifies Argo Infrastructure Partners as principal investor, The page remained current with a 2026 site footer when rechecked
- [Recent operating and contractual evidence](https://prod-www.nypa.gov/-/media/nypa/documents/document-library/annual-web-posting/2024/operations-and-accomplishment-report-2024---nypa.pdf) — NYPA contracts for 575 MW, or 87% of project capacity, NYPA's 2024 report identifies Hudson Transmission Partners as the developer, owner, and operator of the line, The project recorded 99.94% availability in 2024
- [September 2018 acquisition announcement](https://www.lotusinfrastructure.com/news/lotus-to-sell-hudson-transmission-line-to-argo/) — Ares EIF and Starwood agreed to sell their Hudson interests to an Argo-managed investment vehicle, The announced transaction covered the project company that owns the 660 MW HVDC link, The transaction was subject to regulatory approvals and expected to close in late 2018 or early 2019
- [March 2019 closing confirmation](https://www.lotusinfrastructure.com/our-investments/) — Lotus identifies Hudson Transmission Project as a realized investment, Lotus states that it sold its Hudson interest to Argo Infrastructure in March 2019, The asset is described as a 660 MW transmission line between Ridgefield and Midtown Manhattan
