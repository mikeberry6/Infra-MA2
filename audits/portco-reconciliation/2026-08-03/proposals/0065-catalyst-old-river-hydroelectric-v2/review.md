## Recommendation

Correct the existing project-company record to its legal identity and complete its sponsor-ownership history. Catalyst Old River Hydroelectric Limited Partnership is the single operating/project entity for the 192 MW Sidney A. Murray Jr. Hydroelectric Station; its holdcos, lenders, municipal co-licensee and facility names are not separate PortCos or equity owners. Brookfield Renewable affiliates hold the 75% economic balance and managing/control position. ArcLight Energy Partners Fund V first invested in 2011 and transferred its then-current 25% project interest on July 8, 2021 to ArcLight Renewable Infrastructure Fund SM SPV, L.P.; the Fund V period is therefore historical and the continuation vehicle is the current 25% owner. ArcLight continues to list Catalyst Old River as current, Brookfield's March 2026 reporting still reflects the 25% third-party interest in The Catalyst Group, and April 2026 FERC filings continue to group Catalyst with Brookfield Renewable entities. No later sale, signed exit or pending ownership change was identified through August 13, 2026.
