## Recommendation

Preserve Great Western Wind Farm as a single historical PortCo record, but retire the Allianz Global Investors ownership period and mark the company realized because current direct Allianz ownership is not supported. Allianz's February 2017 release confirms that Allianz Capital Partners and MUFG invested tax equity in the EDF-developed project. The official EIA-860 2025 early-release Schedule 4 owner workbook contains no jointly or third-party-owned row for Great Western; EIA states that generators absent from that file are wholly owned by their reported operator. Current EDF and Oklahoma Corporation Commission pages continue to associate the operating project with EDF. The public record reviewed does not disclose the date when Allianz's tax-equity interest ended, so the retired ownership period retains a null exit year rather than inventing one.
