## Recommendation

Create one canonical Salamanca Infrastructure LLC record for ArcLight's Salamanca census holding. The active Delaware company owns the Salamanca floating production system and associated oil and natural-gas export laterals at Keathley Canyon Block 689 in the deepwater U.S. Gulf of Mexico. Count this infrastructure once; exclude the separate Leon and Castile upstream fields, wells and leases, the former Independence Hub identity, LLOG as operator, and the subordinate Salamanca Infrastructure Finance, Salamanca FPS Infra and Salamanca OGL Infra entities. ArcLight Energy Partners Fund VII-backed affiliates financed the development through an ownership arrangement and hold a plurality ownership interest; the exact percentage and legal investment vehicle are not publicly disclosed. Repsol completed a combined 20% sale to ArcLight and Centaurus in December 2023 and retained 2.5%, but public sources do not disclose the complete current capitalization table or allocation of the sold interest. ArcLight continues to list Salamanca as Current, and no ArcLight exit or signed pending Salamanca sale was identified through August 15, 2026.
