## Recommendation

Create one canonical TransMontaigne Partners LLC manager-level operating platform for ArcLight's Pike Holdings census label. A 2021 SEC agreement defines Pike Petroleum Holdings, LLC as Pike Holdings and documents it as an intermediate sponsor shell above TransMontaigne. ArcLight obtained platform control through its February 2016 acquisition of TransMontaigne GP, and Fund VI's February 2019 take-private made TransMontaigne wholly owned. Current filings establish that TLP Finance Holdings owns 100% of TransMontaigne and is an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of ArcLight Energy Partners Fund VI, L.P. Count TransMontaigne once; do not separately count the Pike/PPH/TLP holding layers, operating subsidiaries, terminals, pipelines or other assets beneath it. Pike Corporation and Pike County Light & Power are unrelated and remain unchanged. No TransMontaigne platform-level sale or signed pending ownership transfer was identified through August 15, 2026.
