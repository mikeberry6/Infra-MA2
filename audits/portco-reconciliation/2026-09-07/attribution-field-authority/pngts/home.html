Portland Natural Gas Transmission System (PNGTS) is your trusted partner in delivering energy reliably, economically, and safely to New England and Atlantic Canada

Learn more

PNGTS is a critical conduit in the North American natural gas network

Established in 1999, PNGTS connects major pipeline networks In Canada and the US to serve the New England and Atlantic Canada regions with a steady and secure natural gas supply. We prioritize safety, regulatory compliance, and environmental stewardship, reflecting our dedication to serving the energy needs of our communities while protecting the environment for future generations.
