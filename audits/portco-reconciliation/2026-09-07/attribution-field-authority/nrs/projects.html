Case Study



Shallow Basket Energy

One of the largest renewable energy installations on Tribal Land in the United States

In 2025, NRS energized Shallow Basket Project, a 140 MW solar and 50 MW battery storage project in Rio Arriba County, New Mexico. In addition to leasing its land for the project, the Jicarilla Apache Nation will benefit from the reliable power produced and stored by the project through its wholesale supply agreement with Guzman Energy.

The Shallow Basket Project embodies our commitment to a sustainable future and economic self-sufficiency for the Jicarilla Apache Nation. By harnessing renewable energy on our lands, we're building a legacy of environmental stewardship. We are protecting our environment and creating new opportunities for economic growth that will ensure prosperity for future generations.


— President Adrian Notsinneh
