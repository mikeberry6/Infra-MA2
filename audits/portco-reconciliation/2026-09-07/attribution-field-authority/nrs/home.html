What We Do

Backed by BlackRock Real Assets, NRS is an energy company with wind, solar and energy storage projects in development and operation across the US.

We build long-lasting partnerships with landowners, community members, suppliers, and investors. These relationships are the foundation of our success. We are innovative and flexible in service of our mission to grow the renewables foothold in the US.
