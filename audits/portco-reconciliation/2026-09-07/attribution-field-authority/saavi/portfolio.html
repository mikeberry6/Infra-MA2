Saavi Energía

Industry Energy - Renewables & Power
Location Americas
Status Unrealized
Website saavienergia.com

Saavi Energía is Mexico’s largest independent private power generator with a geographically diverse 3.2 net GW portfolio of 7 CCGTs, 1 GT, 3 compression stations and 65 km of associated natural gas pipelines. Saavi primarily serves the Mexican power market as well as providing reserve capacity for specified markets in California.
