Leadership at Eolian

  • Aaron Zubaty, Chief Executive Officer

    Aaron Zubaty founded Eolian in 2020. His work on the assets that became Eolian began in 2005, when he started a renewable energy investment practice managed by MAP Energy, where he was one of three Managing Partners and owners of the firm. In 2020, after 15 years of growing the renewable energy and energy storage investment activity and ultimately managing more than $2 billion of direct fund equity investment into both renewable energy and natural gas assets, he led a spinoff transaction of the Eolian team and entire clean energy portfolio that was capitalized by GIP Fund IV. Before joining MAP in 2005, Aaron worked for independent power producer AES Corporation in London and across Africa. At AES he served in both business development and financial management roles, leading the planning, implementation, and financing of energy projects as well as directly overseeing the operations of the national utility serving the country of Cameroon. Aaron received an MS, Civil Engineering, Concentration in Fluid Mechanics and Hydrology and a BS, Earth Systems, Concentration in Energy Engineering, both from Stanford University.

    Connect with me on LinkedIn.

  • Peter Blum, President

    Peter Blum joined shortly following the company’s origination in 2005. He is part of the founding ownership team and a member of Eolian’s Board, overseeing all transactions and the management of all Eolian joint ventures. He previously served as Eolian’s Chief Commercial Officer before assuming his current role as President. Leading up to Eolian’s formation, Peter led M&A at MAP and was a member of MAP Energy’s Investment Committee. Prior to joining MAP Energy, Peter focused on power plant acquisitions and asset optimization in the strategy and business development group at Edison International as well as working at both Silicon Energy and Itron as an energy software product manager. He has a Master of Science in Civil Engineering and a Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering, both from Stanford University.

    Connect with me on LinkedIn.

  • Gene Settoon, Chief Operating Officer

    Gene oversees the engineering, procurement, construction, projecting financing, and asset operations teams at Eolian. Prior to joining Eolian in 2021, Gene worked at Invenergy, a renewable energy developer, in a variety of roles spanning M&A, new market strategy, and business development for distributed energy systems.  He also brings knowledge and experience from his previous roles across the energy industry: business development at Ambri, an energy storage start-up; management consulting at McKinsey; and engineering and project management at ExxonMobil’s global upstream project company.  He earned a Master of Business Administration from the University of Chicago Booth School of Business and a Bachelor of Science in Materials Science and Engineering from MIT.

    Connect with me on LinkedIn.

  • Colin Fisher, Chief Financial Officer

    Colin Fisher oversees financial reporting, treasury and internal controls for Eolian. Colin brings two decades of global leadership and CFO experience, garnered at large publicly traded technology companies, venture capital-backed semiconductor and hardware systems companies in Silicon Valley, as well as from private equity-backed portfolio companies. Before joining Eolian, Colin served as CFO for ITRenew, an IT infrastructure services & software company that he helped scale and sell to Iron Mountain. Prior to that, he was the CFO at Raymond Express International, a freight-forwarding and logistics company owned by HIG Capital. Colin earned a BS in Finance from Arizona State University and an MBA from Santa Clara University.

    Connect with me on LinkedIn.

  • Kelly Giddens, Chief Legal Officer

    Kelly Giddens is responsible for overseeing Eolian’s legal affairs and operations. Kelly brings over 15 years of legal expertise in renewables project development, construction, procurement and finance. Prior to joining Eolian, Kelly spent several years as Associate General Counsel at Cypress Creek Renewables, a solar developer and IPP. Prior to her tenure at Cypress Creek Renewables, she was in private practice at Orrick, Herrington & Sutcliffe LLP and Farella Braun + Martel LLP. She has a JD/MBA from New York University and a BA in Economics from U.C. Davis.

    Connect with me on LinkedIn.

  • Eric Stoutenburg, Chief Development Officer

    Eric Stoutenburg leads strategy and execution for Eolian’s portfolio of development projects including energy storage, thermal generation, geothermal, and powered industrial sites. Before that, he was Chief Development Officer at Able Grid Energy Solutions managing investment and development activities for a 10 GW portfolio of energy storage projects. He holds a Doctorate in Civil and Environmental Engineering from Stanford University and a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering from the United States Military Academy at West Point.

    Connect with me on LinkedIn.

  • Chien Si Harriman, Chief Technology Officer

    Chien Si Harriman has been working inside of Eolian as a technology development lead, playing a vital role in the engineering, design, and construction of its battery storage fleet while also managing the inception of Eolian’s proprietary power trading software platform. In past lives, he has held senior engineering roles at large multi-national engineering consulting firms, startups, NASA, and Boeing. He has held key roles in the design and development of hyperscale datacenters, as well as the redevelopment of the World Trade Center Masterplan in lower Manhattan where he analyzed design and efficiency efforts in the World Trade Center central heating and cooling plant design. He also was a product manager at Aligned Data Centers, a Macquarie and Blue Mountain Capital Management investment, where he oversaw development of SCADA and energy management and efficiency software solutions. His work is focused on developing technology solutions to optimize mechanical and electrical systems factoring in climate, weather, process engineering, physics, optimization research and machine learning.  He holds a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering from Stanford University.
