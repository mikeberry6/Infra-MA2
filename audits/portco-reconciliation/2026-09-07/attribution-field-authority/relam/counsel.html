Basalt Infrastructure Partners Acquires 1435 Rail Equipment Leasing and Maintenance, Inc.

05/28/2026

Team: Luke Woolford | Alex Steele | Soha Atiq | Andrew M. Reback | Danielle Karlin | Carla Potter | J’Davia Noel | Michelle McKinnon | Adam Rempel | Davit Akman | Joshua Hollenberg | A. Chandimal Nicholas | Peterson Eugenio | Jay Motwani | Wendy Trinh | Anita Im | Andrea Breden | Paulina Seo | Julia Trinh

On May 28, 2026, Basalt Infrastructure Partners completed the acquisition of 1435 Rail Equipment Leasing and Maintenance, Inc. (“R.E.L.A.M.”) from Paceline Equity Partners. R.E.L.A.M. is a U.S.-based provider of maintenance-of-way and hi-rail equipment leasing and related services supporting rail operators and contractors across North America.

Cassels acted as Canadian counsel to Basalt Infrastructure Partners.
