1PointFive and Bain & Company Announce Agreement for Direct Air Capture Carbon Removal Credits 

01.13.2026

1PointFive announced today that Bain & Company purchased 9,000 metric tons of carbon dioxide removal (CDR) credits over three years enabled by Direct Air Capture (DAC). The agreement is Bain’s first purchase of DAC removal credits as a solution to address residual operational emissions and meet its current net negative commitment.
