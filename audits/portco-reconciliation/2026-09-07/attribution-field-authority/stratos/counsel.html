Overview

Maxwell Baird is a real estate associate in the Dallas office of Kirkland & Ellis LLP. He focuses his practice on complex commercial real estate transactions, including financings, acquisitions, dispositions and joint ventures. Maxwell represents private equity companies, real estate funds, owners, operators and developers in transactions spanning a wide range of asset classes, with an emphasis on data centers and other digital infrastructure assets.
