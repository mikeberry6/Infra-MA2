Our Story

Building a Legacy in Clean Energy

The Excelsior Energy Capital story is rooted in a strong foundation of alignment and trust, established by four innovative founders. With over $1.5 billion in equity capital under management and a team with more than 150 years of combined experience, the Firm continues to expand and innovate within clean energy infrastructure. The legacy of alignment and trust remains at the heart of Excelsior Energy Capital, fueling our success and delivering lasting value to our partners and the communities we serve.
