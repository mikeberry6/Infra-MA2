Weekly Briefing – Infrastructure Sponsor M&A – 15 deals across Power & ET, Transportation, Utilities, Social Infra, and Midstream – August 1–7, 2026
GUGGENHEIM
INFRASTRUCTURE COVERAGE & ADVISORY
Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • August 1 – August 7, 2026
KEY THEMES
EnCap Flatrock’s up-to-US$5.5bn Momentum sale was the week’s largest disclosed transaction, while Meridiam’s majority investment in the €1.9bn Great Sea Interconnector and KKR’s 50% interest in a €1.8bn renewables portfolio anchored a 15-deal week concentrated in Power & ET and transportation, with additional utilities and social-infrastructure activity.
U.S. deployment centered on Apollo’s Maverick Water acquisition, Basalt’s MBI buyout, 3i’s Massachusetts Central / Wildwood rail bolt-on and Terramont’s Hainesport investment, while EnCap’s Momentum sale highlighted sponsor exits from scaled midstream assets.
Power & ET5 Deals
TotalEnergies European Renewables Portfolio | KKR
KKR (Co-Investment) · Onshore Solar & Wind · Germany / Spain / France / Poland
KKR agreed to acquire a 50% interest in TotalEnergies’ largely developed 1.2 GW European onshore solar and wind portfolio at a €1.8bn enterprise value. TotalEnergies will retain 50%, continue operating the assets and market their power.
Source
AVK Power Solutions | Partners Group
Partners Group (Majority Stake) · Data Center Power Infrastructure · United Kingdom
Partners Group agreed to acquire a majority stake in AVK through its fourth direct infrastructure program, with management retaining a minority interest, and plans to invest more than US$1bn of equity. AVK has delivered more than 20,000 projects representing c.3.5 GW of installed power and has a pipeline above 2 GW.
Source
Enpal Residential Energy Systems Portfolio | Equitix
Equitix / Keppel Infrastructure Trust / MM Capital Partners (Majority Stake) · Distributed Solar & Battery Storage · Germany
The infrastructure-fund consortium, also including Mizuho Leasing, acquired 90% of Enpal subsidiaries holding more than 20,000 operating residential energy systems; Enpal retains 10% and remains the operator. Up to €65mm of new capital will support a portfolio with projected capacity of c.240 MW.
Source
Verdant Energy / Aura Power | CVC DIF
CVC DIF (Merger) · Solar & Battery Storage · United Kingdom
CVC DIF-backed Verdant Energy and Aura Power merged to form a UK independent power producer with c.1 GW of operating and under-construction solar and battery assets and a c.10 GW development pipeline. CVC DIF invested through DIF Infrastructure VII, while Aura Power’s founder retains a minority stake.
Source
Bioforce | Amber Infrastructure
Amber Infrastructure / 3SIIF (Primary Capital Raise) · Biomethane · Estonia
Amber-advised Three Seas Initiative Investment Fund invested expansion capital in Bioforce, Estonia’s leading biomethane producer. Bioforce has three operating plants and a fourth commissioning toward combined output above 250 GWh, with a plan to reach 1 TWh by 2032 through Baltic and Polish expansion.
Source
Transportation4 Deals
Cella | I Squared Capital
I Squared Capital (Buyout) · Logistics & Cold Storage · Indonesia
I Squared entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Cella, an Indonesian industrial-logistics and cold-storage platform. Cella owns and operates five facilities totaling approximately 231,000 square meters across major Indonesian distribution markets.
Source
Rail Modal Group | InfraRed Capital Partners
InfraRed Capital Partners (Majority Stake) · Inland Intermodal Rail & Export Logistics · United States
InfraRed acquired a majority stake in Rail Modal Group through its value-add infrastructure strategy, with founder Greg Oberting remaining invested and serving as CEO. The platform operates four U.S. inland intermodal terminals and has shipped more than 1,200 full-length unit trains, equivalent to approximately 200mm truck miles.
Source
Massachusetts Central Railroad / Wildwood Reload | 3i
3i / Regional Rail (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Short-Line Freight Rail & Transloading · Massachusetts, United States
3i-backed Regional Rail acquired the 28-mile Massachusetts Central Railroad and Wildwood Reload’s warehousing and transloading operations. The bolt-on extends a network that now reaches 18 railroads across 10 U.S. states and two Canadian provinces.
Source
Hainesport Transportation Group | Terramont Infrastructure Partners
Terramont Infrastructure Partners (Majority Stake) · Short-Line Rail & Rail-Served Waste Logistics · New Jersey, United States
Terramont acquired a majority stake in Hainesport Transportation Group, which combines a short-line railroad with rail-served waste transfer and recycling facilities in New Jersey. The investment provides growth capital for an integrated regional rail-and-environmental-logistics platform.
Source
Utilities3 Deals
Great Sea Interconnector | Meridiam
Meridiam (Majority Stake) · Power Interconnector · Greece / Cyprus
Meridiam signed an agreement to become the majority shareholder of the Great Sea Interconnector SPV. The approximately €1.9bn, c.900 km subsea link is designed to transfer 1,000 MW between Greece and Cyprus and end Cyprus’ electrical isolation.
Source
Maverick Water Group | Apollo
Apollo (Buyout) · Non-Potable Water Systems · Texas, United States
Apollo-managed funds acquired Maverick Water Group from Crosstimbers-managed funds, with management retaining a minority stake. Maverick develops, owns and operates long-term contracted non-potable water systems for communities, data centers and industrial customers across Texas.
Source
MP Power | EQT Infrastructure
EQT Infrastructure / Osmose (Portfolio Company Acquisition) · Electric Utility Infrastructure Services · United Kingdom
EQT Infrastructure-backed Osmose acquired MP Power, an Ely-based provider of overhead-line inspection, pole assessment, cable-condition and clearance services to UK electricity network operators. The bolt-on expands Osmose’s utility-infrastructure assessment capabilities in Europe.
Source
Social Infra2 Deals
Fallowfield Campus Student Accommodation | Equitix
Equitix / GRAHAM (Joint Venture) · Student Accommodation · Manchester, United Kingdom
Equitix and GRAHAM reached financial close on a £530mm, 50-year partnership with the University of Manchester to redevelop the Fallowfield campus. The project will deliver 3,300 student rooms in what the partners describe as the world’s largest single-phase Passivhaus student-accommodation scheme.
Source
MBI | Basalt Infrastructure Partners
Basalt Infrastructure Partners (Buyout) · Waste Transportation & Transfer-Station Management · United States
Basalt-advised funds acquired MBI from Fortress-managed funds; Basalt announced the transaction on August 3 after its July 31 closing. MBI operates more than 5,000 long-lived specialized assets and serves waste operators and municipalities across more than 40 terminals nationwide.
Source
Midstream1 Deal
Momentum Midstream (M6) | EnCap Flatrock Midstream
EnCap Flatrock / M6 Midstream (Portfolio Company Sale) · Natural Gas Gathering & Transmission · Louisiana, United States
Williams agreed to acquire 100% of EnCap Flatrock-backed Momentum Midstream for US$3.5bn in cash and assumed debt plus approximately US$2bn in Williams equity, for total consideration of up to US$5.5bn. The system spans more than 4,000 miles and connects Haynesville production to Gulf Coast LNG and power demand.
Source
